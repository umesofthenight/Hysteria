Resonance Record

Acting Principles, Resonance Points and potion digestion.

In Lord of the Mysteries, the Acting Method is the primary means through which a Beyonder digests their potion. To digest a potion, a character must understand the meaning behind their Sequence Name and embody that role through their actions. Simply using Beyonder abilities does not count as Acting. Characters progress through:

  • [Acting Principles]
  • [Resonance Points]
  • [Completed Roleplays]

Once all requirements have been fulfilled, the potion is considered fully digested.

ACTING PRINCIPLES

Acting Principle is a character's interpretation of how a they should embody their Sequence. Acting Principles should:

[Relate directly to the Sequence Name.] [Influence how the character behaves.] [Be demonstrated through roleplay.] [Be written in the character’s own interpretation.] [Be meaningfully different from one another.]

Different characters may possess different Acting Principles for the same Sequence. For example, two Hunters might develop the following principles:

[A Hunter must understand their prey before beginning the pursuit.]

[A Hunter should determine the battlefield before the prey realizes it is being hunted.]

Both may be valid despite their different approach to the Sequence. Copying an Acting Principle from another character or guide does not automatically confirm it. The principle must still be demonstrated through roleplay.

RESONANCE POINTS

Completed roleplays can award players between 1 and 3 Resonance Points. The value is determined by how important the character’s Sequence and Acting Principles were to the scene.

Minor Acting: If a Character has displayed simple behavior or played a role that stemmed from their Acting Principle when the major focus wasn't Acting gives 1 Resonance Point. Meaningful Acting: If a Character has made consistent choices, or applied methods, or showcased behavior that is associated with their current Sequence, they gain 2 Resonance Points. Major Acting: This is a rare case where the spotlight of the roleplay was about the Character's sequence, and/or the Character went through major development because of their sequence. This awards the Character 3 Resonance Points.

Examples of each case:

  • Minor Acting:
  • Briefly applying an Acting Principle.
  • A Short reflection following an important event.

Basically, Minor Acting simply contributes to digestion, but it of course cannot carry the whole digestion. This is mainly the tier gained by people just started, or those who simply want to gain the last Resonance Point they require.

  • Meaningful Acting:
  • Using their Acting Principle to resolve an issue.
  • Performing the professional or symbolic role of the Sequence.
  • Making an important decision, solely based on the Character's understanding of the Sequence.

Meaningful Acting is the rather common tier, where those who are used to the Acting Method will be more comfortable, easily reaching this tier.

  • Major Acting:
  • Having an existing Acting Principle challenged.
  • Successfully applying multiple principles during an Event.
  • Facing life-threatening danger because of the Sequence's Role.

Compared to the previous point, Major Acting will be on the very rare end of Resonance Points, as Major Acting is mainly reserved for roleplays where Acting is the primary focus, alongside many other factors.

Advancement Requirements

Low Sequences

  • [1] Acting Principle
  • [6] Resonance Points
  • [3] Roleplays
  • [7] Days

Mid Sequences

  • [2] Acting Principle
  • [15] Resonance Points
  • [5] Roleplays
  • [14] Days

Saint Sequences

  • [3] Acting Principle
  • [21] Resonance Points
  • [7] Roleplays
  • [1] Event
  • [30] Days

Angel and Above

These are unique cases that will be handled individually by staff. At this level, Acting involves more than acting, as there are requirements of anchors, large-scale influence, and maintaining ones humanity against the increasing Godhood within themselves.

SOLO ROLEPLAY

While solo roleplay can contribute towards ones digestion of their Sequence, it cannot replace interaction with the rest of the server. As such, there are restrictions placed upon Solo Roleplays.

  • A Solo Roleplay can only grant a maximum of 1 Resonance Point, meaning it will always count as Minor Acting.
  • No more than 1/3rd of a Character's required Resonance Points can come from Solo Roleplay.

A Solo Roleplay where the player has the freedom to control both the problem and the opposition, and transition all of that into resolution will not receive additional Resonance Points simply because it is "well-written" or "lengthy."

OVER-ACTING

A Beyonder must always keep in mind that they are only acting. They should consciously perform the role that they wish to embody according to their Sequence, but they should never allow it to completely replace their identity, lest they wish to become something they aren't.

Over-Acting can be seen when a Character's personality is shaped by their sequence, when they perform the acting method in every possible scenario, when they use extreme behaviors just to progress their digestion faster, and when they mistake the potions influence for their own desires.

Over-Acting does not provide Resonance Points. Instead, depending on the severity of it, it can cause Instability, or direct Sanity loss.

FULL DIGESTION

A Sequence is fully digested once the character has completed the Advancement Requirements (opens in a new tab). However, fully digesting a Sequence does not directly mean Advancement to next Sequence. The Character must still obtain the following, whenever applicable:

  • The Potion Formula
  • Beyonder Characteristic/Main Ingredient(s)
  • Supplementary Ingredients
  • Advancement Ritual

Once all of these points are fulfilled, an Advancement Roleplay will be conducted, and only once the Advancement Roleplay ends successfully will the Character advance.
