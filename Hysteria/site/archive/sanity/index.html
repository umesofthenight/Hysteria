Sanity Terms

Instability

During missions, casual roleplays, sometimes even during solo roleplays, where Sanity affecting abilities take place, unless specifically specified, the mental damage taken will be stored as Instability. Instability is a stat that starts from [0], and can go up as much as you increase it, though I really don’t suggest that. Instability is stored for 24 hours (after the corresponding RP ends), after which any Instability remaining is transformed into SV (Sanity Value) loss. However, unlike SV which is really difficult to heal, Instability can be healed by most mentally soothing and calming abilities.

The changes that Instability brings are applied to the current RP that the OC is in.

  • This means that if an OC had 85 SV, and they suffered a mental attack that gave them 10 Instability, for the remainder of that roleplay, or until the Instability is healed, whichever is sooner, they will be treated as if they had 75 SV.
  • However, this is only the case for that roleplay. If you have joined another roleplay, the OC would simply be treated as having 85 SV and 10 Instability.

Superior Clarity

Superior Clarity is a state which can only be sustained for 3 days before it naturally decays back to 95 SV.

Superior Clarity, table 3

During the state of Superior Clarity, any Instability received is halved, and analytical abilities, such as an Error’s Superior Observation and Decryption, or a White Tower’s Analysis, gain increased efficiency.

Sanity Recovery

There are 2 available ways to recover lost SV. The first one is through Cogitation, which can be done alone, and the second one is through Therapy, which requires another OC.

Cogitation

Cogitation, which is also known as Meditation, is used to centralize the thoughts and retain rational focus. You can read more about it here: Cogitation (opens in a new tab)

By Cogitating or Meditating, an OC can recover 1d3+2 Sanity Value, or reduce their Instability by that amount.

For Demigods, Cogitation instead recovers 1d5+2 Sanity Value.

Cogitation can only be done once per week.

Therapy

Through receiving Therapy, an OC can recover 1d4+1 Sanity Value, or reduce their Instability by that amount.

Therapy can only be given by Visionary and Mother OCs. Much like Cogitation, it can only be done once per week. For each Sequence the Visionary/Mother is above that of the OC, the dice roll gains a +1.

Sequence 5

From Sequence 5 onwards, staff will categorize rituals 3 Tiers: Great, Average, Bad. Most Rituals at this level will fall into the [Average] tier, as they don’t really offer the Beyonder any creative ways to accomplish it. They are straight to the point, and must be done 1:1 most of the time. For the ones that can afford a bit of creativity, these tiers will be used.

Sequence 5, table 6
Rolls and results will be the same as Low-Mid sequences.
A Great Ritual will cancel out all the SV lost from Advancement.
An Average Ritual will transform the lost SV into Instability, meaning it could be healed.
A Bad Ritual will add 5 Instability alongside any SV lost.

  • A strong personal identity.
  • A faction, cult, church, family, or organization.
  • Devotees, patients, soldiers, students, lovers or followers.
