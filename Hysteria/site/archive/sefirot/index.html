Sefirot

The sources of power beyond the pathways.

Sefirot (源质 Yuánzhì, Source Quality) are collections of symbols, sources of authority, and origins of power. To be considered a Great Old One (opens in a new tab) that's Above the Sequence, one needs to accommodate the corresponding Sefirah (opens in a new tab) and all corresponding Uniquenesses (opens in a new tab), plus one Sequence 1 Beyonder Characteristic (opens in a new tab) from each corresponding Pathway.

The Ancient Gods (opens in a new tab) believed that the Original Creator (opens in a new tab) left nine legacies behind on Earth, which are derived from some part of his body. They might be kingdoms, cities, rivers, oceans, keys, etc. In reality, six of the nine Sefirot on Earth originated from the Original Creator (opens in a new tab), and the remaining three came from the three Outer Deities (opens in a new tab) who are direct descendants of the Original Creator (opens in a new tab). There are specific records on the Second Blasphemy Slate (opens in a new tab) regarding the Sefirot and the secrets of Above the Sequence.

According to Amon (opens in a new tab), the nine Sefirot on Earth are either completely illusory and hidden somewhere unknown, or they're definitely real while existing in the real world. The Chaos Sea (opens in a new tab) is the only Sefirah (opens in a new tab) that merges the real and the illusory and has an entrance in the real world.

The two Sefirot River of Eternal Darkness (opens in a new tab) and City of Calamity (opens in a new tab) are the symbols of the hidden Fourth Pillar (opens in a new tab). However, it wasn't the birth of the Fourth Pillar (opens in a new tab) that would bring about the end of the universe, but the universe reaching its true apocalypse that would give rise to the Fourth Pillar (opens in a new tab) symbolizing the final end. The existence of the Fourth Pillar (opens in a new tab) is also the reason why the Pathways under the Eternal Darkness (opens in a new tab) group and the Calamity of Destruction (opens in a new tab) groups are exchangeable. However, doing so leaves one unable to ascend to a Great Old One (opens in a new tab), unless they think of a way to separate the Beyonder characteristics from before.

The Law of Beyonder Characteristics Convergence (opens in a new tab) is also applicable to Sefirot. This causes things and people related to the different Sefirot to be attracted to each other.

Mixing the abilities of Pathways belonging to the same Sefirah (opens in a new tab) can manifest the Sefirah (opens in a new tab)'s aura or power (for example, the combined attack of Lumian Lee (opens in a new tab) and 0-17 (opens in a new tab), or the Demoness's advancement ritual). Therefore, if one becomes a Multi-Pathway True God (opens in a new tab) of all Pathways under the Above the Sequence group, "They" will be able to manifest the symbolism and powers of "Their" Sefirah (opens in a new tab) through such combinations.

Characteristics

Embodiment of Symbolism

Sefirot are collections of Symbols, sources of Authority, and origins of Power. They are the core aspect of Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab), beings that directly represent of a certain aspect of the universe and can exert influence on the universe as a whole. All Abilities of the Pathways under the Sefirot are merely subordinate and further derived manifestations of Symbolism.

Unique Existence

At any moment in time, the Sefirot and things related to them are Unique. For example, if a marionette or avatar of a Great Old One (opens in a new tab) uses the power of a Sefirah (opens in a new tab), "Their" other avatars cannot use it at the same time.

Indestructibility

Normally, like Uniquenesses (opens in a new tab), Sefirot cannot be divided, copied, or destroyed. They can be damaged, but specific part/essence of them are truly indestructible. Overtime, the Sefirot will heal any damage done to it. They transcend concepts like Space and Time, which is why even Ancient Gods (opens in a new tab) and deities who can manipulate these concepts are unable to destroy them.

Sefirot can only be permanently damaged, split apart, or destroyed by:

  • Encountering the Original Creator (opens in a new tab), who can make the impossible possible.
  • Encountering the Fourth Pillar (opens in a new tab), which holds the Termination symbolism that represents the end of all things and the universe.

Self-Completion

For a Sefirah (opens in a new tab) that was split apart into separate portions, initially those portions are incomplete. However, since torn Sefirot have the ability to mend themselves, over time, both the portion that stayed with the original Great Old One (opens in a new tab) and the portion that was split away would eventually self-complete. As a result, the original Great Old One (opens in a new tab) would still be a complete Great Old One (opens in a new tab) (although "They" would lose certain associated symbolisms), and the Sefirah (opens in a new tab) that was split away can be used to create a new Great Old One (opens in a new tab).

After the two halves have self-completed, if the original Great Old One (opens in a new tab) could retrieve the other half of the Sefirah (opens in a new tab) but not the corresponding Uniquenesses (opens in a new tab), "They" would have "Their" own methods to accommodate (opens in a new tab), but not by immediately merging the two Sefirot into one, just like a True God (opens in a new tab) accommodating another adjacent pathway's Uniqueness (opens in a new tab). After the accommodation (opens in a new tab), "Their" state is a single-Sefirah (opens in a new tab) Great Old One (opens in a new tab) plus a half-Sefirah Great Old One (opens in a new tab).

  • Note that this is different from the state of a Great Old One (opens in a new tab) only losing the Uniquenesses (opens in a new tab) but not part of "Their" Sefirah (opens in a new tab).
  • For example, suppose initially the Original Creator (opens in a new tab) only tore away the Abyss Uniqueness (opens in a new tab) and Chained Uniqueness (opens in a new tab) but not the Tenebrous World (opens in a new tab), then the Mother Tree of Desire (opens in a new tab) would fall to a Half Great Old One (opens in a new tab).
  • However, if the Mother Tree of Desire (opens in a new tab) retrieved and accommodated the Tenebrous World (opens in a new tab) but not the two Uniquenesses (opens in a new tab), "She" would be a single-Sefirah (opens in a new tab) Great Old One (opens in a new tab) plus a half-Sefirah Great Old One (opens in a new tab).
  • Even though the number of "Sefirot" and Uniquenesses (opens in a new tab) are the same for the two cases above, the difference is because for the second case, the two halves of the Sefirah (opens in a new tab) have self-completed, and the Mother Tree of Desire (opens in a new tab) doesn't merge the two halves after the accommodation.

Reverse Corruption

Furthering one's understanding of the Sefirot or things related to them would inevitably result in reverse corruption, thereby establishing a certain connection.

Oaths and Contracts

Since the Symbolism doesn't disappear even when it's ownerless, Oaths made by True Gods (opens in a new tab) or Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab) without the authorities of Order or Contract would still hold effect with the Sefirot as the witness.

  • Oaths sworn upon a Sefirah (opens in a new tab) by a Sequence 0 (opens in a new tab) cannot be undone, even after the Sequence 0 (opens in a new tab) later accommodated said Sefirah (opens in a new tab) and ascended to a Great Old One (opens in a new tab).
  • Although certain Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab) like the Son of Chaos (opens in a new tab) and the Lord of Mysteries (opens in a new tab) have means to resist the backlash and reduce the damage caused by breaching the Contract made with "Their" own Sefirot as witnesses, it won't be without a price. "They" would need to consider the consequences.

Usage as a Source of Power

There are certain methods or abilities for lower-ranked entities to borrow powers from the Sefirot. However, there is no such method without truly harming oneself. As long as one borrows the power of a Sefirah (opens in a new tab), they inevitably will be corrupted. The only questions are whether there is more or less corruption, and whether they can resist it.[[22]](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot#cite_note-22)

  • For example, one can deliberately awaken the Godhood (opens in a new tab) within themself to gain an instantaneous burst of Sefirah (opens in a new tab) authority, but this would make the Innate Great Old One (opens in a new tab) presiding over "Their" Pathway awaken more and more likely to revive in "Their" body.

Gaining preliminary control of the Sefirah (opens in a new tab)'s power is an important prerequisite for accommodating (opens in a new tab) said Sefirah (opens in a new tab). Through certain methods, mediums, or items related to the Sefirah (opens in a new tab), one can establish a mystical connection with the Sefirah (opens in a new tab), and walking a tightrope: bearing a certain level of corruption without losing control or going mad. Over time, "They" would be able to control more and more of the Sefirah (opens in a new tab)'s power, and could eventually reaching the level of a Half Great Old One (opens in a new tab) controlling the Sefirah (opens in a new tab) without needing to accommodate (opens in a new tab) it.

Daoism

A passive or semi-activated (opens in a new tab) Sefirah (opens in a new tab) can serve as a source of Boon (opens in a new tab), which forms the basis of the Daoism (opens in a new tab) system used in the Western Continent (opens in a new tab). Different from praying to Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab), there exists special ways, shortcuts, or tricks to avoid some negative effects.

  • An Imperial Conferment is equivalent to a Boon (opens in a new tab), where one was granted a position within the Church or a deity's kingdom. Through this role, they gained the corresponding powers. It's not a direct gift from a deity (the Sefirah (opens in a new tab)) but rather a ceremonial act performed by the leader of that school of thought, acting on behalf of the deity and bestowing a specific title. This more structured boon system not only linked the power of boons to a specific role but also emphasized the role of the deity's proxy.
  • Receiving Scriptures/Talismans/Ordination is a method to bear a certain level of corruption from the Sefirah (opens in a new tab) without losing control or going mad, thereby gaining more and more control over its power.

The strongest cultivators under the Daoism (opens in a new tab) system are the Dao Integrators (opens in a new tab). At normal state, "They" are similar to Sequence 0 (opens in a new tab), slightly weaker. If "They" sacrifice "Themselves" in outburst, "They" could reach a Tier 1 Half Great Old One (opens in a new tab).

Note: Sefirot can give Sequence 1 Boon (opens in a new tab) without the corresponding Beyonder Characteristics (opens in a new tab), by consuming and occupying part of the their own power. Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab) certainly wouldn't do this, but it's possible with Sefirot that don't have complete consciousness.

Power-burst Abilities

Certain Pathways and Sequences also possess some Power-burst abilities to borrow powers from the Sefirot. For example:

  • The Justiciar Pathway's Order Proxy, which relies on authorization.
  • If the Sefirah (opens in a new tab) gives permission, "They" can act as a proxy. Otherwise, there must have a compliant procedure. This presents a problem: when the Sefirah (opens in a new tab) has no consciousness and operates via symbolism, one needs to figure out specific methods to possibly succeed.
  • The Twilight Giant Pathway's Proxy, which relies on allegiance to the Sefirah (opens in a new tab).
  • Without true allegiance, "They" cannot use as much power.
  • The Red Priest Pathway's Sacrifice, which relies on sacrifice.
  • The quality of the sacrifice, etc., falls under the underlying rules and needs to meet the corresponding conditions.

These Power-burst abilities that rely on external forces have a timely process, and cannot immediately happen when the user wants it. This might give the other side time to react and the possibility to prepare.

During Sacrifice or Proxy, the target pointed to is the corresponding symbolism, which always exists regardless of whether there's a corresponding Great Old One (opens in a new tab) or a Sefirah (opens in a new tab) Consciousness.

Generally, by using these Power-burst abilities:

  • A Sequence 1 can reach a King of Kings of Angels (opens in a new tab) level for around 3 seconds without harming "Themselves", and a Tier 3 Half Great Old One (opens in a new tab) if "They" accept certain death.
  • A Sequence 0 can reach a Tier 1 Half Great Old One (opens in a new tab) for 2-3 seconds, potentially shorter, if "They" accept certain death, and if all conditions are satisfied (which might not be possible in real situations).

Activation

Innate Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab) are the embodiments and symbols of the corresponding Sefirot, thus "Their" indestructible spirit: "Their" True Godhood (opens in a new tab): are most concentrated in "Their" Sefirot. In specific situations, the consciousness and will of the Innate Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab) can shatter, leading to the corresponding Sefirot to become inactive, such as:

  • When the Original Creator (opens in a new tab) awakened and split the Sefirot of the Mother Goddess of Depravity (opens in a new tab), Mother Tree of Desire (opens in a new tab), and Son of Chaos (opens in a new tab) in half, causing the torn portion to be initially incomplete and doesn't have the corresponding will and consciousness.
  • When the Primordial God Almighty (opens in a new tab) and the Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth for Blessings (opens in a new tab) defeated and shattered the will of the other Sefirot on Earth, making them take a long time to recover.

Methods of Sefirot Activation

Inactive Sefirot could slowly Activate (opens in a new tab) on their own because of their indestructible True Godhood (opens in a new tab), but this process could take a long time. Among the inactive Sefirot on Earth, the Brood Hive (opens in a new tab) recovered the fastest because of "Its" symbolism, and could already self-complete and enjoyed "Its" complete form in the First Epoch (opens in a new tab).

  • A highly-activated (opens in a new tab) Sefirah (opens in a new tab) without spiritual impurities is close to Great Old One (opens in a new tab) status but are not yet one, and would be a Tier 0 Half Great Old One (opens in a new tab).

During the natural Activation (opens in a new tab) process, Sefirot can experience external influences, like from the Dao Integrators (opens in a new tab) who become part of the Sefirah (opens in a new tab)'s consciousness at death. The fusion and influence of generations of Dao Integrators (opens in a new tab) imparted some degree of consciousness, thoughts, emotions, and inclinations to the Sefirot in the Western Continent (opens in a new tab) beyond the base primal instincts and pure symbol of the godhood "They" represent.

  • Because of the spiritual impurities from the Dao Integrators (opens in a new tab), even if the Sefirot are completely Activated (opens in a new tab) in this way, "They" are only a Tier 2 Half Great Old One (opens in a new tab), as contrast to a highly-activated (opens in a new tab) Sefirah (opens in a new tab) without spiritual impurities.

History

In the Cosmos (opens in a new tab)

Under normal circumstances, the Sefirot couldn't be separated. Only when the Original Creator (opens in a new tab) split and created an embodiment of convergence and separation at the end of the Pre-Epoch (opens in a new tab) did some of the Sefirot tear apart, allowing them to be sucked into Earth. This in turn made those Sefirot become incomplete at the beginning. However, since the Sefirot had the ability to mend themselves, both the portions that stayed with the Outer Deities (opens in a new tab) and the portions that was sucked into Earth eventually became complete, and the latter can be used to create new Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab).

Three of the Outer Deities (opens in a new tab) that were directly nurtured and created from the Original Creator (opens in a new tab) - the Mother Goddess of Depravity (opens in a new tab), the Mother Tree of Desire (opens in a new tab), and the Son of Chaos (opens in a new tab) - originally possessed their own complete Sefirah (opens in a new tab). However when the Original Creator woke up, part of "Their" Sefirah (opens in a new tab) was torn apart along with some of "Their" Pathways, making "Them" lose "Their" corresponding symbols and authorities. For the Mother Goddess of Depravity (opens in a new tab) who was originally one of the three Pillars, losing the two Beyonder pathways of Moon and Mother and a part of her Sefirah - the Brood Hive (opens in a new tab) - made "Her" not much different from the other Outer Deities (opens in a new tab), though "Her" symbol as a Pillar (opens in a new tab) remained. Thus, "She" could only use a portion of Pillar (opens in a new tab) "She" symbolizes, unless "She" fused with the Brood Hive (opens in a new tab) again.

Ever since the split, the three Outer Deities (opens in a new tab) above had been trying hard to reclaim "Their" Sefirot, which is why "They" are most concerned and proactive when it comes to invading the real world. Asides from "Them", there are also seven other Outer Deities (opens in a new tab) that are looking to obtain the neighboring Sefirot, Uniquenesses (opens in a new tab), and High-Sequence Beyonder Characteristics (opens in a new tab) on Earth that "They" can accommodate.

  • Each of "Them" can accommodate another Sefirah (opens in a new tab) that has similar, close, or adjacent symbolism to "Their" own. This would make "Them" equal in power to the Pillars, though "They" still wouldn't possess the symbolism of Pillars.
  • Although "They" can't further accommodate the Sefirah Castle (opens in a new tab) and the Chaos Sea (opens in a new tab) since they are Sefirot of Pillars, occupying one or two Uniquenesses (opens in a new tab) and the corresponding Sequence 1 Beyonder Characteristics (opens in a new tab) is not a problem. "Their" power will increase slightly, and "Their" abilities will become more diverse. Moreover, even though "They" can't accommodate the Sefirah Castle (opens in a new tab) and the Chaos Sea (opens in a new tab), there're still suitable way for "Them" to use them.

On Earth

In the Pre-Epoch (opens in a new tab), in "His" sleep, the Original Creator (opens in a new tab) had naturally split into multiple personas, each corresponding to a different Great Old One (opens in a new tab). They were also in a state of slumber, but they began to use the authority and Beyonder Characteristics (opens in a new tab) they controlled to influence the world, making many preparations for the true dissociation when the Original Creator (opens in a new tab) awakened. For example, the Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth for Blessings (opens in a new tab) had captured humans in the modern era into the Sefirah Castle (opens in a new tab) as a part of "His" backup resurrection plan. The Ancient Sun God (opens in a new tab) also fell into the Chaos Sea (opens in a new tab) during this era.

At the end of the Pre-Epoch (opens in a new tab), the Original Creator (opens in a new tab) awakened, causing three Sefirot and six Pathways to be sucked to Earth from the Outer Deities (opens in a new tab) in the Cosmos (opens in a new tab). In the First Epoch (opens in a new tab), some of the active Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab) were Outer Deities (opens in a new tab) who had been directly attracted to Earth, and some came alive as Sefirot. In other words, some Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab) were equivalent to the Original Creator (opens in a new tab) - embodiments of the different personalities "He" split into.

After the Original Creator (opens in a new tab) awakened, "His" two dominant split entities - the Primordial God Almighty (opens in a new tab) and the Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth for Blessings (opens in a new tab) - leveraged "Their" long-prepared ancient schemes across the times of the Old Earth (opens in a new tab) to gradually gain advantages in "Their" fight against the other Sefirot. The two Pillars eventually defeated the other Sefirot and shattered "Their" wills (excluding the Brood Hive (opens in a new tab) and the Key of Light (opens in a new tab)), making those wills take a very long time to recover and awaken again.

In the early-mid stages of the First Epoch (opens in a new tab), the Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth for Blessings (opens in a new tab) and the Primordial God Almighty (opens in a new tab) had used different methods to grasp the additional Sefirot. The Celestial Worthy (opens in a new tab) accommodated the Nation of Disorder (opens in a new tab) and the River of Eternal Darkness (opens in a new tab), while the Primordial God Almighty (opens in a new tab) accommodated the City of Calamity (opens in a new tab) and the Tenebrous World (opens in a new tab). This made "Their" inclination to converge more intense, making it impossible for "Them" to control "Themselves", until "They" began taking actions against each other. Since then, the two Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab) that could be called Pillars had completely disappeared, and the rest of the Sefirot other than the Chaos Sea (opens in a new tab) and the Sefirah Castle (opens in a new tab) had been sealed in the Western Continent (opens in a new tab) by the Celestial Worthy (opens in a new tab).

Along with the fading will of the Original Creator (opens in a new tab), the seal on the Western Continent (opens in a new tab) gradually weakened with time. The Sefirot would instinctively or consciously corrode the Uniqueness (opens in a new tab) and High-Sequence Beyonder Characteristics (opens in a new tab) within their symbolic range to influence the outside world. The first to seep out energy was the River of Eternal Darkness (opens in a new tab), which formed a distributary through the Phoenix Ancestor Gregrace (opens in a new tab) in the Second Epoch (opens in a new tab). After that, in the Fourth Epoch (opens in a new tab), more Sefirot traces began to appear on the Northern (opens in a new tab) and Southern Continents (opens in a new tab) and the islands over the Five Seas (opens in a new tab). This is an important factor hidden behind many of the anomalies in this era. For example:

  • Salinger (opens in a new tab) had tried to use the River of Eternal Darkness (opens in a new tab)' distributary in the outside world to fuse with the Red Priest Uniqueness (opens in a new tab) to create a brand new Great Old One (opens in a new tab) path, but "He" failed and went mad. "He" wasn't able to resist the convergence desire for the Twilight and Evernight pathways, causing "Him" to target the Orthodox Gods and start the Pale Era (opens in a new tab).
  • The City of Calamity (opens in a new tab) left reflections outside the Western Continent (opens in a new tab): the headquarters of the Medici Family (opens in a new tab) was situated on Bansy Harbor (opens in a new tab), the Fourth Epoch Trier (opens in a new tab) where Alista Tudor (opens in a new tab) perished, and the city Morora (opens in a new tab) where Salinger's Blood Banner (opens in a new tab) was sealed.
  • The Nation of Disorder (opens in a new tab) corrupted the Justiciar Uniqueness (opens in a new tab), forming the Trunsoest Brass Book (opens in a new tab).
  • The River of Eternal Darkness (opens in a new tab) has various distributaries, such as in Fourth Epoch Trier (opens in a new tab) and Calderón City (opens in a new tab).
  • The Knowledge Moor (opens in a new tab) left remnants in the Fourth Epoch Trier (opens in a new tab) and in Avalon (opens in a new tab). The birth of the Hidden Sage (opens in a new tab) is related to the Brood Hive (opens in a new tab)'s influence on the Knowledge Moor (opens in a new tab) on the Western Continent (opens in a new tab).

On the Western Continent (opens in a new tab), the Sefirot become the basis for the Orthodox Sect System (opens in a new tab) to gain supernatural power, through praying to different semi-active Sefirot for Boons (opens in a new tab). After the fusion and influence of generations of Dao Integrators (opens in a new tab) in the Western Continent (opens in a new tab), the Sefirot became semi-activated (opens in a new tab) and developed "Their" own consciousnesses.

  • The Nation of Disorder (opens in a new tab) became the thirty-three-layered Heavenly Court. Its consciousness came alive as the Celestial Thearch (opens in a new tab).
  • The River of Eternal Darkness (opens in a new tab) became the Netherworld / Yellow Springs, and was the Sefirah (opens in a new tab) of the Haoli Lineage (opens in a new tab).
  • The Key of Light (opens in a new tab) became the Buddhist Kingdom of Light, and was the Sefirah (opens in a new tab) of the sects of the monastic community. Its consciousness came alive as the Golden Buddha (opens in a new tab).
  • The Knowledge Moor (opens in a new tab) was the Sefirah (opens in a new tab) of the Celestial Master (opens in a new tab)'s sect.
  • The City of Calamity (opens in a new tab)'s consciousness came alive as the Malevolent Dragon (opens in a new tab).
  • The Tenebrous World (opens in a new tab) became the Sefirah (opens in a new tab) of the Master of the Shadow Cottage (opens in a new tab)'s sect.
  • The Brood Hive (opens in a new tab), who had awakened self-awareness and enjoyed "Its" complete form in the First Epoch (opens in a new tab), was worshipped by the Luo Sect (opens in a new tab) as the Unborn Ancient Mother. It was unknown whether, or to what extent, "It" was affected by the Dao Integrators (opens in a new tab).
