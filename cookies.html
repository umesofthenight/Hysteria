Cookie policy

Updated 9 September 2026

The page itself does not set cookies or save preferences in browser storage. The Discord widget is off by default. If you allow it, Discord may use its own cookies.

What are cookies?

A cookie is a small piece of information that a service stores in your browser. Cookies and similar storage can remember a preference or recognize a device. Some are needed for a service you request; others are used for optional functions or tracking.

Storage on this site

These pages do not set first-party cookies or use local storage or session storage. They contain no analytics scripts or advertising pixels. The logo and font load from the site's own assets.

Your widget choice stays in the open page's memory. It is not saved as an identifier or sent to an analytics service. Leaving or reloading the page clears it.

The host's access and security services may set cookies independently of these pages. The website does not set their names or lifetimes. Hosting details are in the privacy policy.

The widget makes no request to Discord until you allow it. Once it loads, Discord receives technical information and may use cookies to run the widget and measure its performance.

Allowing or turning off the widget

Use the widget privacy controls on the home page. "Allow Discord widget" loads it; "Keep widget off" leaves it blocked. Doing nothing also leaves it off. You can use the server invite with either choice.

To withdraw permission, select "Turn off widget and withdraw consent" below the widget. This removes it and stops further requests from that embedded instance. It cannot take back information already sent or erase Discord's cookies. You can manage those through your browser's privacy settings or Discord's controls.

When we ask for permission

The landing page has no optional tracking of its own, so it has no site-wide consent banner. We ask for permission at the Discord widget before loading it. That choice covers the widget alone and does not authorize unrelated tracking by the host or another service.

Consent rules depend on the storage technology, its purpose, and the law that applies. Cookies strictly necessary for a requested service may be treated differently from optional storage. The ICO's guidance on storage and access technologies explains the UK rules.

Cookies controlled by providers

Discord controls its widget's cookies. Their names, lifetimes, and behavior can vary with the service, your browser, and your Discord settings. Discord's cookie policy and privacy policy describe its data use and available controls.

The cookies used by the host and the live widget have not yet been checked for the site's final public hosting setup. This policy describes the current page code.

Policy updates and contact

Before adding optional analytics or another embedded service, we must update this policy and its consent controls. For a privacy question, open a private staff ticket in the Discord server and ask for the owner. Our privacy policy explains the rest of the site's data use.
