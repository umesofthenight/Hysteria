Privacy policy

Updated 9 September 2026

You don't need a website account to read these pages. We don't run visitor analytics or keep a visitor database. The Discord widget connects only after you allow it.

Who runs this website?

The owner of Hysteria runs this website. For a privacy question or request, contact staff in the Discord server and open a private ticket addressed to the owner. Keep personal and sensitive details out of public channels.

This policy covers the website and its policy pages. Activity inside Discord, including messages, character submissions, moderation, and accounts, is covered by the server rules and Discord's policies.

What happens when you open a page

Your browser sends the host your IP address, the page address, the time of the request, and information about your browser. Hosting and security services may keep request logs to serve pages, maintain the service, and prevent abuse.

The website has no forms asking for your name, email address, date of birth, precise location, or payment details. It does not require an account of its own. There are no visitor analytics, advertising pixels, session recordings, or fingerprinting scripts in these pages.

Who hosts the site

OpenAI Sites hosts this version on Cloudflare infrastructure. The host's sign-in and security services may use cookies and keep technical records independently of the page.

OpenAI's privacy policy and Cloudflare's privacy policy explain how those providers use data. Their services may process information outside your country, under the arrangements set out in their policies.

If you load the Discord widget

Selecting "Allow Discord widget" connects the widget to Discord. Until then, it makes no connection. Loading it sends technical information to Discord, which may also use cookies. Discord controls the widget and how it uses that information. Read Discord's privacy policy and cookie policy for details.

Permission lasts for the current page visit and is not saved in cookies or browser storage. Select "Turn off widget and withdraw consent" to remove the widget and stop further requests from it. This cannot take back information already sent or erase cookies Discord has set.

You can use the invite without allowing the widget. The invite opens Discord, where Discord's own policies apply.

Why the data is used

Technical information is used to deliver and protect the site. Where applicable, that processing relies on the legitimate interest in running a working, secure website. The optional widget requires your permission. If you contact staff about the website, they use the information you send to handle your request and meet any legal obligations.

The landing page is not used to sell personal information, build advertising profiles, or make automated decisions about visitors.

How long information is kept

This website has no visitor database. Reloading or leaving a page clears its widget choice. The hosting providers and Discord set the retention periods for their own records under their policies; this website does not control those periods.

Staff should keep information about a website request only as long as needed to handle it or meet legal obligations. Send only the details needed to explain your request.

Your choices and rights

You can keep the widget off or withdraw permission after loading it. Your browser's privacy settings let you manage stored cookies. Depending on the law that applies, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information, restrict or object to its use, and receive a portable copy.

For information held by Hysteria, contact the owner through a private staff ticket. For information a provider holds independently, contact that provider. You may also complain to your local data protection authority. Withdrawing permission does not change the lawfulness of processing that took place before withdrawal.

Young visitors and updates

Members must be at least 14 and meet Discord's age requirement in their country if it is higher. This website does not collect age-verification documents. Do not send identity documents or other sensitive details through it.

We will revise this policy if the site's data use changes and update the date above. You can also read the cookie policy and terms and conditions.
