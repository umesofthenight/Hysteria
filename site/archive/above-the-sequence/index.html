Above The Sequence

Beyond Sequence 0 and the boundaries of godhood.

Great Old One, or Great Old Dominator, refers to a level above Sequence 0 (opens in a new tab), where one becomes a direct representation of a certain aspect of the universe. To be a complete Great Old One (opens in a new tab), one needs to accommodate the corresponding Sefirah (opens in a new tab), plus the Uniqueness (opens in a new tab) and at least one Sequence 1 Beyonder Characteristics (opens in a new tab) of each Pathway (opens in a new tab) in the group.

Above the Sequence is an umbrella term that refers to existences involving the Sefirot or higher. It includes Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab), Outer Deities (opens in a new tab), Half Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab), and Quasi Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab).

On Earth, there are nine Sefirot corresponding to the nine Above the Sequence groups and twenty-two Standard Pathways (opens in a new tab). In the Cosmos (opens in a new tab), there is an unknown number of Outer Deities (opens in a new tab) that are Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab). By year 1358 of the Fifth Epoch (opens in a new tab), the ten most powerful Outer Deities (opens in a new tab) in the entire Universe had gathered around the solar system. Some of "Them" wished to retrieve "Their" Sefirot and Beyonder Characteristics (opens in a new tab) that had been ripped from "Them" and were attracted to Earth when the Original Creator (opens in a new tab) awakened in the Pre-Epoch (opens in a new tab). Some of "Them" had the hope of getting neighboring Sefirot, Uniquenesses (opens in a new tab), and High-Sequence Beyonder Characteristics (opens in a new tab) on Earth that "They" could accommodate.

To Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab), the number of authorities has a certain connection to level and status, but not entirely. The corresponding characteristics, power, and symbols are equally important.

To put "Their" power in perspective, Klein (opens in a new tab) surmised that the other Sequence 0 (opens in a new tab) deities (excluding the Evernight Goddess (opens in a new tab) should "She" become a Great Old One (opens in a new tab)) would be able to tie down one or two Outer Deities (opens in a new tab) together, and that it would be considered a miracle. It would take nine to fight one, or even more.

General Powers/Characteristics of Great Old Ones

  • All Authorities and Traits of Gods, at a higher level.
  • Universal Symbolism: "They" become a direct representation of a certain aspect of the universe.
  • "They" are able to respond to prayers across the universe and exert influence on it as a whole in a general way. "They" can also apply "Their" symbols in any known location.
  • For example, the Calamity of Destruction (opens in a new tab) can reduce or increase the frequency of disasters across the entire universe, or create disasters in any known location.
  • However, in actual combat, the immediate area of effect is usually limited to a single Star System (opens in a new tab) or the radiation zone of a black hole, which is achieved mostly by reality (opens in a new tab)-oriented symbolisms like those of the Supernova Dominator (opens in a new tab) or Calamity of Destruction (opens in a new tab). "Their" short term symbolic impacts also remained mostly confined to this range, with only minor, manageable disruptions spreading throughout the universe. The farther from the battlefield, the weaker the disturbance.
  • A Star System (opens in a new tab) refers to tens or hundreds of times the size of the Solar System (opens in a new tab), which is the range "They" can impact in a single strike. Afterwards, the impact will continue to spread outward until it dissipates, with the final range of influence reaching the size of a Galaxy (opens in a new tab).
  • "Their" sensing range does not exceed a large Star System (opens in a new tab) unless pre-established mystical connections existed in areas outside this range.
  • In addition, when becoming a Great Old One, there is a strengthening of related symbolism across the entire Universe which is a one time benefit thing. Under normal circumstances, a Great Old One would need to spend time allowing their symbolism to spread.
  • Using Authorities and Symbols does not consume spirituality.
  • Boon Bestowal:
  • Pathway Reorganization: The number of Pathways "They" inherently own is fixed. When giving Boons (opens in a new tab), "They" can split or merge "Their" own Pathways; however, "Their" Symbols and Uniquenesses (opens in a new tab) are core; everything else can be split or recombined based on this.
  • Different Pathways cannot be completely merged, they are merely mixed together, with a "chemical reaction". For example, some abilities from the two Pathways cannot be mixed.
  • Boon Quantity and Quality: Since Boons (opens in a new tab) come at the expense of "Their" own power, the higher level of the Boons (opens in a new tab) and the more Boons (opens in a new tab) "They" grant, the greater the burden to "Them". At a high enough level, there is even a chance to affect "Their" state.
  • At the Sefirah (opens in a new tab) level: It's not possible to grant a Boon (opens in a new tab) that involves the Sefirah (opens in a new tab), such as at the King of Kings of Angels (opens in a new tab) level.
  • At the Uniqueness (opens in a new tab) level:
  • "They" can grant Boons (opens in a new tab) at the level of a True God (opens in a new tab), but only for a brief period of time, otherwise it will affect "Their" own stability.
  • Granting Boons (opens in a new tab) at the level of a King of Angels (opens in a new tab) that involves the Uniqueness (opens in a new tab) is a very large burden.
  • At the Sequence 1 level: The number of Sequence 1 Boons (opens in a new tab) "They" can give depends on the number of Uniquenesses (opens in a new tab) and Sequence 1 Beyonder Characteristics (opens in a new tab) "They" possess. Assuming "They" possess the Uniqueness (opens in a new tab) and all three Sequence 1 Beyonder Characteristics (opens in a new tab) of each Pathway, "They" could give out at most two Sequence 1 Boons (opens in a new tab) per Pathway (opens in a new tab), unless if it's a special Pathway like the Visionary Pathway.
  • Granting a Sequence 1 Boon (opens in a new tab) will cause one of "Their" own Sequence 1 Beyonder Characteristics (opens in a new tab) to lose its functionality, retaining only the foundation.
  • It is possible to grant Boons (opens in a new tab) at the level of a King of Angels (opens in a new tab) that doesn't involve the Uniqueness (opens in a new tab), although granting a King of Angels (opens in a new tab) Boon (opens in a new tab) comes at the cost of one or even two less Sequence 1 Boon (opens in a new tab) Angels (opens in a new tab).
  • "They" could theoretically bestow Sequence 1 Boon without requiring the corresponding characteristic by consuming and occupying part of "Their" Sefirah (opens in a new tab)'s own power, but "They" would certainly not do so.
  • At the Angels (opens in a new tab) level: The number of Angels (opens in a new tab) "They" can create with purely Boons (opens in a new tab) and no Beyonder Characteristics (opens in a new tab) depends on the number of Pathways "They" own.
  • Reverse Mystical Connection:
  • Knowledge-based Corruption: All existences at the level of the Sefirot and above have the characteristic that, once an individual knows enough information about "Them", "They" can establish a connection to that individual and cause corruption through that connection. Only existences at the Angel (opens in a new tab) level or above can sever this connection in some ways.
  • For Great Existences, anyone who invoked "Their" former names and stole "Their" followers would establish a mystical connection with "Them", allowing "Them" to curse, doom, or even kill the offenders, or seize their body.
  • Unified Consciousness: "Their" Consciousness is a unified whole, indivisible even when in a consciousness space or under similar circumstances. This is the distinction between Dual-Pathway True Gods (opens in a new tab) and Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab).
  • If one has not achieve a unified consciousness, "They" cannot be considered a complete Great Old One (opens in a new tab) even if "They" possess the Sefirah (opens in a new tab) and all necessary Uniquenesses (opens in a new tab) and Sequence 1 Beyonder Characteristics (opens in a new tab). "They" could only temporarily display power close to that of a Great Old One (opens in a new tab).
  • Law of Beyonder Characteristics Convergence: Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab) can unleash massive suction force using the Law of Convergence (opens in a new tab) .
  • Even if weakened, "They" could still directly attract the Uniquenesses (opens in a new tab) and Beyonder Characteristics (opens in a new tab) that once belong to "Them", making them break through any existing seal to arrive at "Their" side: as long as there was no opposition on the same level.
  • "They" could unleash the Convergence force from "Their" Sefirah (opens in a new tab) that could indiscriminately pull every being within the Star Dominion (opens in a new tab) towards "Them", with entities closer to "Them" reaching "Them" first.
  • "Their" Convergence force could attract unresistant Sefirot, and Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab) towards "Them"; it could also fling "Them" towards those entities at the same time.
  • Universal Domain/Dimension: All Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab) have dimension/realm which spans the universe.
  • Astral World: "They" can transform things like stars into Concepts and transport them via the Astral World.
  • Information Control: All Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab) can control and influence their own information that is reflected on the Spirit World.

Levels of Great Old Ones

According to Madam Magician (opens in a new tab), there are three levels of Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab):

  1. Most Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab) on Earth and Outer Deities (opens in a new tab) in the Cosmos (opens in a new tab) are at the first level. "They" can still accommodate another Sefirah (opens in a new tab) that has similar, close, or adjacent symbolism to "Their" own. This would make "Them" equal in power to the Pillars, though "They" still wouldn't possess the symbolism of Pillars.
  2. Between the first and third levels, there's the second level: the Pillars. "They" are the foundation for the universe's maintenance and development and possess special symbolisms, each supporting one of the three layers of the world: the Spirit World, the Astral World, and the Physical World.
  3. The Original Creator (opens in a new tab) is at the third level, representing the ultimate, representing chaos, beginning and end, representing the unknowable and undiscussable. No existence can surpass this level, and this level can only have one being, and it's very unstable because both convergence and separation are "His" instincts.

Relative Strength of Non-Pillar Great Old Ones

Analogically, if the max score for a Non-Pillar (opens in a new tab) Great Old One (opens in a new tab) is 100:

  • Direct children of the Original Creator (opens in a new tab) at complete form = 100.
  • Other Outer Deities (opens in a new tab) = 90–95.
  • Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab) directly originated from the Original Creator (opens in a new tab) ≈ 95. If they leverage their unique traits, they can exceed 100. For example, a fully realized Calamity of Destruction (opens in a new tab) (merging the Original Creator (opens in a new tab)'s masculine and feminine aspects) would be outright overpowered.

In addition, it will be hard for the complete Mother Tree of Desire (opens in a new tab) and the Son of Chaos (opens in a new tab) to fight two other Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab) at the same time.

Categories of Great Old Ones

Innate Great Old Ones

Innate Great Old Ones are also referred to as True Deities. In a narrow sense, only the following can be called True Deities:

  • The Original Creator (opens in a new tab).
  • The personas split from "Him".
  • "His" three direct offspring.
  • The Outer Deities (opens in a new tab) born from "His" union with the Mother Goddess of Depravity (opens in a new tab).

This is the condition that subsequent advancers can never achieve. Even if they truly become Great Existences (opens in a new tab), they would only be newly born Above the Sequence.

True Godhood

True Deities possess True Godhood, which is the indestructible spirit left in "Their" Sefirot and the corresponding Uniquenesses (opens in a new tab) and Beyonder Characteristics (opens in a new tab). This is the godhood that every later arriver must contend with. The higher the level of a Beyonder (opens in a new tab) following "Their" pathways is and the more knowledge of "Them" are known, the more "Their" remnant psyche awakens on the Beyonder (opens in a new tab)'s body, increasing the chance for "Them" to revive on or take control of the Beyonder (opens in a new tab). Even after "Their" death, "They" have a chance to revive through "Their" indelible psyche within the Sefirot, Uniquenesses (opens in a new tab), and Beyonder Characteristics (opens in a new tab) that once belonged to "Them".

  • The essences of godhood in different Beyonder Characteristics (opens in a new tab) are different, and is symbolized by the sequence name. The purpose of the Acting Method is to deceive the mental imprint of the True Deities within the Beyonder Characteristics (opens in a new tab) and digest it bit by bit, by acting and pretending to align with the godhood inside them. However, since acting and digesting means getting closer to the nature of the godhood, if one is too immersed in acting or if one digests the potion at the Uniqueness (opens in a new tab) level, it's easy to lose their sense of self, defeated by godhood.

After "They" achieve a near-resurrection on a Beyonder (opens in a new tab)'s body, "Their" consciousness would significantly strengthen. Without hundreds or thousands of years to wear it down, none of "Their" pathways are suitable for birthing a corresponding Sequence 0 (opens in a new tab).

Only the Original Creator (opens in a new tab) and the Fourth Pillar (opens in a new tab) can destroy "Their" spirit and will completely, and leave "Them" unable to resurrect.

Godhood Instincts

Godhood's tone is fundamentally madness, chaos, filled with desires for destruction and creation. In addition, each True Deity possess "Their" own Godhood Instinct, which is determined by "Their" Sefirot and Uniquenesses (opens in a new tab) (and the symbolisms they represent), affecting "Their" tendency to love or destroy.

  • For example, whenever a situation arose that could threaten the entire universe, the godhood instinct of the Circle of Inevitability (opens in a new tab) would compel "Him" to perform redemption, since this was perhaps "His" most fundamental symbolism and the reason "He" had been born as the child of the Original Creator (opens in a new tab) and the Mother Goddess of Depravity (opens in a new tab).

If True Deities were to lose a part of "Their" Sefirah (opens in a new tab), depending on which symbolisms and authorities is torn away, "Their" tendency to love or destroy would change accordingly. For example:

  • After re-accommodating the Brood Hive (opens in a new tab), the Mother Goddess of Depravity (opens in a new tab) would become more "motherly" in comparison to when "She" only possessed the Physical World and Evil symbolisms and authorities.
  • The complete Mother Tree of Desire (opens in a new tab) had a certain inclination toward temperance of controlling desire. However, this changed after the "Temperance" part of "Her" was torn away.

Convergence Instinct

True Deities have an inherent Godhood Instinct for Convergence upon oneself. The higher the level, the stronger the instinct. If one exceeds the level of the Pillar (opens in a new tab), just accommodating an additional Sefirah (opens in a new tab) will lead to being controlled by the convergence instinct in an irreversible manner. "They" would be unable to restrain "Themselves" from actively consuming more Sefirot until "They" become the embodiment of the entire Universe, which is also the innate instinct of the Original Creator (opens in a new tab).

Emotions beyond Godhood Instincts

Beyond "Their" Godhood Instinct, True Deities would still have the most basic self-awareness of a living being's core and various instincts: selfishness, greed, survival, indifference, only caring about oneself, only doing things beneficial to oneself, not influenced or constrained by group morals, group interactions, or group perceptions. "They" can experience anger and violence, and can exhibit extreme and maddening tendencies and emotions.

Advancing Great Old Ones

Through the Paths of the Divine, Beyonders (opens in a new tab) can also ultimately complete the convergence of the required Sefirah (opens in a new tab), Uniquenesses (opens in a new tab), and Sequence 1 Characteristics to become a newly-born Great Old One (opens in a new tab). At this point, in terms of status and strength, "They" are the same as Innate Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab), with the difference being that the newly-born Great Old One (opens in a new tab) does not possess the characteristic of eternally indestructible will.

Advancement Condition

To become a complete Great Old One (opens in a new tab), one has to satisfy the following conditions:

  • Accommodated the Sefirah (opens in a new tab), plus the Uniqueness (opens in a new tab) and at least one Sequence 1 Beyonder Characteristic (opens in a new tab) of each Pathway in the group.
  • Completely merged with the above components to form a cohesive whole and achieved a Unified Consciousness.

Below are some known specific methods for different existences to achieve the above conditions.

For Beyonders (opens in a new tab) following the Pathway System

There is no advancement ritual required to become a Great Old One (opens in a new tab) since no ritual can change the awakening of the Original Creator (opens in a new tab)'s will, but the order of accommodation can improve self-awareness to some extent and enhance the success rate:

  1. Become a Sequence 0 (opens in a new tab) of a pathway, then control and fuse with the Sefirah (opens in a new tab), and lastly, accommodate the other Uniquenesses (opens in a new tab). This is the best option.
  2. Become a Dual-Pathway True God (opens in a new tab), then control and fuse with the Sefirah (opens in a new tab), and lastly, accommodate the other Uniquenesses (opens in a new tab). This is neither the best nor the worst option. This has hope of success, but not a great chance.
  3. Fuse with the Sefirah (opens in a new tab) last. This is the worst option.

Essentially, integrating neighboring Uniquenesses (opens in a new tab) before fully accommodating the Sefirah (opens in a new tab) would enhance the will and spirit of the Innate Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab) (i.e., Godhood (opens in a new tab)) inside the Sequence 0 (opens in a new tab)'s body, accelerating "Their" recovery and significantly disadvantaging the Sequence 0 (opens in a new tab) in "Their" struggle to accommodate the Sefirah (opens in a new tab). The more additional Pathways the Sequence 0 (opens in a new tab) accommodated, the harder it is for "Them" to succeed.

The above is the general approach, though some Pathways also have their own unique details, like the Calamity of Destruction (opens in a new tab) Pathways.

For Dao Integrators (opens in a new tab)

A Dao Integrator (opens in a new tab) can consume potion, advancing from Sequence 9 to Sequence 0, and retrieve the remaining Uniquenesses (opens in a new tab) and Sequence 1 Beyonder Characteristics (opens in a new tab) to become a Great Old One (opens in a new tab).

For Activated Sefirot

The Sefirot sealed in the Western Continent (opens in a new tab) came alive after the fusion and influence of generations of Dao Integrators (opens in a new tab) in the Western Continent (opens in a new tab). Since "Their" consciousnesses are interwoven with too many external spirits, even with all the components, "They" cannot immediately undergo a fundamental transformation, and might take decades or centuries for a new, Unified Consciousness to emerge, enabling a Great Old One (opens in a new tab) to truly manifest in reality.

Sefirot Accommodation Limit

  1. For complete Pillars: The limit is the Pillar (opens in a new tab) Sefirah (opens in a new tab) by itself.
  • The Pillar (opens in a new tab) is the limit of stable existence and suppression of the Convergence instinct. If a Pillar (opens in a new tab) accommodates one more Sefirah (opens in a new tab), "They" will lose control over "Their" convergence instinct and will actively consume more Sefirot until "They" converge into the Original Creator (opens in a new tab), causing "His" revival. Once this process begins, it can only be interrupted by "Their" complete fall and division.
  • Similarly, a Non-Pillar (opens in a new tab) Great Old Dominator (opens in a new tab) cannot further accommodate a Sefirah (opens in a new tab) with Pillar (opens in a new tab) symbolism. However, occupying one or two neighboring Uniquenesses (opens in a new tab) and the corresponding Sequence 1 Beyonder Characteristics (opens in a new tab) from a Pillar (opens in a new tab) is not a problem. "Their" power will increase slightly, and "Their" abilities will become more diverse. Besides, just because "They" can't accommodate a Pillar (opens in a new tab) Sefirah (opens in a new tab) doesn't mean there's no suitable way for "Them" to use it.
  1. For Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab) with torn Sefirah (opens in a new tab):
  • For the Mother Goddess of Depravity (opens in a new tab), who is a Pillar (opens in a new tab): The limit is the two halves of "Her" original Sefirah (opens in a new tab).
  • "She" could not accommodate other Sefirah (opens in a new tab) except the Brood Hive (opens in a new tab). If "She" accommodated other adjacent Sefirah (opens in a new tab) before retrieving the Brood Hive (opens in a new tab), "She" would exceed the critical value.
  • For the Mother Tree of Desire (opens in a new tab) and Son of Chaos (opens in a new tab), who are not Pillars: The limit is the two halves of "Their" original Sefirah (opens in a new tab), plus an additional Non-Pillar (opens in a new tab) Sefirah (opens in a new tab). For example:
  • The Mother Tree of Desire (opens in a new tab) can reunite with the lost Tenebrous World (opens in a new tab) to become the most powerful Great Old One (opens in a new tab) outside of the Pillars. In addition, "She" can still accommodate (opens in a new tab) an additional Non-Pillar (opens in a new tab) Sefirah (opens in a new tab) to gain power comparable to the Pillars, but without a corresponding Pillar (opens in a new tab) symbolism.
  • If an Outer Deity (opens in a new tab) accommodated the Nation of Disorder (opens in a new tab), "They" would in turn pursue the Uncertain Mist (opens in a new tab) to complete the Son of Chaos (opens in a new tab).
  1. For other Non-Pillar (opens in a new tab) Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab): The limit is "Their" Sefirah (opens in a new tab) plus an additional Non-Pillar (opens in a new tab) Sefirah (opens in a new tab).
  • A Non-Pillar (opens in a new tab) Great Old One (opens in a new tab) can still accommodate (opens in a new tab) another Non-Pillar (opens in a new tab) Sefirah (opens in a new tab) that has similar, close, or adjacent symbolism to "Their" own. This would make "Them" equal in power to the Pillars, though "They" still wouldn't possess the symbolism of Pillars.
