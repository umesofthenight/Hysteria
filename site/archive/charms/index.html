Creating and using charms in roleplay.

Charms

Charms are single-use Mystical Items created through Ritualistic Magic. They store or borrow supernatural power within a prepared medium, allowing the holder to produce a predetermined effect through the correct activation method.

Acquiring Charms

Charms can be acquired through three primary methods:

Trading

Charms can be purchased or traded from:

  • Other OCs.
  • Churches.
  • Secret Organizations.
  • Gatherings.

-# Only approved charms may be traded.

Rewarded

Charms may be issued as:

  • Mission/Event rewards.
  • Organization equipment.
  • Gifts from powerful figures.

Creation

Characters meeting the necessary requirements may create charms themselves. -# All original or custom charms must first be submitted through #𝖠bi̇li̇ty・𝖲ubmi̇ssi̇on.

Charm Creation

Charm creation requires a prepared [Mystical Medium], a valid source of [Mystical Power], and an exact ritual method.

Sources of Power

A Charm may obtain its power through one of the two methods:

  • Prayer and Bestowment
  • The creator performs a ritual directed towards a Deity, Angel, Saint, or another qualified existence and requests that power be bestowed into the charm.
  • Safe creation usually requires the creator to be:
  • A believer of the target.
  • A member of the target's organization.
  • A recognized subordinate or Blessed.

-# - Sequence 3 Saints can respond to prayers within a limited region.

  • Mystical Medium
  • The charm draws power from an object already carrying the corresponding supernatural influence.
  • Possible sources include:
  • Divine blood.
  • A Demigod level creature or characteristic.
  • A Sealed Artifact.
  • A temporary blessing stored within another object.

-# - While this method doesn't necessarily require worship of the original source, it is generally more dangerous.

Universal Requirements

Every charm creator must posses:

  • Sufficient Spirituality.
  • Sequence 9: Fool , Hanged Man , Darkness , Hermit , Moon .
  • Sequence 8: Error , Visionary , Sun , Tyrant , White Tower , Death , Demoness , Paragon , Wheel of Fortune , Mother , Abyss , Chained , Black Emperor , Justiciar
  • Sequence 7: Twilight Giant , Red Priest
  • Appropriate Ritualistic Magic knowledge.
  • A valid [Source of Power]
  • Correct physical medium and ritual ingredients.

Creation Requirements by Level

Low Level Charms

Low-Level Charms may be created by trained Beyonders with basic proficiency in Ritualistic Magic. Characters do not need to belong to a ritual-specialist Pathway, but if that is the case, they must have actually learned the process of creating one.

Mid-Level Charms

Mid-Level Charms require advanced ritual knowledge or formal organization training. Pathways such as Hermit, Fool, Darkness, Hanged Man, and other spiritually or mystically oriented Pathways will find this easier, but no Pathway receives free access.

High-Level Charms

High-Level Charms require a rare and sufficiently powerful source. Working directly beneath a Church or powerful organization is the most reliable route, but it is not the only possible method.

Demigod Charms

Demigod Charms are exceptional creations rather than ordinary crafted consumables. The creator does not necessarily need to be a Sequence 3 Saint or Sequence 2 Angel. However, a lower-Sequence creator must possess an extraordinary external source capable of providing and containing the power.
