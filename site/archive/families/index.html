Tudor Family

The Tudor Family was the royal family of the Tudor Empire (opens in a new tab) during the Fourth Epoch (opens in a new tab). They controlled the Black Emperor Pathway and through their ancestor, Alista Tudor (opens in a new tab), the Red Priest Pathway.

History

They were once nobles of the Solomon Empire (opens in a new tab), which ruled the Northern Continent (opens in a new tab) under the Black Emperor Solomon (opens in a new tab). "He" was supported by multiple angel families and by the True Creator (opens in a new tab). The high nobles at that time (i.e. families with dukes) included the Zoroast Family (opens in a new tab), Abraham Family (opens in a new tab), and Zaratul Family (opens in a new tab), plus the Medici Family (opens in a new tab) and Ouroboros (opens in a new tab). Tudor, Trunsoest (opens in a new tab), Augustus (opens in a new tab), and Castiya (opens in a new tab) were also nobles, albeit ranked lower than others.

The ancestor of the Tudor Family, Alista Tudor (opens in a new tab), along with Trunsoest (opens in a new tab), went on to overthrow the Solomon Empire and establish the Tudor-Trunsoest United Empire (opens in a new tab).

Later, with the imminent revival of the fallen Black Emperor (opens in a new tab), the Tudor-Trunsoest United Empire fell, and Alista Tudor, now called the "Blood Emperor", founded the Tudor Empire (opens in a new tab).

After the fall of the Blood Emperor in the War of the Four Emperors (opens in a new tab), the Tudor Empire collapsed. The angel families and supporters of the Tudor Empire, including the Tudor Family have been hunted by the seven orthodox gods since then.

Due to the Primordial Demoness (opens in a new tab)’s curse, all members of the Tudor Family except for Judith (opens in a new tab)’s descendants are dead.

Abraham Family

The Abraham Family was a powerful aristocratic family of the Tudor Empire (opens in a new tab) during the Fourth Epoch (opens in a new tab). They control the Door Pathway.

History

They were once nobles of the Solomon Empire (opens in a new tab), which ruled the Northern Continent (opens in a new tab) under the Black Emperor Solomon (opens in a new tab). "He" was supported by multiple angel families (opens in a new tab) and by the True Creator (opens in a new tab). The high nobles at that time (i.e. families with dukes) included the Zoroast (opens in a new tab), Abraham, and Zaratul families (opens in a new tab), plus the Medici family (opens in a new tab) and Ouroboros (opens in a new tab).

Their ancestor, Bethel Abraham (opens in a new tab), also called 'Mr. Door', was a King of Angels (opens in a new tab) of the Door Pathway, and was recognized as the angel who had the highest possibility to become a deity in the Warring Era (opens in a new tab). Despite the family's apparent high standing in the Solomon Empire, "He", along with the Abraham Family, turned to the Alista Tudor (opens in a new tab)'s side after the fall of the Black Emperor and the Solomon Empire. . They supported the establishment of the Tudor-Trunsoest United Empire (opens in a new tab).

After the collapse of the Tudor-Trunsoest United Empire, Abraham, Antigonus (opens in a new tab), Amon (opens in a new tab), Jacob (opens in a new tab), and Tamara (opens in a new tab) were the five great families that supported the establishment of the Tudor Empire (opens in a new tab), and they were second only to the Blood Emperor (opens in a new tab).

In the War of the Four Emperors (opens in a new tab), the family's ancestor, Bethel Abraham (opens in a new tab) went missing. The remaining High-Sequence Beyonders in the family all perished.

After the Blood Emperor perished in the War of the Four Emperors, the angel families of the Tudor Empire, including the Abraham Family fell from the top of their pedestals and were hunted by the Seven Deities (opens in a new tab).

Bloodline Curse

Since then, the Abraham Family has been under a terrible curse. They started to suffer from unknown, terrifying ravings which made advancing to high sequences nigh impossible. Generations after generations of ancestors attempted to revive the family, but without exception, all of them went mad and lost control.

Because of this, they started training people outside of their family to eventually stop the ravings, yet this backfired when those people started to eye the Sealed Artifacts (opens in a new tab) of the family. Some of the Beyonders (opens in a new tab) who stole their artifacts joined the True Creator (opens in a new tab). The main traitor was Botis (opens in a new tab), a Traveler who stole the Grade 0 Sealed Artifact, Box of the Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab), and advanced to Sequence 4 with the help of the True Creator. He became a Saint of the Aurora Order (opens in a new tab).

Afterward, the family separated so as not to affect other family members. As a result of the ravings, many members chose not to become Beyonders and risk losing control. The family has therefore been continuously declining in power and influence. However, as a former angel family with a long history, they still had quite a bit of resources, including land, tree farms, property, manors, and mines, albeit divided among various branch families. Moreover, they still possessed two Grade 0 Sealed Artifacts corresponding to Sequence 2 of the Door Pathway.

Sights in the Spirit World

Sights in the Spirit World was written by a certain ancestor of the Abraham Family. He recorded in it all the strange and wonderful things that happened when he traveled through the spirit world. It talks about the Great White Brotherhood (opens in a new tab) and his encounter with the Yellow Light Venithan (opens in a new tab). He also mentioned the curse of the Abraham Family.

The solution was in the future, in the hands of an Apprentice who was assisted by a hidden existence. [...] The day the curse was lifted was the beginning of the Abraham family’s true doom.

Bloodline Curse Removed

Klein Moretti (opens in a new tab) later discovered that the origin of the curse was their ancestor himself. Bethel Abraham (opens in a new tab) had been banished to an isolated space of storms and darkness by the Evernight Goddess (opens in a new tab) and the Lord of Storms (opens in a new tab) in the War of the Four Emperors (opens in a new tab). "His" constant pleas for help were what was making "His" descendants go mad.

Gehrman Sparrow (opens in a new tab) gave Dorian Gray Abraham (opens in a new tab) a way to temporarily lift the curse by changing his faith to Mr. Fool (opens in a new tab). Later, many members of the family also changed their faith to Mr. Fool.

After learning the truth of the curse from Fors Wall (opens in a new tab) as well as the way to solve it, Verdu Garcia (opens in a new tab), a member of the Abraham Family, decided to help Mr. Door return to the real world. He was tricked by Medici (opens in a new tab) and Amon (opens in a new tab) to hold a ritual to let Mr. Door out of his banishment. However, Mr. Door gave up his life to Amon. Amon sent Mr. Door's Beyonder Characteristics back to the Abraham Family. With the death of Mr. Door, the curse of the Abraham Family finally came to an end.

Antigonus Family

The Antigonus Family was a powerful aristocratic family of the Tudor Empire (opens in a new tab) during the Fourth Epoch (opens in a new tab). They control the Fool Pathway.

History

Their founder and ancestor, Antigonus (opens in a new tab), is a descendant of the Demonic Wolf Flegrea (opens in a new tab) and brother of Mother of the Sky (opens in a new tab).

The Antigonus Family was once an angel family (opens in a new tab) of the Solomon Empire (opens in a new tab) in the early Fourth Epoch. Despite the family's apparent high standing in the Solomon Empire (opens in a new tab), at some point, their ancestor, Antigonus (opens in a new tab) made a secret transaction with Alista Tudor (opens in a new tab) and eventually went over to the Blood Emperor (opens in a new tab)'s side after the fall of the Black Emperor (opens in a new tab) and the Solomon Empire. They supported the establishment of the Tudor-Trunsoest United Empire (opens in a new tab).

After the collapse of the Tudor-Trunsoest United Empire, Abraham (opens in a new tab), Antigonus, Amon (opens in a new tab), Jacob (opens in a new tab), and Tamara (opens in a new tab) were the five great families that supported the establishment of the Tudor Empire (opens in a new tab), and they were second only to the Blood Emperor.

After the Blood Emperor (opens in a new tab) perished in the War of the Four Emperors (opens in a new tab), the angel families of the Tudor Empire, including the Antigonus Family fell from the top of their pedestals and were hunted by the Seven Deities (opens in a new tab). The Antigonus Family was later destroyed by the Church of the Evernight Goddess (opens in a new tab). Their ancestor went mad and was sealed in the Foggy Town (opens in a new tab) on top of the Hornacis Mountain Range (opens in a new tab) by the Evernight Goddess (opens in a new tab).

During Klein's adventure in Foggy Town (opens in a new tab), he witnessed a group of transparent maggots that he suspected to be the mad angel of the Antigonus Family. The suspicion turned out to be true as the transparent maggots were indeed Antigonus (opens in a new tab) who had turned to a monster because "He" was unable to suppress the will of the Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth for Blessings (opens in a new tab).

The last of their descendants perished with the deaths of Ray Bieber (opens in a new tab) and his mother.

Amon Family

The Amon Family's founder and ancestor is Amon (opens in a new tab), also known as the Angel of Time, one of the Kings of Angels (opens in a new tab) from the Third Epoch (opens in a new tab) and a child of the Ancient Sun God (opens in a new tab).

In the Fourth Epoch, Abraham (opens in a new tab), Antigonus (opens in a new tab), Amon, Jacob (opens in a new tab), and Tamara (opens in a new tab) were the five great families that supported the establishment of the Tudor Empire (opens in a new tab), and they were second only to the Blood Emperor (opens in a new tab).

They were rumored to be a family of blasphemers, claiming to be descended from the Ancient Sun God (opens in a new tab).

After the Blood Emperor perished in the War of the Four Emperors (opens in a new tab), the angel families of the Tudor Empire, including the Amon Family fell from the top of their pedestals and were hunted by the Seven Deities (opens in a new tab).

Jacob Family

The Jacob Family was a powerful aristocratic family of the Tudor Empire (opens in a new tab) during the Fourth Epoch (opens in a new tab). They control the Error Pathway.

History

In the Fourth Epoch (opens in a new tab), Abraham (opens in a new tab), Antigonus (opens in a new tab), Amon (opens in a new tab), Jacob, and Tamara (opens in a new tab) were the five great families that supported the establishment of the Tudor Empire (opens in a new tab), and they were second only to the Blood Emperor (opens in a new tab).

Some time in the Fourth Epoch, the angel of the Jacob Family, Jacob (opens in a new tab) was killed by Amon (opens in a new tab) without anyone noticing. Amon pretended to be "Him" for a while and watched his descendants panic due to the disappearance of their ancestor.

After the Blood Emperor perished in the War of the Four Emperors (opens in a new tab), the angel families of the Tudor Empire, including the Jacob Family fell from the top of their pedestals and were hunted by the Seven Deities (opens in a new tab).

The descendants of the Jacob Family had hidden their treasure somewhere in Backlund (opens in a new tab), but this turned out to be part of a thousand-year trap set by Amon.

Currently, the Jacob Family has split into different smaller families, as they are afraid of being caught by Amon (opens in a new tab). They have established a secret organization with the descendants of the Zoroast Family (opens in a new tab), as well as other Beyonders of the Error Pathway, called the Hermits of Fate (opens in a new tab).

Tamara Family

The Tamara Family was a powerful aristocratic family of the Tudor Empire (opens in a new tab) during the Fourth Epoch (opens in a new tab). They control the Justiciar Pathway and the Door Pathway.

History

In the Fourth Epoch (opens in a new tab), Abraham (opens in a new tab), Antigonus (opens in a new tab), Amon (opens in a new tab), Jacob (opens in a new tab), and Tamara were the five great families that supported the establishment of the Tudor Empire (opens in a new tab), and they were second only to the Blood Emperor (opens in a new tab). The Tamara Family held a hereditary duke aristocratic title, akin to the Amon Family (opens in a new tab), albeit slightly lower in status. The Tamara Family had existed for many years before Alista Tudor (opens in a new tab) became the Blood Emperor. They were renowned nobles from the previous empire (opens in a new tab).

Originally they were in control of the Justiciar Pathway. Later they were in marriage alliances with the Abraham Family (opens in a new tab) for extended periods and gained control of the Door Pathway.

After the Blood Emperor (opens in a new tab) perished in the War of the Four Emperors (opens in a new tab), the Angel Families (opens in a new tab) of the Tudor Empire (opens in a new tab), including the Tamara Family fell from the top of their pedestals and were hunted by the Seven Deities (opens in a new tab).

Descendants of the Tamara Family members can produce the corresponding Mirror People (opens in a new tab) in the Mirror World under some unknown conditions. This isn't limited by location or environment. This is because the Tamara of the Fourth Epoch (opens in a new tab) suffered from some relevant corruption before the War of the Four Emperors (opens in a new tab). By the Fifth Epoch (opens in a new tab), some Mirror People (opens in a new tab) have successfully replaced their real selves in the real world, such as Moran Avigny (opens in a new tab).

Internal Divisions

The Tamara Family had their coat of arms changed once and had two internal divisions. This is why their members were buried on different levels of the Trier catacombs (opens in a new tab) at different times. The change in the coat of arms represents a significant change in the Tamara Family. It shifted from being dominated by the Justiciar Pathway to being dominated by the Door Pathway. A small number of members of the Tamara Family, who adhere to the Justiciar Pathway, have survived to this day and are secretly active. The Justiciar branch and the Door branch has been estranged from the other one for many years, refusing to acknowledge each other.

Justiciar Branch

The Justiciar branch also experienced a division at some point. The exact situation is unknown. By the Fifth Epoch (opens in a new tab), the Justiciar branch had splintered into three factions:

  • Those who believe in The Truth (opens in a new tab) and are members of the School of Truth (opens in a new tab).
  • According to Jebus Lata (opens in a new tab), the School of Truth (opens in a new tab) was founded with some members of the Tamara Family as its core.
  • Before Project Vortex (opens in a new tab), the School of Truth (opens in a new tab) faction was looking for three things: clues to the Fallen Heaven (opens in a new tab) in the Underground Trier (opens in a new tab), Roselle (opens in a new tab)'s Final Mausoleum, and the Magic Wishing Lamp (opens in a new tab).
  • Those replaced by the Mirror People (opens in a new tab).
  • Those who adhered to their original traditions. This faction had cut ties with the School of Truth (opens in a new tab) members and has been trying to deal with them.
  • This is the group that Alicia Tamara (opens in a new tab) belongs to. They don't identify with the Theosophy Order (opens in a new tab) (i.e., the Door branch), and also act against those that are looking for the Magic Wishing Lamp (opens in a new tab).
Door Branch

The Door branch established a close connection with the Demoness Family (opens in a new tab) during the Tudor Empire (opens in a new tab)'s era. During the Fifth Epoch (opens in a new tab), a secret organization (opens in a new tab) known as the Theosophy Order (opens in a new tab) emerged. It had Demoness figures and originated from Tamara's Door Pathway. There's reason to believe that they are still cooperating. Lumian Lee (opens in a new tab) suspected that the Door branch is also fractured because of their connection with Demoness Family (opens in a new tab), making them have a greater risk of being replaced by the Mirror People (opens in a new tab).

Trunsoest Family

The Trunsoest Family was the royal family of the Trunsoest Empire (opens in a new tab) during the Fourth Epoch (opens in a new tab). They controlled the Black Emperor Pathway and through their ancestor, Trunsoest (opens in a new tab), the Justiciar Pathway.

History

They were once nobles of the Solomon Empire (opens in a new tab), which ruled the Northern Continent (opens in a new tab) under the Black Emperor Solomon (opens in a new tab). "He" was supported by multiple angel families and by the True Creator (opens in a new tab). The high nobles at that time (i.e. families with dukes) included the Zoroast (opens in a new tab), Abraham (opens in a new tab), and Zaratul families (opens in a new tab), plus the Medici family (opens in a new tab) and Ouroboros (opens in a new tab). Tudor (opens in a new tab), Trunsoest, Augustus (opens in a new tab) and Castiya (opens in a new tab) were also nobles, albeit ranked lower than others.

The ancestor of the Trunsoest Family, Trunsoest (opens in a new tab), along with Alista Tudor (opens in a new tab), went on to overthrow the Solomon Empire and established the Tudor-Trunsoest United Empire (opens in a new tab).

Later, with the imminent revival of the fallen Black Emperor (opens in a new tab), the Tudor-Trunsoest United Empire (opens in a new tab) fell, and Trunsoest (opens in a new tab), now called the "Night Emperor", founded the Trunsoest Empire (opens in a new tab). "He" also gained the support of the Sanguines (opens in a new tab) through "His" marriage with the Blood Moon Queen Auernia (opens in a new tab).

In the aftermath of the War of the Four Emperors (opens in a new tab), only the Trunsoest Empire (opens in a new tab) left behind by the Night Emperor remained. However, while trying to reap the final fruits of victory, the four noble families under the Trunsoest Empire (opens in a new tab) - Augustus (opens in a new tab), Einhorn (opens in a new tab), Sauron (opens in a new tab), and Castiya (opens in a new tab)⁠ - split the empire, and destroyed the royal Trunsoest Family which was lacking in high-level members. These four families established the Four Great Nations of the Northern Continent, under the support of different orthodox gods.

Bloodline Curse

The Sauron Family had been in decline due to a bloodline curse affecting their members. Two hundred years ago, the family's angel (opens in a new tab), Vermonda Sauron (opens in a new tab) became the first victim of the curse. "He" had vanished suddenly. But "He" was still alive, residing somewhere in the underground chamber of Red Swan Castle. The hearts of the Sauron Family's core members were often extracted through a unique ritual and used to suppress "Him". The entire underground maze of Red Swan Castle was linked to perilous Sealed Artifacts (opens in a new tab), designed to limit Vermonda Sauron (opens in a new tab)'s movements to the greatest extent. This has had a profound impact on generations of the Sauron Family, driving them to the brink of insanity.

Removal of the Bloodline Curse

Unknown to most, below Red Swan Castle of the Sauron Family lay a node for the sealing of Fourth Epoch Trier (opens in a new tab). Vermonda Sauron (opens in a new tab), who was a Sequence 1 Conqueror of the Red Priest Pathway, had gone mad and entered the upper levels of Fourth Epoch Trier (opens in a new tab). His frenzied spirit lingered at the seal, inextinguishable. His anguished roars echoed, affecting everyone in Red Swan Castle and those of the same bloodline. This was the cause of the Sauron Family's bloodline curse (opens in a new tab), which had plagued them for generations. This bloodline curse was similar to the Abraham Family's (opens in a new tab) curse caused by their previous King of Angels (opens in a new tab) Bethel Abraham (opens in a new tab).

The curse of the Sauron Family was finally lifted with death of Vermonda Sauron (opens in a new tab) in Fourth Epoch Trier (opens in a new tab) at the hand of Red Angel Medici (opens in a new tab), who subsequently ascended to a King of Angels (opens in a new tab) after 2081 years.

Sanguines

Sanguines are what we usually call vampires. They live a very long life, drink blood, and sleep in coffins. They are usually born with the power of sequence 7 of Moon Pathway, and can convert humans into their kind, by using excess characteristics, and the sign of becoming a mature sanguine is fully grasping their power. When Sanguines worship the Moon they are referring to their ancestor Lilith (opens in a new tab) (whose authority was the moon), not the Primordial Moon (opens in a new tab).

They distinguish themselves from what they call “artificial vampires” i.e. sequence 7 human Beyonder of Moon Pathway, who usually are believers of the Primordial Moon (opens in a new tab). They hate these people because their sequence 7 Beyonder characteristics often come from Sanguines.

Sanguines promote Sequence through rituals and drink potions like humans. They also need to use the Acting Method to digest the potion. But because of the limited number of Beyonder characteristics, it is extremely hard to promote within the clan. One needs to wait for many years until someone of the sequence level dies, but high-level sanguines can live for thousands of years. Most sanguines stayed at the level they were born with. Due to the same reason, it is also extremely hard for them to bear new babies because they need to have spare Beyonder characteristics ready. A noble Sanguine is able to distinguish each person based on their different smell and blood characteristics. According to Emlyn (opens in a new tab), the most outstanding potion masters are all from the Sanguine, which is logical since the Sequence 6 of the Moon Pathway is Potions Professor.

Sanguines have a unique set of names for each sequence in the Moon Pathway after sequence 7, based on noble titles: Baron (Sequence 6), Viscount (5), Earl (4), Marquis (3), Duke (2), Queen (1), and Sanguine Ancestor Lilith on top of all. There are 3 Dukes / Sequence 2 in the Sanguines clan right now who rule the clan.

According to Emlyn (opens in a new tab), the total number of important existences at the angel level, including ancient Sanguines that lived since the Second Epoch (opens in a new tab) to this day and Grade 0 Sealed Artifacts (opens in a new tab), didn’t exceed five in number.

Characteristics

Red eyes are a trademark characteristic of Sanguines. According to Emlyn (opens in a new tab), every red-eyed human had a Sanguine ancestor. Humans begin to develop this characteristic at Sequence 7. There was a long time when humans and Sanguines copulated in the Fourth Epoch (opens in a new tab). They were all residents of the Trunsoest Empire (opens in a new tab); hence, with the widespread fellowship, many descendants were produced. There was an increasing number of red-eyed mixed-bloods as they passed down their genes, becoming an uncommon eye color for humans.

Their heart beats relatively slowly, and it is one of the Sanguine’s fatal weaknesses. Compared to humans, the Sanguine had a long lifespan. Even without a noble title, ordinary ones could live about three hundred years old. Under such circumstances, they could slowly accumulate and occupy every level, preventing any excess in Beyonder characteristics.

According to Derrick Berg (opens in a new tab), in the Forsaken Land of the Gods (opens in a new tab), the so-called Vampire was quite a commonly seen monster in the depths of darkness. They are very ferocious. Their bodies run with pus and are very disgusting. Like giants and elves, the Sanguines seem to be descendants of humans who mutated because of Beyonder characteristics. After many generations, they were able to purge the incompatible characteristics.

History

The Sanguines were ruled by the Sanguine Ancestor Lilith (opens in a new tab) in the Early Era of Fire (opens in a new tab) in the Second Epoch before “She” perished together with the Mutated King Kvastir (opens in a new tab) and the Demonic Wolf Flegrea (opens in a new tab).

After Lilith (opens in a new tab)'s fall, the  ![Moon Symbol2 (opens in a new tab)](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Moon_Pathway) Moon Uniqueness (opens in a new tab) was seized and eventually ended up in the hands of the Evernight Goddess (opens in a new tab). The Beauty Goddess Beyonder Characteristic was retrieved by the Sanguines and became a sacred object. At that time, the First Blasphemy Slate (opens in a new tab) was not yet known to the world, and the Sanguines did not dare let their Sanguine Dukes ascend recklessly. Auernia (opens in a new tab) also did not dare to risk it.

After the Catastrophe in the Third Epoch (opens in a new tab), the Sanguines left the central stage of the Northern (opens in a new tab) and Southern Continent (opens in a new tab) and became nobles of different empires or kingdoms as individuals instead of as a race. They either governed a territory or guarded a castle in key areas. This continued until the Blood Moon Queen Auernia (opens in a new tab) became the wife of the Night Emperor Trunsoest (opens in a new tab). "She" gathered Sanguines as a clan again and they became an important supporter of this dynasty. At some point during this period, Auernia (opens in a new tab) used the Sanguines' sacred relic - the Beauty Goddess originally from the Sanguine Ancestor Lilith (opens in a new tab) - to concoct a potion, completed the ritual, and became a dual-Sequence 1 King of Angels (opens in a new tab).

After the War of the Four Emperors (opens in a new tab), Queen Auernia died, and the Sanguines suffered a terrible blow. The Trunsoest Empire (opens in a new tab) was split by the four current royal families, and the Sanguines had no choice but to retreat to the uninhabited mountains to hide in the shadows so as to maintain the continuation of the race. Fortunately, the Seven Gods were fractured back then and the four countries attacked each other. After paying a certain price, the Sanguines finally escaped the disaster.

Out of Auernia (opens in a new tab)'s two Beauty Goddess Beyonder Characteristics (opens in a new tab), "Her" original characteristic was seized by the Earth Mother (opens in a new tab), fearing the exposure of "Her" identity and latent corruption. The other characteristic bearing Lilith (opens in a new tab)'s spiritual imprint and Auernia (opens in a new tab)'s spiritual remnants was retrieved by the Sanguines and once again became a sacred object, the Shadow of the Beauty Goddess (opens in a new tab). It was later stolen by the Rose School of Thought (opens in a new tab), who used it to create the public identity of the Primordial Moon (opens in a new tab).

In the Fifth Epoch (opens in a new tab), they hid in all walks of life and had completely integrated into human society.

As for those who were unable to control their own destructive urges and bloodlust, they were either sent to castles deep in the mountains or were dealt with in advance to prevent official Beyonder organizations from finding clues, including the Church of the Earth Mother (opens in a new tab) that usually converts Sanguines to become their priests.

After the 1350 World War (opens in a new tab) in the Fifth Epoch, the Sanguines merged with the Church of the Earth Mother (opens in a new tab), with Emlyn White (opens in a new tab) as the representative of the Sanguines in Backlund (opens in a new tab).
