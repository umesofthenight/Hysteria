Gods

Deities and the authorities they embody.

Evernight Goddess

The Evernight Goddess is the Goddess of the Church of the Evernight Goddess. "She" was a Subsidiary Goddess of the Annihilation Demonic Wolf Flegrea known as the Goddess of Misfortune in the Second Epoch.

Although it is widely believed that as the Lady of Crimson, the Evernight Goddess' symbol is The Moon, Mr. Door has said that the Tarot Card that better represents "Her" is The Star. "She" lost authority over the moon after giving away the Moon Uniqueness to Lilith at the end of the 1350 World War.

"She" was the first god Klein became familiar with by joining the Nighthawks. After escaping from the Death Mausoleum, Klein finally confirmed that the Evernight Goddess has been watching him closely ever since the start and that he is actually "Her" Blessed, and receives immediate responses and help from "Her". "She" has since helped Klein multiple times afterwards.

It is revealed later that "She" is a Transmigrator from the modern era that first came out of the Sefirah Castle.

With the arrival of The Apocalypse, "She" completed "Her" trials and accepted the position of Sect Master for the Haoli Sect. "She" was certified by the Underworld Daoist and became the Eternal Darkness with authorities over the Darkness, Death and Twilight Giant Pathways.

"She" is the Goddess worshipped by the Church of the Eternal Darkness.

Farbauti

The Devil Monarch Farbauti is a Survivor from the Modern Era and one of the Ancient Gods from the Second Epoch. "He" is a Sequence 0 God of the Abyss Pathway, ascended in the period after the previous Father of Devils Primordial God Almighty died.

In addition, "He" has a Sequence 1 Abomination Beyonder characteristic that was later given to Naboredisley, "His" other identity.

According to Amon, "He" is the only surviving Ancient God from the Second Epoch (this statement excludes Lilith who survived under a false identity). "He" is also known as the Dark Side of the Universe and is currently in the Abyss. "He" is worshiped as a god by the Blood Sanctify Sect.

Later on, "He" was corrupted alongside the Chained God Tolzna by the originator of the Great Old One Father of Devils, the Mother Tree of Desire. "He" restored lucidness during the Fifth Epoch with the rise of the new Calamity of Destruction and just before The Apocalypse.

God of Knowledge and Wisdom

The God of Knowledge and Wisdom is the Sequence 0 God of the White Tower Pathway and the God of the Church of the God of Knowledge and Wisdom. After the Battle of Violated Oath Lenburg, Masin, and Segar believe in "Him".

"He" was a Subsidiary God of the Dragon of Imagination Ankewelt known as the Dragon of Wisdom in the Second Epoch and the Wisdom Angel under the Ancient Sun God in the Third Epoch.

Klein suspects that the two items he gained from Edwina Edwards, Groselle's Travels and a key that opens the gate to where Sasrir sleeps in Giant King's Court, were given by God of Knowledge and Wisdom intentionally.

Eternal Blazing Sun

The Eternal Blazing Sun is the Sequence 0 God of the Sun Pathway and the deity worshipped by the Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun. The Intis Republic follows this faith.

God of Steam and Machinery

The God of Steam and Machinery, is the Sequence 0 True God of the Paragon Pathway and the god of the Church of the God of Steam and Machinery.

In the Fifth Epoch, because of Roselle Gustav's exploits, "He" sent a sacred commandment to change the title God of Craftsmanship to God of Steam and Machinery.

From 1352 to 1359, The Fool's Dream allowed "Him" to improve "His" knowledge of human civilization, and become an ally of the Tarot Club.

After the Hunting Hidden Sage event in year 1359 of the Fifth Epoch, "He" acquired the Uniqueness of the Hermit Pathway.

In The Apocalypse, "He" joined the Celestial Master's sect as "His" true disciple and obtained the Dao Name True Blessing. "He" joined the Celestial Master's formation to help "Him" become a temporary Quasi-Demon of Knowledge to fight against the Outer Deities.

Grisha Adam

[Adam]

Adam, also known as the Angel of Imagination, was one of the Kings of Angels from the Third Epoch and a child of the Ancient Sun God. "He" was born as a Sequence 1 Author with the Uniqueness of the Visionary Pathway.

When "He" first appeared in Book 1, "He" had already accommodated two sequence 1 Beyonder characteristics and the Uniqueness of the Visionary Pathway.

At the end of Volume 4, "He" gained the last sequence 1 Beyonder characteristic (0-08 - Quill of Alzuhod). Adam completed the advancement ritual at the end of volume 6 and finally became a Sequence 0 Visionary. As of Book 2, Adam and the True Creator have fully fused, with Adam becoming the current True Creator.

[Grisha]

The Ancient Sun God, named Grisha, is the human Survivor from the Pre-Epoch who woke up from the Chaos Sea in Chernobyl at the beginning of the Second Epoch. "He" became the human savior who killed the Ancient Gods and led humans to dominate the world in the Third Epoch. "He" was a Quasi-God Almighty, and was extremely close to being a Great Old One. "He" had accommodated all required Uniquenesses and Sequence 1 Characteristics of all 5 pathways, and was almost equivalent to the owner of the Chaos Sea through Sasrir and the First Blasphemy Slate. "He" also possessed the Error Uniqueness. "He" had 8 Kings of Angels following "Him":

  • Dark Angel Sasrir
  • Angel of Fate Ouroboros
  • Red Angel Medici
  • Wind Angel Leodero
  • Wisdom Angel Herabergen
  • White Angel Aucuses
  • as well as "His" two sons;
  • Angel of Time Amon
  • Angel of Imagination Adam

"He" died in the hands of Rose Redemption and betrayed by Leodero, Aucuses, and Herabergen. "His" death caused the Forsaken Land of the Gods to be cursed and separated from the world. The Second Blasphemy Slate appeared after "His" death. "His" humanities and extreme emotions at "His" death fused with "His" Hanged Man Pathway characteristics and the Hanged Man Symbol2 authority of Degeneration, together with "His" negative personality to create the True Creator; while "His" godhood awoke within Adam.

[True Creator]

The True Creator, is the Sequence 0 God of the Hanged Man Pathway. "He" is considered to be evil. "He" claims to be the inheritor of the Almighty Creator (Ancient Sun God) who was worshiped during the Second Epoch and Third Epoch. Klein figured out that the True Creator is equivalent to an Evil Spirit of the Ancient Sun God, formed after "He" retrieved Sasrir's personality and perished. As of Book 2, Adam and the True Creator have fully fused, with Adam becoming the current True Creator. It is believed by "His" worshipers that the True Creator inherited the core of the Original Creator. The temple of the True Creator showed paintings claiming that when the Cataclysm in the Third Epoch took place, the Orthodox Gods came from the Abyss while the True Creator endured all the sins and saved the world. His believer from Aurora Order also claims that every human being had divine qualities, and as long as they persevered and made it through the countless trials, they would be able to accumulate enough divine qualities to become Angels. Other Gods are merely powerful Angels. The True Creator's followers believe that there exists a holy residence where the True Creator once lived. They seem to know the direction as the little boy Jack successfully entered the Forsaken Land of Gods and into the True Creator's Temple.

[Sasrir]

Sasrir was the leader of the 8 Kings of Angels under the Ancient Sun God, known as the Dark Angel, and one of the founders of the Rose Redemption. Ancient Sun God created Sasrir with part of "His" personality, the Authority of Degeneration and characteristics of the Hanged Man Pathway, in order to wield the Chaos Sea and separate "Himself" from corruption. "He" also held the First Blasphemy Slate. According to the priest Snowman of the Third Epoch, Dark Angel Sasrir was the leader of the Kings of Angels, the one closest to the Ancient Sun God. Sasrir was the first Angel to be created by "Him", "His" left hand, "His" proxy, and the Deputy of Heaven. According to Arrodes, there was a myth that when the Ancient Sun God was born, light and darkness were integrated. Later, "He" separated the darkness in "His" body and created the first angel with it and one of "His" ribs, and that is the origin of Dark Angel Sasrir. This was later confirmed by Amon, who says that Ancient Sun God separated a part of "His" characteristics and "His" negative personality, and created Sasrir with "His" ribs. In "His" conversation with Klein, Evil-spirit Sasrir revealed that soon after Ancient Sun God got out of Chaos Sea, "He" noticed that the Primordial God Almighty might awake in "His" body. "He" created Sasrir with part of "His" personality, Authority of Degeneration, and characteristics of Hanged Man Pathway, in order to wield the Chaos Sea and separate "Himself" from corruption.

Hidden Sage

The Hidden Sage is a quasi-Sequence 0 of the Hermit Pathway. "He" is the Hermit Uniqueness coming to life and acquiring sentience through some unknown means.

Lord of the Storms

The Lord of Storms, is the Sequence 0 God of the Tyrant Pathway and God of the Church of the Lord of Storms. "He" is one of the two official religions in the Loen Kingdom. The royal family of Loen, Augustus Family, worships the Lord of Storms. Sailors on the sea, including pirates, mostly worship "Him", even in countries that worship another deity.

Lilith

Lilith was once an Ancient Goddess of the Moon Pathway, known as the Sanguine Ancestor Lilith, and was believed to have perished during the Second Epoch.

In fact, "She" faked "Her" death then took on the identity and destiny of Omebella, the Earth Mother, In this role, Lilith ascended to Sequence 0 on the Mother Pathway, becoming both a Goddess of that pathway and of the Church of the Earth Mother.

After the end of 1350 World War, "She" came into possession of the Uniqueness of the Moon Pathway.

It was later revealed that "She" was one of Survivors who survived the Earth Cataclysm.

Mother Goddess of Depravity

The Mother Goddess of Depravity is one of the Outer Deities from the Cosmos that attempted to invade Earth. "She" is also known as the Primordial Moon, The Great Mother, the Sovereign of the Physical World and the Mother of All Things. "She" has been occupying the Moon since the First Epoch, terraforming it to the Crimson Moon.

"She" is the first child of the Original Creator, and is one of "His" three direct children along with the Mother Tree of Desire and the Son of Chaos. "She" is also the ruler of all feminine power of the Cosmos.

"She" was the original owner of the Mother and Moon Pathways. "She" is also the current owner of the Chaos Primogenitor Pathway.

"She" is the Sovereign of the Physical World, one of the three Pillars that are the foundation for the universe's maintenance and development. When the Original Creator split into pieces, "He" created a force strong enough to tear the Sefirot apart. Part of "Her" Sefirah was torn from "Her" and became the Brood Hive, which was attracted to Earth along with the Moon and Mother characteristics "She" held. Then the Mother Goddess of Depravity became not much different from the other Outer Deities, but still with the symbol of Pillar on "Her".

Besides the Mother Goddess of Depravity, the Mother Tree of Desire, and the Son of Chaos who were directly born from the Original Creator, the remaining Outer Deities were all born from the union of the Mother Goddess of Depravity and the Original Creator. "They" are the first batch of beings created by "Her", thus "She" is the true Mother of All Things.

"She" is said to have a conflict with the Mother Tree of Desire, but "They" have also cooperated.

Authorities & Symbolisms

"She" possesses full authority over the Chaos Primogenitor Pathway and partial authorities over the Mother Pathway and Moon Pathway (through accommodating the Brood Hive but without the Uniquenesses), and the corresponding symbols, which include but are not limited to the following:

  • Mother: Authorities over Mother, Earth, Nature, Death, and Life.
  • Moon: Authorities over Summoning, Beauty, Spirituality, Life, Darkness, and Bizarreness with the symbol of the Moon.
  • Chaos Primogenitor: Authorities over Reproduction, Propagation, Reality, and the Symbol of Nurturing the nascent Original Creator of the next Universe.
  • This Pathway is a mix of two different Pathways:
  • The Pathway of Original Sin, Evil and Corruption, which includes the Exploitation of Life. The Sequence 0 name is Chaos Primogenitor.
  • The Pathway of Reality and Procreation. The Sequence 0 name is World.

To match the Mother Goddess of Depravity's Honorific Names to "Her" Pathways and Authorities:

  • "Origin of Evil" - Chaos Primogenitor Pathway
  • "The Indestructible" - Physical World (Reality) Authority
  • "Sovereign of the Physical World" - World Pathway

Derived from the above, "She" also possesses the following abilities/symbols:

  • Sovereign of the Physical World: One of the most important symbolisms for the Mother Goddess of Depravity is "Her" symbolism as a Pillar. "She" possesses control over the Physical World, one of the three layers of the world.
  • A Pillar is slightly stronger but has no fundamental difference compared to the other Great Old Ones. In general, by relying on the unique symbolism of a Pillar, "She" has the status and power to contend with two or three other Great Old Ones at most.
  • However, the Mother Goddess of Depravity is invincible as a support, and as the wielder of the The Indestructible symbolism, except for scenarios where "She" faces the Fourth Pillar, the Original Creator, or overextends the Chaos Primogenitor symbolism, "She" will never perish even when facing hundreds of Great Old Ones at once.
  • The Indestructible: Reality is the foundation of the universe. Unless everything ended, reality would eternally exist. Thus, the Soul and the body of Mother Goddess of Depravity possessed the characteristic of Indestructibility, never to perish except three scenarios:
  • Encountering the Original Creator, who could make the impossible happen.
  • The emergence of the Fourth Pillar symbolizing the end of everything.
  • Overextending the Chaos Primogenitor's unique symbolism.
  • As long as "She" wishes, "She" cannot be Banished or Sealed.
  • "She" can transform into a colossal, ethereal oak tree, rooted deeply in reality, supporting the heavens, and intertwining "Herself" with the fabric of the real universe, to prevent "Herself" from being Banished or Sealed.
  • "She" can "fix" mystical connections, spirits, and consciousness, among other things, in place, disrupting interference and influences from other entities. This is symbolized as an illusory, slightly damaged oak tree.
  • The Mother Goddess of Depravity solidified the mystical connection between the Mother Tree of Desire and Tenebrous World, disrupting Klein's Marionettes' attempt to Graft the connection back to themselves.
  • The Mother Goddess of Depravity "fixed" the Goddess of Fate's consciousness and spirit in place, disrupting Lord of Mysteries Klein's Theft and saving "Her" from the fate of being Sealed into the Chained materials.
  • Unlike the Lord of Mysteries, "She" can't Seal off reality to prevent unauthorized entry or expel all adversaries, however, "She" can impose a continuous weakening effect on targets within reality.
  • As the Sovereign of the Physical World, "She" possess the authority of "Foundation", making all Outer Deities rely on "Her" for "Their" existence, which is why "She" cannot be Destroyed or Sealed.
  • Genesis: "She" and the God Almighty both hold a portion of the Genesis symbolism - opposing the symbolism of Termination of the Fourth Pillar - with the God Almighty holding a bigger portion.
  • Motherhood/Maternal Instinct: Excluding Mother Tree of Desire and Son of Chaos, "Her" union with the Original Creator gave birth to all other Outer Deities. "They" are the first batch of beings created by "Her", thus "She" is the true Mother of All Things and The Great Mother.
  • This symbolism later became part of the Goddess of Origin, the Great Old One title corresponding to the Brood Hive that was separated from "Her" and attracted to Earth.
  • "She" later reaccommodated the Brood Hive during The Apocalypse, but did not retrieve the Mother Uniqueness and Moon Uniqueness.
  • In the First Epoch, the Primordial God Almighty and the Brood Hive gave birth to Omebella and Zedus, making certain arrangements. In The Day of Many Disasters, the birth of Omebella strengthened the symbolism of the Mother in mysticism, allowing the Mother Goddess of Depravity to bypass the Astral Barrier to descend on Earth.
  • Chaos Primogenitor: "She" is the Nurturer of the Chaos Primogenitor - the Original Sin and the nascent Original Creator of the next universe, it symbolizes the new beginning of the universe and thus cannot be completely destroyed. This is one of "Her" two most vital symbols, and a key reason "She" remains a Pillar even after the Brood Hive's separation.
  • Origin of Evil: The Pathway of the Chaos Primogenitor is the Pathway of Evil and Corruption, which includes the Exploitation of Life. As such, "She" possesses the corresponding Honorific Name and symbolism of the Origin of Evil.
  • "She" can invoke the special symbolism of the Chaos Primogenitor once every Major Month (2,160 years).
  • The normal heartbeats of the Chaos Primogenitor helped "Her" accelerate the accommodation of the rebelling Brood Hive, despite being disrupted by the countless Marionettes and Avatars from the Lord of Mysteries.
  • The extremely special heartbeat of the Chaos Primogenitor about to be born and "Its" imminent entry into reality shattered the Astral Barrier that originated from the Original Creator, as if its original master had recalled it.
  • The Chaos Primogenitor is no exception from the Fourth Pillar's Termination Symbolism, but with the destruction of the universe and the beginning of a new cosmic cycle, it means a new Chaos Primogenitor is born and grows.
  • Invoking this special symbolism would put "Her" in a state of prolonged weakness for centuries that cannot be recovered using New Life.
  • If "She" borrowed this special symbolism for less than 2,160 years, the Chaos Primogenitor would truly descend into the real world - a devastating catastrophe to "Her", and could cause "Her" to perish despite the symbolism of The Indestructible.
  • New Life: "She" can give New Life to "Herself", Souls, consciousness, spirits, intellect, and Beyonder powers.
  • Using New Life on "Herself":
  • Rebirth: "She" can initiate New Life to reset "Her" form, reverting "Herself" to an embryonic state for Rebirth. This process would reset all symbols associated with "Her", except those intrinsic to "Her" being, erasing external mystical correspondences.
  • If "She" used Rebirth during the process of accommodating the Brood Hive, this would reset "Her" progress to zero.
  • "She" is capable of accelerating the reconstruction of the Mirror World to its original stability through Rebirth.
  • Every part of the Mother Goddess of Depravity was in a state of constant New Life, including "Her" soul, consciousness, spirit, and intellect. This caused "Her" to recover "Her" intellect as soon as "She" became Foolish, rendering "Her" immune to the core symbolism of the Lord of Mysteries.
  • Using New Life on others' consciousnesses:
  • The natural influence of "Her" symbolism let all the consciousnesses within Calamity of Destruction Lumian to gain New Life, including that of Cheek, Tudor, the Primordial God Almighty, the divided Mirrored Original Creator, and the Malevolent Dragon.
  • Grisha Adam was bathed in crimson moonlight, bringing New Life to all consciousnesses within "Him", including that of Herabergen, Leodero, and the Primordial God Almighty.
  • Using New Life on others' Beyonder powers:
  • The interferences from the Eternal Blazing Sun, Evernight Goddess, Earth Mother, God of Steam and Machinery, and God of Knowledge and Wisdom in Bethel Abraham's apotheosis ritual were all given New Life, no longer able to be controlled by their original owners.
  • A portion of the billions of Marionettes under the Lord of Mysteries Klein's control gained New Life and consciousness, ceasing to be mere puppets and starting to resist his control. Many Marionettes attempted to use various abilities, but the majority of these powers betrayed their original owners, instead destroying them.
  • Each strand of the Lord of Storms' lightning form was tainted scarlet, gaining New Life as they detached and scattered, killing "Him".
  • Her" corruption will still remain even after a Black Emperor dies and revives through the Astral World, accompanying "Their" resurrection with a New Life of corruption.
  • "Her" symbolic nature would continue to influence the surrounding area even when "She" hesitates to attack due to other entities' influences, such as Charm from the Calamity of Destruction.
  • The symbolism of New Life has immensely powerful healing properties, "She" can quickly heal "Herself" and other Great Old Ones' wounds.
  • Only certain symbols and concepts would weaken the effectiveness of New Life, such as the symbol of Destruction and the power of Termination.
  • New Life cannot heal "Her" from the prolonged state of weakness caused by invoking the symbolism of the Chaos Primogenitor.
  • The emergence of the Eternal Darkness allows the previously wandering Souls on Earth in the Spirit World to finally have Eternal Rest in the River of Eternal Darkness, but without the Mother Goddess of Depravity's symbolism, the reincarnation cycle will not be complete.
  • The Feminine Aspect of The Universe: "She" represents the Feminine Aspect of The Universe.
  • Even though the Demoness isn't neighboring to the Mother Goddess' Pathways, the Oldest One's Feminine Aspect and the Universe's Feminine Aspect are, to some extent, equivalent, leading to convergence without causing excessive madness compared to other pathways.
  • Beauty and Spirituality: "She" is the symbol of beauty in the universe, radiating an unmistakable maternal brilliance. Even "Her" enemies would hesitate to strike at "Her". This makes "Her" a less likely target of malice.
  • Moon: "She" is able to make a Blood Moon to suddenly descend into the Astral World, illuminating all the distorted concepts there, as well as preventing any Deity from escaping.
  • Endpoint: "She" is the final Destination of all things, the common Endpoint of all Life.
  • This authority overlaps with the authority of the Death and Darkness.
  • Spirit Body & Spirituality: "She" is the destination of Spirits and their Master.
  • This authority overlaps with the authority of the Death and Darkness.
  • Death and Desolation: "She" can influence other entities to wither, collapsing into death and returning to the embrace of Mother Earth.
  • Many of Lord of Mysteries Klein's Marionettes withered under "Her" moonlight and "Her" Banshee-like scream.
  • Under "Her" Banshee-like scream, the collapse of Calamity of Destruction Lumian's consciousness and body accelerated further from the influence of Death and Desolation.
  • Corruption: As an Above the Sequence, those without sufficient rank who discover "Her" existence will be Corrupted: manifestations of this Corruption include sprouting genitalia, staining blood-red, or swelling as fetuses prepare to tear their way out of their body.

Countering Symbolisms

Symbols of other Great Old Ones can counter some of "Her" symbols and powers, which include but are not limited to the following:

  • Calamity of Destruction:
  • Destruction counteracts New Life
  • Wounds and compound weaknesses dealt to "Her" would take longer to heal under the influence of the symbolism of Destruction.
  • Masculinity mutually counteracts with Femininity.
  • Lord of Mysteries:
  • History, Fooling, and Error can inflict severe damage to the Sovereign of the Physical World.
  • The Lord of Mysteries could use the symbolism of History to summon the History of Reality being shaken during The Apocalypse war, then employ the symbolism of Fooling to blur the lines between History and Reality, and then uses the symbolism of Error to equate the shaking of Reality with the Sovereign of the Physical World suffering severe damage, thereby inflicting heavy losses on the Mother Goddess of Depravity. However, this isn't enough to kill or Seal "Her".

Primordial Demoness

Cheek, the Primordial Demoness, is the Sequence 0 God of the Demoness Pathway. According to the Demoness Sect, the Primordial Demoness is the true inheritor of the Original Creator. "She" was born out of Chaos and was the earliest to be born from the Creator's body. "She" is also the ultimate Ender that ends everything.

The Mirror People believe that the Chaos Demoness they revere deep within the Mirror World is the true Primordial Demoness. The White Primordial Demoness Figurine depicts the Primordial Demoness, while the Black Primordial Demoness Figurine depicts "Her" mirror self.

"She" was formerly a male.

Originally, the Demoness and Red Priest would combine and became a Trinity within City of Calamity and it would result in the resurrection of Primordial God Almighty. However, because City of Calamity was sealed in the Western Continent, Primordial's will that recovered in Tudor and "Her" was unable to resonate with the Sefirah, resulting in it being easy to control and suppress. Additionally, because "Their" consummation took place in a special mirror world voluntarily created by Bethel whose intentions could have been influenced by the Celestial Worthy's will in "Him", this resulted in birth of the Mirrored Original Creator. Subsequently, Salinger's forcible accommodation of Red Priest Uniqueness caused the Mirrored Original Creator to produce certain self awareness. "She" was in a way an ideal copy of Primordial Demoness. The only difference was the colour of "Her" dress, being black instead of white, and conviction that "She" is a pure female Demoness. This is also the true identity of "Her" Mirror Self.

Mother Tree of Desire

The Mother Tree of Desire is one of the Outer Deities from the Cosmos that attempted to invade Earth. "She" is the second child of the Original Creator, and is one of "His" three direct children along with the Mother Goddess of Depravity and the Son of Chaos.

"She" was the original owner of the Abyss and Chained Pathways. "She" is also the current owner of the Patriarch Pathway.,

Authorities and Symbolisms

"She" possesses partial authority over the Abyss Pathway and Chained Pathway, but full authority over the Patriarch Pathway, and the corresponding symbols, which include but are not limited to the following:

  • Abyss: Authorities over Blood, Filth, and Corruption, and Symbol of -. Partial via the Tenebrous World.
  • Chained: Authorities over Curses, Binding, and Symbol of Binding. Partial via the Tenebrous World.
  • Patriarch: Authorities over Love, Envy, Reason, and Sentient Beings, and Symbol of Desires.
  • This Pathway is a mix of two different Pathways:
  • The Pathway of Desires (Reasons).
  • The Pathway of Blathering, the corresponding authorities are Raving, Madness and Propagation. The Sequence 0 name is Patriarch.

Derived from the above, "She" also possesses the following abilities/symbols:

  • Father of Devils (Former): "She" was the original Father of Devils before "She" lost the Tenebrous World and the two corresponding Pathways when the Original Creator awakened.
  • Because of "Her" connection, "She" held corruptive influence over "Her" former two pathways after the Astral Barrier had sufficiently weakened.
  • After The Apocalypse, "She" is re-accommodating the Tenebrous World but without the Chained Symbol2 Chained Uniqueness and Abyss Symbol2 Abyss Uniqueness and the corresponding Beyonder Characteristics.
  • Perpetual Blatherer: "She" is the initiator of Reason and endless Desires.
  • "She" can awaken the survival instincts of all sentient beings in a region, including other Great Old Ones.
  • "She" can manipulate the desires within the heart of all creatures.
  • "She" can rely on the symbolism of Desire to locate the true body of the Lord of Mysteries.
  • With "Her" birth came the rise of Life and the introduction of Evil and Desire.
  • Reason: "Her" existence blesses all Sentient Beings with Rationality.
  • Before "Her" birth, no creature had Reason, even other Great Old Ones such as the Mother Goddess of Depravity were no exception.
  • Tree Supplication: "She" is able to Supplicate powers from the Mother Goddess of Depravity.
  • However, it will depend on the mood of the Mother Goddess of Depravity whether "Her" prayer will be accepted or not.
  • Raving: "Her" authority emphasizes the effects of Ravings instead of Ravings concept itself.

Countering Symbolisms

Symbols of other Great Old Ones can counter some of "Her" symbols and powers, which include but are not limited to the following:

  • Lord of Mysteries:
  • Marionettes and Grafting counter Desires:
  • Marionettes were dead people, puppets devoid of Desires.
  • The Lord of Mysteries can Graft the surging Desires within "Their" true body onto Marionettes, then release them from manipulation, killing them.
  • High-Dimensional Overseer:
  • Dimensionality counters Desires.
  • Beings in higher dimensions or within the High-Dimensional Overseer's dimensional painting will not be fully consumed by the explosion of desires from the Father of Devils, "They" would only enter a brief state of stagnation.
