Laws of Mysticism

The principles behind supernatural forces.

The Law of Similarity and the Law of Contagion are the two laws that form a fundamental part of the mysticism system in Lord of Mysteries (opens in a new tab) and Circle of Inevitability (opens in a new tab).

The Law of Similarity states that like produces like, or that an effect resembles its cause. One can produce any effect he desires merely by imitating it.

The Law of Contagion states that things which have once been in contact with each other continue to act on each other at a distance after the physical contact has been severed. Whatever one does to a material object will affect equally the person with whom the object was once in contact, whether it formed part of his body or not.

Usages in the Mysteries series

In the Mysteries series, these two laws are behind the concepts of Mystical Similarity and Mystical Connection.

Examples

By using the authority of the  ![Error Symbol2 (opens in a new tab)](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway) Error Pathway and with a suitable medium, one can elevate the mystical connection between two separate entities that were originally one into a connection akin to that between an avatar and its true body, or oneself and oneself: allowing the damage and consequences experienced by one to be transmitted to the other.

  • Since the Genie (opens in a new tab), Uncertain Mist (opens in a new tab), the Nation of Disorder (opens in a new tab), and the  ![Black Emperor Symbol2 (opens in a new tab)](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Emperor_Pathway) Black Emperor Uniqueness (opens in a new tab) were once a single entity, by using the Black Emperor Roselle Gustav (opens in a new tab) (with corruptions from both the Uncertain Mist (opens in a new tab) and the Nation of Disorder (opens in a new tab)) as a medium, damaging the Genie (opens in a new tab) equals damaging the Uncertain Mist (opens in a new tab).
  • Since the Tenebrous World (opens in a new tab), Mother Tree of Desire (opens in a new tab), and the  ![Chained Symbol2 (opens in a new tab)](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway) Chained Uniqueness (opens in a new tab) were once a single entity, by using the  ![Chained Symbol2 (opens in a new tab)](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway) Chained Uniqueness (opens in a new tab) (which was deeply corrupted by the Mother Tree of Desire (opens in a new tab)) as a medium, damaging the Tenebrous World (opens in a new tab) equals damaging the Mother Tree of Desire (opens in a new tab).

When certain events happen that strengthen the corresponding Mystical Similarity and the corresponding Symbols (opens in a new tab) between two parties, circumstances happening to one will be reflected on the other. As a result, one can deliberately arrange for events to happen in certain way to achieve their desired objective.

  • The Mother Goddess of Depravity (opens in a new tab) arranged for Lumian Lee (opens in a new tab) to gain the symbolism of the Mother, by orchestrating certain events so that. By having the symbol of Calamity (Fourth Epoch Trier (opens in a new tab)) come into contact with the Mother (Lumian), this led to the birth of Omebella (opens in a new tab).
  • Lilith (opens in a new tab), who had once stolen the identity and fate of Omebella (opens in a new tab), couldn't bear this when Omebella (opens in a new tab) was reborn in this way, forcing "Her" to enter slumber.
  • Through Omebella (opens in a new tab)'s birth, the symbol of the Father was strengthened. Since the Primordial God Almighty (opens in a new tab) was the father of the original Omebella (opens in a new tab), and the Eternal Blazing Sun (opens in a new tab) was the mystical father of the current Omebella (opens in a new tab) and was also trying to gain the title Father of All Life in mysticism, the consciousness of the Primordial God Almighty (opens in a new tab) awakened in the Eternal Blazing Sun (opens in a new tab).
  • Through Omebella (opens in a new tab)'s birth, the symbol of the Mother was strengthened. Since the Brood Hive (opens in a new tab) was the mother of the original Omebella (opens in a new tab), and because the Brood Hive (opens in a new tab) was once a part of the Mother Goddess of Depravity (opens in a new tab) and can almost considered to be the same being in mysticism, the Mother Goddess of Depravity (opens in a new tab) was able to use this strengthened symbolism to bypass the Astral Barrier (opens in a new tab).
  • Lumian, who held the symbolism of the Mother, strengthened this symbolism by various arrangements (such as making use of the Gift of the Land (opens in a new tab), Zedus (opens in a new tab), Gregrace (opens in a new tab)'s egg, and Lugano Toscano (opens in a new tab)). By becoming a Great Existence (opens in a new tab) himself, the damage sustained by him was also transferred to the Mother Goddess of Depravity (opens in a new tab) through the mystical Law of Similarity.
  • If he collapsed and potentially led to the birth of the Mirrored Original Creator (opens in a new tab), this might also reflect on the Mother Goddess of Depravity (opens in a new tab) in the form of the birth of the Chaos Primogenitor (opens in a new tab).

Some other examples include:

  • In the mystical world, there are some underlying rules that can be exploited, many ways to defeat powerful existences by harming the weak, which is the basis of many curses.
  • The Demoness mirror curse is a utilization of the Law of Similarity and the Law of Contagion to achieve its magical effects.
  • During The Fool's Gambit (opens in a new tab), Amon (opens in a new tab) tried to disguise himself as the owner of the Sefirah Castle (opens in a new tab) using the Error authority to utilize its power, which can also be considered an application of the Law of Similarity.
  • The Hostel (opens in a new tab) is a ritual to create "A world in a painting". When the abnormality truly occurs, such a painting world will temporarily replace some streets in the real market district. In mysticism, this represents the application of the Law of Similarity. When the similarity reaches a certain level, acts on the counterfeits can be reflected in reality.
  • The Special Mirror World (opens in a new tab) was jointly created by the True Gods (opens in a new tab) of the  ![Red Priest Symbol2 (opens in a new tab)](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Priest_Pathway) Hunter Pathway and the  ![Demoness Symbol2 (opens in a new tab)](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway) Demoness Pathway. Lumian (opens in a new tab)'s corpse wax candle was crafted from the corpse wax of demigods from both pathways, mixed with other substances. According to the Law of Similarity in mysticism, there exists a connection between the two.

Law of Beyonder Characteristics Indestructibility

A Beyonder Characteristic can never be destroyed or reduced. It cannot be erased from existence: only passed from one bearer to another. Even when a sealed artifact or Beyonder body is destroyed or purified, the characteristic itself will resurface.

Law of Similar Sequence Beyonder Characteristics Conservation

Every pathway group contains a fixed quantity of characteristics. That number never increases or decreases: it simply shifts depending on where it resides in the sequence chain.

  • If more Beyonders exist at higher sequences, there will be fewer at the lower sequences, and vice versa.
  • A single pathway may only have one Sequence 0, or up to three Sequence 1s, but never both.
  • The higher one climbs, the greater the tension and competition between Beyonders of the same path. This principle also explains the inheritance of characteristics and why conflicts naturally arise as individuals approach the peak.

Law of Pathway Exchange

Beyonders gain the ability to switch pathways to a similar one within the same group starting at Sequence 4. Some rare groups have stricter conditions, only allowing this exchange from Sequence 3 onward. If a Beyonder dies after switching pathways, the digested characteristics of both the former and current path will be released: even if the two pathways are not adjacent.

Law of Beyonder Characteristics Convergence

“Whatever separates will definitely converge, and whatever converges will definitely separate.”: A universal law describing the natural pull between characteristics of the same group or pathway. Powerful (especially High-Sequence) characteristics will gradually and unconsciously attract lower ones to them.

This pull influences not only mystical forces but also fate itself: drawing people, objects, and events together without their awareness. • Low and Mid-Sequence Beyonders often feel compelled or coincidentally pulled toward such items or individuals.

  • These effects may seem like chance or instinct: a sudden decision, a strange urge to visit a place: but stem from this deeper convergence.
