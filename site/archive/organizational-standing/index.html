Organizational Standing

Your character’s relationship with an organisation.

Organizational Standing

Organizational Standing, also known simply as Standing, represents an organization's confidence, trust, and investment in an OC.

Unlike £ Pounds, Standing is not a currency. It cannot be spent, traded, gifted, or exchanged. Instead, it functions similarly to a permanent service record: as an OC proves themselves useful to their organization, their Standing increases and additional resources, privileges, and advancement opportunities become available to them.

Reaching a Standing requirement unlocks something. It does not consume the Standing required to unlock it.

If an OC has 9 Standing, and an item requires 5 Standing, purchasing that item does not reduce the OC to 4 Standing. They continue to possess 9 Standing.

Standing is always associated with a particular organization. Having 10 Standing with one organization does not grant the same trust or privileges within another.

Standing Unlocks

Organizational Standing has three primary purposes; Organization Shops, Advancement Sponsorship, and Organizational Privileges.

Organization Shops

Certain supplies within an organization's custom shop require a minimum amount of Standing before they can be accessed.

For example, an organization may restrict specialized ammunition, mysticism materials, charms, ritual supplies, or other sensitive equipment to members who have achieved a certain amount of Standing. Unless otherwise stated, the item itself is still purchased using £ Pounds.

Advancement Sponsorship

Members who have shown adequate service can get advancement resources from organisations. Instead of an affiliated OC simply buying their next potion from their organization, the organization helps with their advancement once they have accumulated enough Standing.

Organizational Privileges

Standing may also be used as a requirement for especially sensitive organizational privileges. These can include but are not limited to:

  • Unique Missions
  • Classified Intelligence
  • Access to certain NPCs

-# Not every organization will provide identical benefits.

Earning Organizational Standing

Standing is awarded when an OC performs something their organization genuinely values. Simply participating in Roleplay does not grant Standing. Much like Resonance Records, gaining Organizational Standing is also done in 3 different categories:

Losing Standing

Standing represents organizational confidence as well as accumulated service. It is therefore possible for Standing to be reduced or suspended when an OC seriously acts against their organization's interests.

Purchasing an item or receiving an Advancement Potion never removes Standing. If an OC falls below an access threshold, privileges requiring that threshold may become unavailable again.

Multiple Organizations

If an OC is legitimately permitted to hold membership within more than one organization, each organization has a separate Standing record. However, this is only the case for some OCs.

For example, someone in both the Einhorn Family and Moses Ascetic Order wouldn't be able to gain Standing for both organizations. However, someone who is from the Augustus Family and also in the Church of the Lord of Storms would be able to. Each Organization also has their own standing.

Organization A: 9 Standing
Organization B: 2 Standing

Service to Organization A does not increase Organization B's Standing.

Standing Tracking

Organizational Standing is tracked through WabbitBot's secondary currency. Staff will update the OC's Standing after an RP has been conducted that can result in the OC gaining Standing. For those who are eligible for gaining Standing for multiple organization, their secondary Organization Standing must be tracked within their corresponding https://discord.com/channels/1515611113042087958/1518478388807864512.
