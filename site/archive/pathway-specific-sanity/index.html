Pathway Specific Sanity

Sanity effects and exceptions for individual pathways.

FOOL

  • Worms of Spirit: Spreading their Worms of Spirit will cause Instability for the Fool Beyonder. For every Worm spread from their main body after the fifth, they gain 3 Instability.
  • Retracting a spread Worm of Spirit recovers the Instability gained from spreading it.
  • If one of these Worms of Spirit dies, the Fool loses 2 SV for each dead Worm. This does not include additional Instability.
  • At Sequence 3, each Worm of Spirit death instead causes -0.15 SV.
  • At Sequence 2, each Worm of Spirit death instead causes -0.02 SV.
  • Historical Void: If a Scholar of Yore hides within the Historical Void of a period they are not fully familiar with, they lose 2 SV and gain 3 Instability for every turn spent there.

ERROR

  • Thought Theft: Stealing the current thoughts of Beyonders from corrupt Pathways can cause 1d3 Instability.
  • The corrupt Pathways are Abyss, Chained, and Hanged Man.
  • Memory Theft: Stealing a large number of memories, or particularly corrupted memories, from another person causes 1d3 Instability.
  • Identity Theft: If an Error Beyonder completely steals another OC’s identity and successfully convinces that OC’s closest relationships, the victim loses 1d6+9 SV and gains 20 Instability.
  • Worms of Time: As a Lord of Mysteries Pathway, the same punishment for losing Worms applies. For every Worm of Time spread from their main body after the fifth, the Error Beyonder gains 3 Instability.
  • Retracting a spread Worm of Time recovers the Instability gained from spreading it.
  • If one of these Worms of Time dies, the Error Beyonder loses 2 SV for each dead Worm. This does not include additional Instability.
  • At Sequence 3, each Worm of Time death instead causes -0.15 SV.
  • At Sequence 2, each Worm of Time death instead causes -0.02 SV.

DOOR

  • Scribe: Copied abilities retain the same restrictions imposed upon the original Pathway ability.
  • For example, an ability that cannot treat the same person twice within one week retains that restriction when copied.
  • Worms of Star: As a Lord of Mysteries Pathway, the same punishment for losing Worms applies. For every Worm of Star spread from their main body after the fifth, the Door Beyonder gains 3 Instability.
  • Retracting a spread Worm of Star recovers the Instability gained from spreading it.
  • If one of these Worms of Star dies, the Door Beyonder loses 2 SV for each dead Worm. This does not include additional Instability.
  • At Sequence 3, each Worm of Star death instead causes -0.15 SV.
  • At Sequence 2, each Worm of Star death instead causes -0.02 SV.

VISIONARY

  • Therapy: An OC may receive Therapy from a Visionary, restoring 1d4+1 SV.
  • Much like Cogitation, this is limited to once per week per OC.
  • Placate: Restores 1d6+4 Instability.
  • This may be used on an OC once per week.
  • At Sequence 5, the amount healed is doubled when used on an OC whose current SV is below 40.
  • Awe: Causes the next Sanity-reducing or Instability-inducing attack against the target to become 1.5× more effective.
  • For example, an attack that would normally inflict 10 Instability instead inflicts 15 Instability.
  • Mind Deprivation: Inflicts 1d5 Instability and causes the next Instability-inducing or SV-reducing ability used against the target to become 2× as effective.
  • Psychological Cue / Hypnosis: May either restore or inflict 1d2+3 Instability.
  • The healing application may be used on an OC once per week.
  • Frenzy: Inflicts 1d6+4 Instability alongside 1d3 SV Damage.
  • Mind Dragon Breath becomes an AoE attack and gains +1 to both its Instability and SV Damage rolls.
  • Alteration: Its total effect is fixed, but the period over which the effect is spread determines whether the Dreamwalker remains unnoticed.
  • Alteration can cause a maximum of 1d1+2 SV Damage.
  • To remain undetected, the alteration must be performed gradually over the course of one full week.
  • Mental Plague: Causes the victim to suffer 1d3+2 Instability on three separate occasions, for a total of 3d3+6 Instability.
  • Any other OC who contacts the victim’s Spirit Body will also be infected, as the plague spreads through Spirit Body Threads.
  • Mind Storm: Its effect depends upon how corrupted the Manipulator currently is.
  • If the Manipulator’s Sanity Value is Fractured, Half-Mad, or Critical, Mind Storm inflicts 1d6+6 Instability alongside 1d3+2 SV Damage.
  • If the Manipulator’s Sanity Value is Unstable or Restrained, Mind Storm inflicts 1d6+7 Instability.
  • If the Manipulator’s Sanity Value is Stable or higher, Mind Storm inflicts 1d5+5 Instability.
  • If the Manipulator has been corrupted by the Abyss, Hanged Man, or another corruptive influence, the effect will be determined on a case-by-case basis.
  • Dream Weaving: Every attack suffered within the Dream is transferred into lost SV, with each attack inflicting 1d3 SV Damage.
  • Sanity-reducing and Instability-inducing attacks are transferred at their normal values.
  • Plague Storm: An instantaneous combination of Mental Plague and Mind Deprivation.
  • Inflicts 1d11+19 Instability alongside a flat -5 SV.
  • Sequence Advantage: For every Sequence the Visionary is above their target, their applicable rolls receive +1.

SUN

  • Singing: Restores 1d3+2 Instability.
  • This may be used once per week per OC.
  • Blessing: Reduces the effect of the next Instability-inducing ability used against the target by half.
  • Horror Immunity: When activated on the turn immediately following an OC suffering SV Loss or gaining Instability, the amount suffered is reduced by half.
  • Notarization: Does not mechanically alter the SV or Instability inflicted or healed by an ability.
  • Its effect is instead increased narratively within the RP.
  • Purification: Purifies mental madness and restores 1d9+6 Instability.
  • Purification may cause the purified target to become more extreme in their existing beliefs, emotions, or behaviour.

TYRANT

  • Wrath: Activating Wrath causes the Folk of Rage to gain 1 Instability.
  • Singing: May restore or inflict 1d3+2 Instability, depending upon whether it is used supportively or offensively.
  • The restorative application may be used once per week per OC.
  • At Sequence 4, the roll becomes 1d4+3.

WHITE TOWER

  • Imitation: Retains its effectiveness percentage when healing or damaging SV and Instability.
  • For example, if a Polymath has imitated Placate at 50% effectiveness, they roll the normal 1d6+4 amount and reduce the final result to 50%.
  • Any decimal result is rounded up.

HANGED MAN

  • Forbidden Knowledge: Having received knowledge concerning secret entities with sinister names, all Hanged Man Beyonders possess a maximum Sanity Cap of 95.
  • They can therefore never attain the Superior Clarity state.
  • Secrets Suppliant: Gains 1d3+2 Instability every week.
  • Passive Listening: After becoming a Listener, the Beyonder must experience Passive Listening once per RP, excluding solo RPs.
  • Roll 1d15.
  • The result becomes the amount of Instability gained.
  • Active Listening: Requires three separate rolls.
  • First, roll 1d3 to determine the Listening modifier.
  • Then, roll 1d20 to determine the base Instability gained.
  • On a result of 1 from the modifier roll, multiply the 1d20 result by 1.5, rounding up.
  • On a result of 2, the 1d20 result remains unchanged.
  • On a result of 3, divide the 1d20 result by 1.5, rounding up.
  • Finally, roll 1d100 to determine the quality of the information received through Listening.
  • Shadow Ascetic: No longer receives the weekly 1d3+2 Instability.
  • Because their attacks invoke Degeneration, each successful Shadow attack inflicts 1d2 Instability.
  • This may trigger from a maximum of three hits per post.
  • Further hits still retain their narrative and mechanical effects, but do not inflict additional Instability during that post.
  • Rose Bishop: Must consume at least the equivalent of one adult human’s worth of flesh per week.
  • Failure to do so reduces the effectiveness of their Flesh and Blood Magic by 50%.
  • This includes slower transfiguration, weaker explosions, and similar reductions.
  • They also gain 1d6+4 Instability at the beginning of the week.
  • Shepherd: Is no longer restricted by the Rose Bishop’s weekly flesh-consumption requirement.
  • Grazing: Every soul Grazed by a Shepherd reduces their Sanity Cap.
  • Every Low or Mid Sequence Graze reduces the Sanity Cap by 2.
  • Every Saint-level Graze reduces the Sanity Cap by 10.
  • After becoming a Black Knight, Low and Mid Sequence Grazes reduce the Sanity Cap by 1, while Saint-level Grazes reduce it by 3.
  • After becoming a Trinity Templar, only Saint-level Grazes reduce the Sanity Cap, at 1 per Saint-level Graze.
  • Black Greatsword: As its power originates from the Degeneration domain, every successful hit from a Black Knight’s Black Greatsword inflicts 1d3 Instability.
  • Unlike a Shadow Ascetic, a Black Knight may trigger this effect from a maximum of five hits per post.

DARKNESS

  • Fear Poem: A Midnight Poet’s Fear Poem inflicts 1d3+2 Instability.
  • Tranquilize Poem: A Midnight Poet’s Tranquilize Poem restores 1d3 Instability to an ally.
  • Agitate Poem: A Soul Assurer’s Agitate Poem inflicts 1d3+3 Instability.
  • Dream Shaping: A Nightmare’s Dream Shaping inflicts 1d5 Instability.
  • Nightmare World: While within a Nightmare World:
  • All SV Damage values are reduced by 1.
  • All Instability-inducing values are reduced by 2.
  • Horror Aura: Anyone who looks upon a Horror Bishop for the first time loses 1d3 SV and gains 1d3+2 Instability.
  • This aura cannot be deactivated until the Horror Bishop’s potion is at least halfway digested.
  • It also affects allies who are unable to resist it.

TWILIGHT GIANT

  • Supernatural Resistance: Grants 10% resistance when converting Instability into SV Loss.
  • The result is rounded up.
  • For example, instead of losing 10 SV from 10 Instability, the Twilight Giant loses 9 SV.

DEMONESS

  • Instigation: Inflicts 1d2 Instability upon the target.
  • Charm: If a Charmed target attacks the Demoness who Charmed them, they gain 1d2 Instability per turn for every turn during which they continue attacking the Demoness.
  • This effect is halved when caused by Passive Charm.

RED PRIEST

  • Provocation: Inflicts 1d5 Instability upon the target.
  • Courage: Grants 25% resistance when converting Instability into SV Loss.
  • The result is rounded up.
  • For example, instead of losing 10 SV from 10 Instability, the Red Priest loses 8 SV.

HERMIT

KNOWLEDGE PURSUIT

  • Whenever a Hermit Beyonder advances or actively pursues dangerous information, roll:
  • 1d20: The amount of SV lost.
  • 1d10: The quality and relevance of the information received.
  • Custom Spells and Scrolls: Any custom spells or scrolls are reviewed on a case-by-case basis through Ability Submission.
  • Eyes of Mystery Prying: Its SV and Instability effects are determined on a case-by-case basis.
  • The Hermit may accidentally pry into something above their level, potentially causing severe Instability or even direct SV Loss.
  • Advancement: Hermits experience Knowledge Pursuit during every advancement.
  • GM Activation: Knowledge Pursuit may also be triggered unexpectedly during missions at the GM’s discretion.

PARAGON

  • Knowledge Spirit: When used correctly, a Knowledge Spirit may corrupt or reduce a target’s intelligence.
  • On a successful application, the target suffers 1d2+1 SV Loss and gains 1d3+2 Instability.

WHEEL OF FORTUNE

  • Monster Instability: A Monster gains 1d3 Instability once per month, on the 15th day of each month.
  • Monster Sanity Cap: A Monster possesses a maximum Sanity Cap of 95.
  • This restriction applies only while the Beyonder remains at the Monster Sequence.
  • Psyche Storm: Inflicts 1d3+2 Instability alongside 1d2 SV Damage.

MOTHER

  • Therapy: An OC may receive Therapy from a Mother, restoring 1d4+1 SV.
  • Much like Cogitation, this is limited to once per week per OC.
  • Soul Suture: Cannot be used during combat.
  • Restores up to 1d9+6 Instability.
  • Its restorative application may be used once per week per OC.
  • When used offensively, Soul Suture may inflict up to 100 Instability.
  • Instead of healing Instability, it may temporarily increase an OC’s Sanity Cap by 5, up to a maximum of 100.
  • The Sanity Cap increase lasts for 3 days.
  • This application has a cooldown of one week per OC.
  • Mutation: Inflicts 1d3+2 Instability.
  • If Mutation is successfully used on the same target three times, that target suffers -10 Sanity Cap.

MOON

  • Potions and Perfumes: Only a Potions Professor may create Potions or Perfumes that increase SV or reduce Instability.
  • High Summoner: Summoned creatures may possess abilities that affect SV or Instability.
  • These abilities must be reviewed on a case-by-case basis through Ability Submission.

ABYSS

  • Devil Transformation: After becoming a Devil, Criminal Pathway Beyonders undergo a fundamental transformation.
  • Their maximum Sanity Cap is permanently reduced to 90.
  • Treatments or abilities that would ordinarily increase their Sanity Cap cannot raise it above this value.
  • Corruption Resistance: Devils can resist status effects that increase the amount of Instability or SV Damage they receive, such as Awe or Magnify.
  • This applies only when both participants are of equal Sequence, or when the Abyss Beyonder is of a higher Sequence.
  • Desire Catalysation: When the amount of catalysed Desire exceeds the maximum volume the target’s body can withstand, the target gains 1d9+6 Instability and suffers 1d5 SV Damage.
  • If the amount of catalysed Desire does not exceed what the target’s body can withstand, the target instead gains 1d4+3 Instability.
  • Each of these effects may trigger only once per OC per RP.
  • Desire Manipulation: At Sequence 4, Desire Manipulation undergoes a significant transformation.
  • If the effect lands successfully, the target gains 75 Instability.
  • Blatherer’s Corruption: A Blatherer constantly exudes an aura of corruption.
  • Those within close proximity to the Blatherer gain 5 Instability per turn.
  • Every word spoken aloud by a Blatherer also possesses a degree of corruption.
  • Spoken words can inflict up to 7 Instability across every 3 turns.

CHAINED

-# **Curse of the Full Moon:** Occurs twice per month, on the 14th and 28th.

  • Lunatic: Due to their curse, a Lunatic possesses a maximum Sanity Cap of 85.
  • Berserk: Activating Berserk causes the Lunatic to gain 10 Instability in exchange for enhancing their strength and all other physical aspects.
  • Werewolf: Upon becoming a Werewolf, their maximum Sanity Cap increases to 90.
  • Dark Horror: Inflicts 1d3 Instability.
  • Werewolf: Full Moon: During the Full Moon, a Werewolf must either surrender to their instincts and kill to calm their bloodlust, or suppress their instincts.
  • Suppressing their instincts causes them to gain 15 Instability.
  • Killing to satisfy their bloodlust instead causes them to lose 3 SV.
  • Zombie: Upon becoming a Zombie, their maximum Sanity Cap increases to 95.
  • Zombie: Full Moon: Zombies also bear the Curse of the Full Moon.
  • Suppressing their instincts causes them to gain 15 Instability.
  • Killing to satisfy their instincts instead causes them to lose 3 SV.
  • Wraith: Full Moon: Wraiths also suffer from the Curse of the Full Moon.
  • Suppressing their instincts causes them to gain 15 Instability.
  • Consuming human souls instead causes them to lose 3 SV.
  • Puppet: Upon becoming a Puppet, their maximum Sanity Cap increases to 100.

BLACK EMPEROR

  • Corrosion: Inflicts 3 Instability per turn.
  • Magnify: May double the Instability inflicted by an ability and multiply its SV Damage by 1.5.
  • The modified SV Damage is rounded down.
  • This offensive application may be used a total of three times per week.
  • Once per week, Magnify may instead double the amount of Instability healed by an ability.

JUSTICIAR

  • Area of Jurisdiction: While within their Area of Jurisdiction, a Justiciar possesses 15% resistance when converting Instability into SV Loss.
  • The result is rounded up.
  • For example, instead of losing 10 SV from 10 Instability, the Justiciar loses 9 SV.
  • Illusory Torture Devices: Prolonged use outside of combat causes the target to suffer a flat -10 SV.
  • Punishments: May inflict 1d5 Instability.
  • This may occur once per RP.
  • Imperatives: May increase the effectiveness of Instability-inducing, SV-damaging, or restorative abilities by 1.5×.
