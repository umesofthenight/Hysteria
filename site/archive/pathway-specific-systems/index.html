Pathway Specific Systems

Additional mechanics for individual pathways.

Shadow Ascetic

The Shadow Summoning system requires rolls for Strength and Loyalty.

  • Strength: 1d10
  • 1-6: A shadow at the strength of one sequence below the summoner.
  • 7-9: A shadow at the strength of the same sequence as the summoner.
  • 10: A shadow at the strength of one sequence above the summoner.
  • Loyalty: 1d10
  • 1: A Shadow with this level of Loyalty will attack its summoner.
  • 2-5: The bare-minimum. While it will listen to the summoner, it won't tire itself or risk its death.
  • 6-9: Rather Loyal Shadow that will only reject orders that will result in its death 100%.
  • 10: A Shadow willing to die for its summoner.

Shadows can only be summoned in RPs where multiple OCs are in, and once the RP ends, they will disappear. Barriers such as Saint/Angel cannot be crossed over by [Sequence 5]s and [Sequence 3]s etc. In such a case i.e. a [Sequence 5] rolling 10, the strength of the shadow will be similar to a fully stacked + prepped [Sequence 5].

Warlock

At Sequence 7 and onwards, Hermits are able to create their own spells. To be able to create spells, they must have knowledge on that domain of study. To gain this knowledge, there are two ways:

  • Either do a Knowledge Pursuit, praying to the [Hidden Sage] to bless you with information on a certain domain of knowledge, or have this information received through the Knowledge Pursuit done during a Hermit OCs Advancement.
  • Within certain missions, rewards could include books or information that grant the OC a spell or knowledge of a certain small domain.

Polymath

For a Polymath or above to imitate an ability, 3 steps are required.

  1. Observation of the Ability

The Polymath (or above) must witness the ability being meaningfully demonstrated. The demonstration must show enough of the ability to establish:

  • How it is activated.
  • What it targets.
  • Its primary effect.
  • Its visible or spiritually perceptible result.

Observation may occur through:

  • Cooperative training.
  • Casual RPs.
  • The examination of a sufficiently detailed mystical recording.
  1. Comprehension of the Ability

This may be accomplished through any one of the following: [▢] The demonstrator explains the ability. [▢] The ability is observed under different circumstances. [▢] The OC researches the relevant Pathway. [▢] The OC acquires notes, records, or instructional material. [▢] An appropriate teacher assists with the analysis. [▢] The OC examines materials or traces left by the ability. Comprehension does not require a technical essay. Examples include:

  • "The ability converts spirituality into heat and explosive force."
  • "The user establishes a spiritual connection with the target’s dream."
  • "The power interferes with the target’s Body of Heart and Mind."
  • "The ability uses shadows as a medium for concealment and movement."
  1. Reconstruction of the Ability

The OC attempts to reproduce the ability through their own spirituality and knowledge. The reconstructed version must differ meaningfully from the original, as it isn’t a perfect copy.

This can be reduced range, reduced strength, shorter duration, lower precision, longer activation time, greater spirituality consumption, the need for gestures, spoken words, or a material medium, fewer applications than the original ability, or even a different appearance or method of manifestation. ~~ ~~ After these 3 steps are done, a staff member will approve the Imitation, giving it one of the 3 different grades: Crude(20%), Functional(35%), and Refined(50%), at the level of a Polymath.

In the Comprehension stage, if the OC fulfills only 2, then their Imitation will be Crude. If they fulfill 3-4, then it will be a Functional imitation. If they fulfilled 5-6, then it will be a Refined imitation.

Wheel of Fortune

At Sequence 7 and onwards, Beyonders of the Wheel of Fortune Pathway gain abilities related to Luck. Here are the systems for them to apply this luck through their variety of abilities:

Sequence 7: Lucky One

Passive Luck: This is an ability that simply let's a Lucky One, as the name suggests, be lucky.

  • To accomplish a similar effect, where they do feel luckier, but aren't able to heavily rely on it, whenever asked to roll a dice, instead of rolling 1d100, they will roll 2d100, picking the result with the higher roll.
  • If the roll required was 2d25, they would instead roll 4d25. If it was 1d16+4, then they would roll 2d16+4.

Sequence 6: Calamity Priest

Calamity Attraction: This enables a Calamity Priest to attract Calamities, both Passively and Actively.

Sequence 5: Winner

Curse of Misfortune: This ability lasts for 3 turns. For the whole duration of these 3 turns, whenever the target rolls a dice, they will roll twice, similar to the Lucky One. However, instead of taking the higher roll, they will take the lower roll.

Passive Luck: As their ability is further strengthened at this level, instead of rolling 2 of the same dice, they will roll 3 of the same dice. At this level, they also have the ability to pick and choose which of the rolls they want.

  • If they choose to keep the lowest roll out of the 3 rolls, they will gain Luck. This must be kept track of within the #𝖱oleplay・𝖳racker. For each Luck they have, they can reroll again. A maximum of 5 Rerolls can be done per turn. Once rerolled, old rolls cannot be picked.

Unique Spirits

Certain Pathway, such as Death , Hermit , and Darkness , gain the ability to summon and familiarize spirits. These may be simple messenger spirits, or unique combat spirits. For most, these will be very hard to come by, as even for Death Pathway Beyonders, they sometimes require rare materials to communicate with spirits safely.

From certain missions, information networks, and also by following a lead, Beyonders of most pathways can come across information regarding unique Spirits. These would be crucial for the forementioned Pathways, as they could take said Unique Spirits for themselves. Other than this, Organizations in control of the High Sequences of aforementioned Pathways can offer leads or direction location on such Unique Spirits.

As for Contracted Messengers, all pathways can summon them once they have enough Spirituality. For balancing sake, all Pathways can summon a Contracted Messenger utilizing an incantation to summon them at Sequence 7, with the sole exception of the Red Priest Pathway, which can do so at Sequence 6.

Utilizing a rather unorthodox incantation to summon Messengers can result in very unexpected scenarios, so proceed carefully.
