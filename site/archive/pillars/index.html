The foundational authorities of the universe.

Pillars' Symbolism

The Three Realms

From a mystical point of view, this world is divided into three levels: the Physical World, the Spirit World, and the Astral World. The rest are formed by attaching to one of these three, like the Underworld to the Spirit World. Unless entirely and specifically isolated, every place is connected to the Spirit World and the Astral World. This is one of the symbolic meanings of a Pillars.

Compared to other Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab), the Pillars, also known as Pillars of Origin, possess special symbolism. "They" are the foundation for the universe's maintenance and development, each supporting one of the three layer of the world. Each controls a realm, but not completely. "Their" authorities overlap with each other.

  • God Almighty (opens in a new tab), Lord of the Astral World
  • Lord of Mysteries (opens in a new tab), Dominator of the Spirit World
  • Mother Goddess of Depravity (opens in a new tab), Sovereign of the Physical World

The three Pillars differ significantly in how "They" wield "Their" respective symbols and how "They" can utilize "Their" power:

  • God Almighty (opens in a new tab):
  • "They" can disrupt the Astral World by causing abstract concepts and symbolic markers there to descend into chaos. This can delay or disorient other Great Old Dominators (opens in a new tab) and prevent "Them" from using the Astral World to travel elsewhere.
  • "They" can use "Their" influence over Astral World concepts to temporarily interfere with, distort, suppress, or partially seal the symbols and authorities of other Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab).
  • Lord of Mysteries (opens in a new tab):
  • "They" can seal the Spirit World. For a set period, no creatures, including other Great Old Dominators (opens in a new tab), could enter the Spirit World without "Their" explicit permission. All their abilities, authorities, and symbols related to the Spirit World were rendered ineffective.
  • As the Spirit World's Dominator, if information is not hidden, "They" can theoretically see and know every piece of information within the Spirit World.
  • "They" can utilize the power within the Spirit World to make "Their" subordinates exceed their sequence, this power comes in the form of Boons (opens in a new tab), with the upper limit attainable being the level of a King of Angels (opens in a new tab).
  • Mother Goddess of Depravity (opens in a new tab):
  • "She" could not seal off Reality to prevent unauthorized entry or expel all adversaries. Such actions were unachievable. "She" could only impose a continuous weakening effect on targets within Reality.
  • Reality is the foundation of the universe. Unless everything ended, Reality would eternally exist. Thus, the Mother Goddess of Depravity (opens in a new tab) possessed the characteristic of Indestructibility, never to perish. As long as "She" wished, "She" could not be displaced or sealed.

If there's no restriction, the three Pillars can traverse through each other's domain freely.

Note: If the universe is destroyed and the Original Creator (opens in a new tab) revives to recreate the world, whether the three Pillars will change or not depends on the Original Creator (opens in a new tab). This is because the Original Creator (opens in a new tab) has the ability to change things, and the Astral World, Spirit World, and Reality (opens in a new tab) are not necessarily divided or exist in this way by default.

Fourth Pillar

Besides the Astral World's Lord (opens in a new tab), the Spirit World's Dominator (opens in a new tab), and Reality (opens in a new tab)'s Sovereign (opens in a new tab), there is another important symbolism - one that represents the endpoint of destiny, the universe's destruction, and the finale of all things.

Fate

Although Fate (opens in a new tab) was mixed with Reality (opens in a new tab), the Spirit World, and the Astral World at the beginning of creation, the reason Fate (opens in a new tab) has no Pillar (opens in a new tab) is that its essence is complete chaos, making it impossible for a ruler to exist above it.

The Controller/Wielder of Fate can be formed by either:

  • Key of Light (opens in a new tab) + any two Fate (opens in a new tab) Sefirot (such as the Circle of Inevitability (opens in a new tab) and the Goddess of Fate (opens in a new tab)).
  • Key of Light (opens in a new tab) + Sefirah Castle (opens in a new tab).

However, because this would exceed the Sefirot Accommodation Limit, "They" would be unable to control the Convergence instinct, moving closer to the Oldest One (opens in a new tab).

Power

Complete Pillars hold powers and statuses higher than other Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab), but are not fundamentally different. By relying on the unique symbolism of a Pillar, "They" all can contend with two other Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab) with symbolisms countering "Their" own.

  • Lord of Mysteries (opens in a new tab):
  • Against Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab) with specific symbols capable of countering the "Their" abilities to manifest in countless forms and fight against multiple enemies at once, "They" could only rely on the unique symbolism of a Pillar to contend with two at most.
  • In the absence of counter abilities and symbolisms, there is no fundamental difference between battling two or eight Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab). There is no limit to the number of Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab) "They" can fight.
  • God Almighty (opens in a new tab):
  • The symbolism of The Omnipotent and Omniscient makes it very hard for "Them" to be truly countered or suppressed. For symbolisms of other Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab) that counter "Them" or "Their" allies, "They" can temporarily reduce the degree of countering.
  • "They" can set global parameters to influence other Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab)' performance, weakening "Them".
  • Mother Goddess of Depravity (opens in a new tab):
  • As a support, "She" is invincible. "She" can also hold off two or three Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab) by "Herself".
  • However, even if 100 Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab) besiege "Her", as long as there is no Fourth Pillar (opens in a new tab) or something similar, "She" can only be severely injured at most, and will still be immortal and indestructible.

A normal Great Old One (opens in a new tab) can have "Their" will shattered, like how the Sefirot had "Their" wills shattered by the Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth for Blessings (opens in a new tab) and the Primordial God Almighty (opens in a new tab) in the First Epoch (opens in a new tab). However, unlike a normal Great Old One (opens in a new tab), the will of a Pillar (opens in a new tab) cannot be shattered, even by another Pillar (opens in a new tab).

Current Status

  • The God Almighty (opens in a new tab) position is unoccupied. The Primordial God Almighty (opens in a new tab) almost revived during The Apocalypse (opens in a new tab), but was ultimately unsuccessful. Without hundreds or thousands of years to wear down "His" strengthened consciousnesses, none of the God Almighty (opens in a new tab)'s five pathways are suitable for birthing a corresponding Sequence 0 (opens in a new tab).
  • The Lord of Mysteries (opens in a new tab) position is occupied by Klein Moretti (opens in a new tab) who ascended using the Potion System and merged with the Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth for Blessings (opens in a new tab).
  • The Mother Goddess of Depravity (opens in a new tab) was once a Pillar, but when the Original Creator (opens in a new tab) split and created an embodiment of convergence and separation, part of "Her" Sefirah (opens in a new tab) was torn from "Her" and became the Brood Hive (opens in a new tab), which was attracted to Earth along with the Mother Pathway and Moon Pathway. Then the Mother Goddess of Depravity (opens in a new tab) became not much different from the other Outer Deities (opens in a new tab), but "She" still possesses the symbolism and status of a Pillar due to the unique symbolism as the Nurturer of the Chaos Primogenitor.
  • After The Apocalypse (opens in a new tab), "She" managed to accommodate the Brood Hive (opens in a new tab) and 2 Sequence 1 Moon Characteristics. However, "She" did not retrieve the Moon Uniqueness (opens in a new tab), Mother Uniqueness (opens in a new tab), or any Sequence 1 Mother Characteristic.
