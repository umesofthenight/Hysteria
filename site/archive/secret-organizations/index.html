Secret Organizations

Hidden societies and their place in the world.

Twilight Hermit Order

The Twilight Hermit Order is an ancient organization founded by Adam (opens in a new tab), the eldest son of the Ancient Sun God (opens in a new tab). They aim to guide history from behind the shadows, shaping events so that the Original Creator (Ancient Sun God) can resurrect in the future.

History

Roselle Gustav (opens in a new tab) had joined the Twilight Hermit Order. He found out that many important figures were members of this organization. He learned about the Blasphemy Slate (opens in a new tab) and obtained much knowledge from the gatherings and Hermes (opens in a new tab).

In 1349, they ordered Qilangos (opens in a new tab) and Jason Beria (opens in a new tab) (after the former died) to assassinate Pallas Negan (opens in a new tab) in order to escalate the conflict between the New Party and the Conservative Party of the Loen Kingdom (opens in a new tab).

They warned Amyrius Rieveldt (opens in a new tab) to not participate in the matter regarding the governor general on Sonia Sea.

They helped support George Augustus III (opens in a new tab)'s attempt to become a Black Emperor. This includes covering up his true sequence level and his schemes (including the human trafficking and the mausoleums) from 1342 to 1350.

Through exacerbating the conflict between the New Party and the Conservative Party and manipulating important figures in the Loen Kingdom, they successfully invoked the war between Loen (opens in a new tab) and Feysac (opens in a new tab), which eventually became a World War.

Philosophy

They believe that people will always lose some of their individuality bit by bit until they "sleep". Hence, by trying hard to observe and remember oneself, one can use the various types of information to achieve the purification needed to face the final end of days.

They believe that dusk is inevitable, and that doomsday cannot be avoided.

They believe in the Ancient Sun God (opens in a new tab), and that “He” is not truly dead. When dusk comes and everything ends, “He” will wake up from “His” slumber and let everything return to “Him “. “He” will create a new world, a new history.

Characteristics

As an organization, they are extremely secretive with members in various high-level positions in the churches and military. With help from these members, they are able to push events to happen as their wish. Any mention of the order's name will be known.

This order is in possession of the Second Blasphemy Slate (opens in a new tab). They require their members to take on either the Visionary Pathway, Sun Pathway, White Tower Pathway or Tyrant Pathway. This rule does not apply to members who are already Beyonders of other pathways before joining.

They hold a grudge against the True Creator (opens in a new tab).

Rules

Without permission, the organization must not be named. Once the name of the Twilight Hermit Order is mentioned in the outside world, it will be immediately noticed. The member who violates the rule will be eliminated.

Gatherings

They gather on a realistic dreamscape that connects the eastern and western ends of the continent.

Moses Ascetic Order

The Moses Ascetic Order is a secret organization founded in the Fourth Epoch (opens in a new tab) that believes in the Hidden Sage (opens in a new tab).

History

Lord of Mysteries (opens in a new tab)

The Moses Ascetic Order was first established by a few humans who had read the Blasphemy Slate (opens in a new tab). They believed in a non-anthropomorphic god, called the 'Hidden Sage (opens in a new tab)'.

Being morally upright pursuers of knowledge, the original Moses Ascetic Order was a very respectable organization, and it maintained a good relationship with the other major churches. According to Old Neil (opens in a new tab), they had a very good relationship with the Church of the Evernight Goddess (opens in a new tab) before their fall. Their beliefs and results on ritualistic magic have heavily influenced them.[[1]](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Moses_Ascetic_Order#cite_note-1)

Sometime later, the Hidden Sage (opens in a new tab), the god they believe in, came to life as an evil god that corrupted the order. The Moses Ascetic Order has fallen since then, turning into an evil cult that spreads evil and corruption.

Circle of Inevitability (opens in a new tab)

During spring 1359 of the Fifth Epoch (opens in a new tab), the Moses Ascetic Order was destroyed after being attacked by the Tarot Club (opens in a new tab), the Church of the God of Steam and Machinery (opens in a new tab), and the Church of the Evernight Goddess (opens in a new tab) as a part of Hunting Hidden Sage (opens in a new tab).

Philosophy

The Moses Ascetic Order believes in the 'Hidden Sage'. The description is that of a god, but it is more of an ideology, a natural law. For example, all objects are numeric. They held the view that the Hidden Sage is an embodiment of Spirit Numerology, or that knowledge is supreme, and the Hidden Sage is knowledge itself.

The members of the organization led ascetic lives to resist losing control and to resolve the effects of the remnant potion. They strictly kept their order’s secrets, and they upheld moral and religious precepts, determined in their pursuit of knowledge. They believe that humans continuously reincarnate after death. Anyone who entered the order would be barred from speaking for five years after becoming a Mystery Pryer. They would learn to remain silent, thereby cultivating and enhancing their focus. Their maxim is "Do as you wish, but do no harm."

Demoness Sect

The Demoness Sect is a secret organization which began as the Demoness Family in the Fourth Epoch (opens in a new tab). They worship the Primordial Demoness (opens in a new tab). They control the Demoness Pathway.

History

They were originally a family, the Demoness Family, with all members being descendants of the Primordial Demoness (opens in a new tab). They had very few members, and their beliefs were passed down through their bloodlines.

From the Fifth Epoch (opens in a new tab) on, when such an organizational structure was deemed unable to sustain their influence, they began to incorporate Beyonders of the Demoness Pathway who also believed in the Primordial Demoness (opens in a new tab) as members. This recruitment of non-blood related people into their fold turned them from the Demoness Family into the Demoness Sect.

The Demoness Sect is widely believed to have founded the Theosophy Order (opens in a new tab).

Philosophy

The Demoness Sect worship the Primordial Demoness (opens in a new tab). They believe that this secret existence is the true inheritor of the Creator (opens in a new tab). They believe that “She” was born out of the Chaos and was the earliest to be born from the Creator (opens in a new tab)’s body. “She” is also the ultimate Ender that ends everything.

Characteristics

It was rumored that they would kill the fathers of their children and abandon the baby boys; resulting in all the members being female.

In reality, it was the opposite. The fathers and the male children would all become females through advancing in the Demoness Pathway, and the female children would be given away. It was further stated that due to the influence of the Primordial Demoness (opens in a new tab), the Demoness Sect despises true female born Demonesses and if they ever encounter one they will eliminate them.

Before The Day of Many Disasters (opens in a new tab), if we don't count Sequence 4 Despairs, and only consider the accumulated number of currently living Sequence 3 and Sequence 2 Beyonders, the Demoness Sect has more than any orthodox god church (opens in a new tab). This is due to the power Sequence 3 Unaging has over resurrection and rebirth, giving them long lifespans and making them notoriously hard to kill. In this regard, only the Sanguine (opens in a new tab), whose lives are equally long, can compare with them, but still fall short.

However, the Demoness Sect is inferior to orthodox god churches in that, because their high-level members must be Demonesses, there are no angels from other pathways.

Hierarchy

After the Pale-White War near the end of the Fourth Epoch (opens in a new tab), the Primordial Demoness (opens in a new tab) fell into slumber, occasionally waking. All sect affairs are handled by high-ranking Demonesses titled by color. The right to name oneself with a color is received they reach Sequence 3.

They do not have clear subordination among themselves, directly reporting to the Matriarch. Currently, there are thirteen of them, some of whom are Angels (opens in a new tab), others powerful Saints. Their Matriarch, the Demoness of Gray Judith (opens in a new tab), is the child of Alista Tudor (opens in a new tab) and the Primordial Demoness (opens in a new tab).

Equipment

Many Sealed Artifact (opens in a new tab) were lost or stolen by Amon (opens in a new tab) during The Day of Many Disasters (opens in a new tab), leaving only 6 Grade 0 Artifacts.

Secret Order

The Secret Order is a secret organization founded by the Zaratul Family (opens in a new tab) at the end of the War of the Four Emperors (opens in a new tab).

History

The Secret Order is one of the most secretive organizations around. What is known is that their founder, Zaratul (opens in a new tab), botched the advancement into a Sequence 1 Beyonder and turned into a monster.

Although they rarely take direct action, the organization appears to be actively searching for relics of the Antigonus Family (opens in a new tab), an angel family (opens in a new tab) that once held control over the Fool Pathway. They previously possessed the Antigonus Family's Notebook (opens in a new tab), but it was accidentally sold by Regence (opens in a new tab). The Secret Order later dispatched members to retrieve it.

Following Zaratul’s escape from the Foggy Town (opens in a new tab) into the real world, Lucca Brewster (opens in a new tab) received some intel that there has been some reshuffling among the Secret Order in Intis (opens in a new tab).

Characteristics

Their goals and philosophy are unknown.

The Secret Order has deep connections to the Intis Republic (opens in a new tab). They form Bureau 8 (opens in a new tab), which is a branch of the Intis Intelligence Department.

Theosophy Order

The Theosophy Order is a secret organization that appeared in the early Fifth Epoch (opens in a new tab). It is a branch of the Demoness Sect (opens in a new tab).

History

Their origin is linked to the the Tamara Family (opens in a new tab), which experienced fractures at some point, leading to a group of them having close connections with the Demoness Family (opens in a new tab) in the Fourth Epoch (opens in a new tab).

Philosophy

They believe that the mind is fundamental to a person, while the physical flesh is a cage that restrains the mind. The reason why humans do evil is a result of the physical body’s influence. One has to use their spirituality to obtain knowledge, allowing the mind to gradually extricate from the body. Then, through the trials of the stars, they will eventually be separated from the material world, returning to the purest and truest self, obtaining eternal redemption.

Therefore, many extremist members of the Theosophy Order will make destroying the bodies of others their goal, which has led to many bloody massacres.

Characteristics

They hold Low-Sequence formulas of two pathways. The first is the more common Door Pathway which come from members of the Tamara Family (opens in a new tab). The other is the Demoness Pathway which rarely appears. There is currently no evidence that the Theosophy Order possesses Sequence 7 or higher potions.

Numinous Episcopate

The Numinous Episcopate is a secret organization founded in the early Fifth Epoch (opens in a new tab). They originate from the Southern Continent (opens in a new tab) and seek to revive Death (opens in a new tab), the first Underworld Emperor, who perished in the Fourth Epoch (opens in a new tab).

History

After Death (opens in a new tab) perished in the late Fourth Epoch (opens in a new tab), the Balam Empire (opens in a new tab) and the Church of Death which ruled the Southern Continent (opens in a new tab) collapsed as a result. A few surviving members of the Eggers Family (opens in a new tab) then started a new secret organization. The Numinous Episcopate was thus formed with goal of resurrecting Death (opens in a new tab).

However, the Seven Orthodox Churches (opens in a new tab) knew of their intentions and were quite proactive in rooting them out. After the colonial era, they almost vanished under attacks by the Seven Orthodox Churches, however, they stubbornly survived and spread toward the countries of the Northern Continent (opens in a new tab). The Church of the Evernight Goddess (opens in a new tab) consider them as one of their main targets.

Later, when the Evernight Goddess (opens in a new tab) acquired the Uniqueness (opens in a new tab) of the Death Pathway, "She" began to usurp the authority of Death from Artificial Death (opens in a new tab). "She" entrusted Klein Moretti (opens in a new tab) with managing the Artificial Death Faction of the Numinous Episcopate in Backlund (opens in a new tab) through Patrick Bryan (opens in a new tab).

Two members of the Royal Family Faction, Reaza (opens in a new tab) and Maslow, were ordered to take part in the Dream Festival (opens in a new tab) of 1358, conducted in Tizamo Town (opens in a new tab) of West Balam (opens in a new tab). Their goal was to obtain the ancient Death Mask (opens in a new tab) inside the tomb in the Dream Festival (opens in a new tab)'s center, which had endured corrosion from the River of Eternal Darkness (opens in a new tab) for over a millennium.

After The Fool (opens in a new tab)'s initial awakening, "He" had promoted reconciliation between the Royals of the Numinous Episcopate and the Orthodox Churches (opens in a new tab). Subsequently, colonial institutions of the Northern Continent (opens in a new tab) countries would gradually withdraw from the Southern Continent (opens in a new tab), returning governance to local populations. The Southern Continent (opens in a new tab) populations would have religious freedom. As long as it didn't involve evil gods, they could believe in deities like Death (opens in a new tab) or The Fool (opens in a new tab), and could continue believing in the Orthodox Gods (opens in a new tab) of the Northern Continent (opens in a new tab). Meanwhile, the Royals of the Numinous Episcopate promised The Fool (opens in a new tab) political-religious separation. They also provided substantial intelligence to the Orthodox Churches (opens in a new tab), helping them uncover several hidden bases of the Rose School of Thought (opens in a new tab).[[8]](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Numinous_Episcopate#cite_note-8)

After The Apocalypse (opens in a new tab), the Church of the Evernight Goddess (opens in a new tab), Church of the God of Combat (opens in a new tab), and the Church of the Death merged to form the Church of the Eternal Darkness (opens in a new tab), one of the three mainstream faiths in the world along with the Church of the Fool (opens in a new tab) and the Church of the Ruler of Calamity (opens in a new tab). Their worshipped God is the Eternal Darkness (opens in a new tab).

Philosophy

Their members believe that death is not the end and that everything can be reversed, even the dead can be resurrected.

Characteristics

Although they don’t resort to blood sacrifices or terrorism like some secret faith-based organizations, rituals are inherent to their nature. The Numinous Episcopate’s pursuit of Death (opens in a new tab)'s revival necessitates sacrificial rites.

In the Southern Continent, the Numinous Episcopate holds a comparable status to the Rose School of Thought (opens in a new tab). One of their goals is to eradicate the colonists and rebuild the Balam Empire (opens in a new tab).

Factions

Numinous Episcopate has many factions in their organization.

The strongest faction is the Royal Family Faction. With the descendants of the Eggers Family (opens in a new tab) as the core, they have established many organizations. Besides their leader, Sia Palenque Eggers (opens in a new tab), the royal family faction has five other saint apostles. Some of them are true demigods, while others rely on Sealed Artifacts (opens in a new tab).

In the Artificial Death Faction, the leader is Haiter (opens in a new tab) who was once an archbishop of the Church of Death from the time of the Balam Empire (opens in a new tab). They have three saints and an angel in West Balam (opens in a new tab).

The other factions are relatively weaker. The main ones are the Repose Faction and the Underworld Faction. Only by working together do they have three powerful Beyonders who are equivalent to the saint apostles.

Life School of Thought

The Life School of Thought is a Secret Organization (opens in a new tab) that controls the Wheel of Fortune Pathway and also has parts of the Moon Pathway.

History

Ever since the disappearance of the President of the Life School of Thought, Will Auceptin (opens in a new tab), an internal conflict broke out in the organization. Guided by the Primordial Moon (opens in a new tab), a branch of the Life School of Thought which grasped the Moon Pathway defected and joined the indulgence faction of the Rose School of Thought (opens in a new tab).

As of 1358, the Life School of Thought is an ally of the Tarot Club (opens in a new tab).

Philosophy

The Life School of Thought members revere fate and seek to understand fate. Their doctrine proposes that the world is divided into three major divisions, the world of absolute rationality, the spirit world, and the material world. The Life School of Thought specializes in astromancy, seeking to use medicine, music, light, wine, and fragrance, to eliminate paths with unfavorable star readings and fate. They believe that disasters and diseases are caused by the loss of balance between man and nature, as well as the balance between man and his own mind.

Hierarchy

Unlike most organizations that use a strict ranking hierarchy based on contribution to slowly disseminate information and knowledge, the Life School of Thought developed in a secretive manner with the master and apprentice format, but they have an Elder Council among the upper echelons. They resolved the conflict between the different internal factions. Since the Fifth Epoch (opens in a new tab), the Elder Council changed to a more trendy name known as the Fate Council. There are seven councillors, and the Snake of Mercury (opens in a new tab) is the president.

Rose School of Thought

The Rose School of Thought is a secret organization which used to worship the Chained God (opens in a new tab). Later, they changed their faith to the Mother Tree of Desire (opens in a new tab) who had corrupted "Him" and took "His" place.

History

The Rose School of Thought's origins can be traced back to before the Fourth Epoch (opens in a new tab), boasting a history stretching back over two millennia, all the way before the Cataclysm back to the time of Mutated King Kvastir (opens in a new tab) in the Second Epoch (opens in a new tab).

The organization took shape at the beginning of the Fifth Epoch (opens in a new tab), after the storms of the Berserk Sea (opens in a new tab) cut off the Northern (opens in a new tab) and Southern Continents (opens in a new tab), and the Southern Continent (opens in a new tab)'s Highlands Kingdom (opens in a new tab) and the Paz Kingdom were founded. The Rose School of Thought was an orthodox organization in the Southern Continent (opens in a new tab). It ruled the Paz Valley (opens in a new tab) and Star Highlands (opens in a new tab), where the government and the church were one.

The organization has always been focused on the  ![Chained Symbol2 (opens in a new tab)](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway) Chained Pathway. The original belief was about temperance, until the Mother Tree of Desire (opens in a new tab) corrupted the Chained God (opens in a new tab). The organization slowly transformed into believing in releasing and indulging in desires. In the beginning, the Indulgence Faction and the Temperance Faction barely tolerated each other. But later, the Indulgence Faction started claiming that the Chained God (opens in a new tab), whom both factions believe in, is actually the embodiment of the Mother Tree of Desire (opens in a new tab).

After Suah (opens in a new tab) was born in the year 428 of the Fifth Epoch (opens in a new tab), the belief in releasing desires and bloody sacrifices became dominant in the organization. When the invasion by the Northern Continent (opens in a new tab)'s countries occurred, they lost their country and went underground, becoming clandestine. On the one hand, they resisted the colonists and attempted to expel them from the Southern Continent (opens in a new tab). On the other hand, they frequently caused terrorist acts in the Northern Continent (opens in a new tab). This actually had no effect on their resistance to colonization. Instead, it incurred the hatred of the people of various countries.

After Suah (opens in a new tab) successfully advanced to become a true Abomination, an internal war began. The Indulgence Faction launched a surprise attack on the Temperance Faction and inflicted severe injuries. The remaining members of Temperance Faction, such as Reinette Tinekerr (opens in a new tab), Sharron (opens in a new tab) and Maric (opens in a new tab), fled in a sorry state and were hunted down for a long time until they joined the Church of the Fool (opens in a new tab). The Indulgence Faction has fully transformed the Rose School of Thought to their belief towards the Mother Tree of Desire (opens in a new tab). If it weren't for The Fool (opens in a new tab)'s protection, the Temperance Faction members would have more or less been influenced by the Mother Tree of Desire (opens in a new tab) in the past 1356-1358.

Factions

Indulgence Faction

The Indulgence Faction believes in the Mother Tree of Desire (opens in a new tab). After the internal war broke out and the Temperance Faction was defeated and had to flee, they became the main faction of the Rose School of Thought, and transformed the rest of the organization towards Indulgence ideologies.

Temperance Faction

The Temperance Faction believed in Temperance of desires, and originally believed in the Chained God (opens in a new tab). They believe that "Temperance is not about perpetual suppression; it is about rationally understanding oneself and preparing for an explosive moment when it truly matters".

After being defeated by the Temperance Faction and were forced to flee away, they eventually became part the Church of the Fool (opens in a new tab). They introduce themselves as "the Temperance Faction of the Rose School of Thought under the Church of the Fool (opens in a new tab)", and maintained that they are the true orthodox branch of the Rose School of Thought while viewing the Indulgence Faction as traitors who should change their organization's name.

Primordial Moon Faction

The Primordial Moon Faction were originally a branch of the Life School of Thought (opens in a new tab) from the  ![Moon Symbol2 (opens in a new tab)](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Moon_Pathway) Moon Pathway. Later, guided by the Primordial Moon (opens in a new tab), they defected from the Life School of Thought (opens in a new tab) and joined the Indulgence Faction, becoming the Primordial Moon Faction.

The Primordial Moon Faction also includes other believers of the Primordial Moon (opens in a new tab): such as Klarman (opens in a new tab): who had received the blessings of the Primordial Moon (opens in a new tab), and later on received a revelation to join the Rose School of Thought. Klarman (opens in a new tab) sometimes believed that the Mother Tree of Desire (opens in a new tab) and the Primordial Moon (opens in a new tab) were different sides of the same great existence, but other times, he felt that "They" were not only different, but there was also a deep conflict between "Them". This resulted in the Primordial Moon (opens in a new tab) believers not getting enough attention when they later joined the Rose School of Thought. Apart from receiving some bestowments in the form of items, they were only placed in noncritical positions.

Devil Faction

According to Leonard Mitchell (opens in a new tab), he has observed some rudimentary cooperation among the Mother Tree of Desire (opens in a new tab)'s organizations over the past year or two, with even some members of the Devil Families (opens in a new tab) becoming involved with the Indulgence Faction.

From the series of events involving Hisoka (opens in a new tab) and the Dream Festival (opens in a new tab), Lumian Lee (opens in a new tab) suspected that there was also a Devil Faction within the Rose School of Thought.

Philosophy

The Rose School of Thought was believes in the Chained God (opens in a new tab), and they believe that magic is a science and art that changes according to one's will to change things. This requires the establishment of a religious ritual system, including law and order. At the same time, they believe that their wills originated from various desires. Combined with Beyonder powers, they were able to accomplish all sorts of unimaginable things. Because of these ideas, they retained an ancient and bloody tradition of primitive sacrifice including the skinning of people and using the skulls of children as ritual objects, letting a large number of believers fervently release all sorts of desires.

The Rose School of Thought has held different beliefs for ages. Their core principle revolves around the notion that willpower is born from diverse desires, capable of reshaping reality and achieving incredible feats. Yet, there's a divide in how they approach these desires.

Some adhere to indulgence, embracing passionate cravings at all times, even resorting to bloody or primitive sacrificial rituals to bolster their willpower. On the other hand, the temperance faction, working from the potions' names, advocates for suppressing desires, keeping them pent up within their hearts, only unleashing them during critical moments to unleash formidable forces.

Characteristics

Their main sphere of influence is the Southern Continent (opens in a new tab). They are known for promoting unrestrained desires and bloody rituals.

According to Mr. Poet (opens in a new tab)'s experience, while the organizations exhibit clear purpose, decisiveness, accurate foresight, and excellent planning at a high level, they often appear chaotic, disorderly, and even mad in specific events. This reflects the nature of the evil god itself.

In simpler terms, these organizations don't cooperate or communicate well, often acting erratically and unpredictably.

According to Klein's conjecture, the Rose School of Thought has about the same number of demigods as the Aurora Order (opens in a new tab): the number of Saints number around 5, and the number of Angels (opens in a new tab) and Grade 0 Sealed Artifacts (opens in a new tab) number around 2 to 3.

Aurora Order

The Aurora Order is a newly founded secret organization (opens in a new tab) that sprang into existence about two to three hundred years ago. They worship the True Creator (opens in a new tab).

History

They were behind an incident in Tingen City (opens in a new tab) that involved the True Creator's Spawn (opens in a new tab).

They had participated in the Great Smog of Backlund (opens in a new tab) and tried to allow the True Creator (opens in a new tab) to descend on earth soon after.

After The Apocalypse (opens in a new tab), the Angel of Fate, Ouroboros (opens in a new tab), had returned from the Western Continent (opens in a new tab), merging the Aurora Order with the Eternal Blazing Sun Church (opens in a new tab), turning the Aurora Order into the shadow under the sun's rays, handling tasks inconvenient for clergy, and executing acts of punishment.

Philosophy

They believe that the True Creator (opens in a new tab) was the rightful heir to the legacy of the Ancient Sun God (opens in a new tab). "He" was the great being who created this world, the father of all living creatures.

They believe that they all originate from the True Creator, carrying the divine essence "He" bestowed upon them. Divinity exists within every living being, and no one is inherently more noble than another. They distinguish status based on their proximity to "Him" and their ability to embrace "His" teachings. It is through their shared divinity that they can consume potions, face trials, and accumulate more divine power. Ultimately, they can become Angels (opens in a new tab) in service to the True Creator.

Characteristics

The Aurora Order is known to the public as an illegal terrorist organization. Their reputation has been solidified by the papers so many times to the point of them becoming a horror element or classic antagonist in many novels.

That said, their members are either lunatics or potential lunatics who are always looking for opportunities to take revenge on the world, and one can’t use common sense to guess their actions. Because of that, they are sometimes used as a scapegoat by the officials.

The Aurora Order's primary pathway is the Hanged Man Pathway. They also have some influence over the Door Pathway through disciples of the Abraham Family (opens in a new tab) who later turned traitor and joined the Aurora Order.

They are enemies with the Rose School of Thought (opens in a new tab) and those who side with them and the Outer Deities (opens in a new tab). They possess an uncanny ability to detect them. Although not every person they target is their believer, at least 70% are.

The number of Angels (opens in a new tab) and Grade 0 Sealed Artifacts (opens in a new tab) that the Aurora Order has number around 2 to 3.

Idol Statue

Unlike the Orthodox Churches (opens in a new tab), where there are only Sacred Emblems and no idols. The symbol of the True Creator (opens in a new tab) is the True Creator's Idol Statue. Its a naked male deity statue hanging upside down on the cross. The rusty iron nails piercing the deity's body and the fresh red blood rendered around them. At the face, all features were blurred except for the eyes, which were very clear, tightly closed, as if bearing all pain and guilt.

Prayer Gesture

Initially, their prayer gesture involved making a motion from the bottom to the top, right to left on their chest, resembling an upside-down cross.

After the True Creator (opens in a new tab) completely fused with Adam (opens in a new tab), their gesture transformed into a sign of the cross, starting from the top and moving to the bottom, left, and right on the chest.

Hierarchy

The Aurora Order has seven Saints and twenty-two Oracles. These Oracles use the alphabet as their code names. They are all Beyonders (opens in a new tab), with the weakest Oracle being Sequence 7 and the strongest Sequence 5.

Although Oracles have a Saint directly above them, most Oracles can directly hear the ravings of the True Creator (opens in a new tab) and His instructions, giving them strong autonomy. Yet, they still follow the orders of their Saint superiors and report to them on most matters, but there are a few matters that completely bypass their superiors. Recommending a candidate Oracle is one of them.

They are adept at hiding themselves. Should an Oracle die, a new Oracle will take their place. The Oracles are usually conveners of Beyonder Gatherings (opens in a new tab).

Psychology Alchemists

The Psychology Alchemists is a secret organization holding formulas of the Visionary Pathway. It has its own theory on consciousness and subconsciousness. Due to the influence of Hermes (opens in a new tab), it became a division of the Twilight Hermit Order (opens in a new tab) and has been functioning under Adam (opens in a new tab)'s will.

History

The original Psychology Alchemists was just a seminar for enthusiasts who all believed that the mind has limitless power and infinite wonders. Later on, this seminar obtained a treasure map and found the relics left behind by Hermes (opens in a new tab).

The information he left behind shows that mind dragons have advanced far into this field and have achieved the same level of achievement as deities. Those materials laid the foundation and direction for the research of the Psychology Alchemists.

The discovery of the ruins marked the establishment of the organization. The members at the beginning wanted to maintain a state of pure academic discussion, but they would always need help when encountering matters. Similarly, they lacked items and research materials. That’s why the Psychology Alchemists slowly became systematic and transformed into a real secret organization.

However, compared to other secret organizations, its structure and relationships are still relatively loose.

In Year 1352, after Klein became a King of Angels (opens in a new tab), he destroyed and killed The President (opens in a new tab) inside their headquarter, the Garden of Eden (opens in a new tab). Klein suspected the Psychology Alchemists might appear again in the future, no longer as a shadow of the Twilight Hermit Order (opens in a new tab), but under the control of Hermes (opens in a new tab).

Characteristics

They believe that a person's consciousness is an island, and the subconscious is the portion of the sea beneath the island. The collective subconscious is the surrounding sea. The sky belongs to the Spirit World.

Headquarters

Their headquarters is at a city inside the Sea of Collective Consciousness in everyone's mind. This city is called the Garden of Eden (opens in a new tab). The city can be accessed from everywhere in human society as long as there are two people around, excluding the one who wants to enter. Members of the council can enter the city as long as they bring their persona mask with them.
