Sequence Protection System

The rules governing Sequence protection.

The [Sequence Protection System] exists mainly to prevent stronger characters from unfairly targeting, overwhelming, or simply removing weaker/less active characters without proper reason. The aim of this system isn't to remove danger from roleplay, but rather to prevent one-sided abuse of Sequence gaps. Characters can and will still face consequences for their actions.

BASICS

A Higher Sequence OC cannot attack or harm another OC that is a Lower Sequence than them without valid reason, or consent.

For clarity, when talking about a "Higher Sequence OC" and a "Lower Sequence OC," here is what is exactly meant:

  • A Sequence 7 OC and a Sequence 8 OC.
  • A Sequence 5 OC and a Sequence 7 OC.

-# Basically, if there at least a one-sequence gap.

Under normal circumstance, for example, a Sequence 5 OC cannot attack a Sequence 6 OC. However, there are cases where this [Sequence Protection System] doesn't apply.

APPLICATION CASES

The [Sequence Protection System] only applies when the Higher Sequence OC tries to initiate a hostile action. If such a hostile action was instead initiated by the Lower Sequence OC, then the Higher Sequence OC has all the right to defend themselves, and even retaliate against their aggressor.

Examples where a Higher Sequence OC can attack a Lower Sequence OC include, but are not limited to:

  • The weaker OC attacks the stronger OC first.
  • The weaker OC harms, curses, steals from, blackmails, or exposes the stronger OC.
  • The weaker OC kills, injures, betrays, or seriously harms a member of the stronger OC’s organization.
  • The weaker OC has a public bounty on their head/is wanted by official organizations.
  • The weaker OC interferes with a mission, ritual, investigation, hunt, or operation.
  • The weaker OC is openly affiliated with an enemy organization.

-# Any OC with a Boon from Outer Deities will not have the Protection System for them. They can be hunted and killed by OCs of any level at any time.

ATTACKING HIGHER SEQUENCES

Lower Sequence OCs can attack OCs who have a Higher Sequence than them. However, in doing so, they forsake their [Sequence Protection System] for that conflict specifically. If any of the above situations are triggered by the Lower Sequence OC, the Higher Sequence OC can retaliate in turn.
