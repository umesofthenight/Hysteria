Soul

The layers of the soul and their spiritual properties.

According to the mainstream way of mysticism, the non-physical parts (soul) of a human is split into four levels. They are the Spirit Body (Soul Body), Astral Body (Astral Projection), Body of Heart and Mind and Ether Body. They need the Eternal Darkness (opens in a new tab) and the Mother Goddess of Depravity (opens in a new tab) to ensure true rest and reincarnation respectively.

Soul Body

At a soul's core is the innermost Soul Body, or Spirit Body (精神体, Jīngshén tǐ), which is also everyone’s basic spirituality. All biological creatures have spirituality and have a Soul Body (Spirit Body). The goal of Cogitation (opens in a new tab) is to strengthening the Spirit Body. At low sequences, Spirit Vision (opens in a new tab) places a great burden on the Spirit Body.

The Cards of Blasphemy can each fuse with their possessors by wrapping the Soul Body (Spirit Body) around the card. This will materialise the tarot attire and solidify the Spirit Body into reality.

Parasitisation at this level level only shows as a blob of light hiding in their body. It would seem to be made of countless ringed worms, hidden deep inside their Soul Body.

Spirit Body Threads

Attached to the Spirit Body are illusory, thin black lines known as Spirit Body Threads (灵体之线, Líng tǐ zhī xiàn). These threads stem from inside the Spirit Body, and extend outwards in different directions into infinity. Spirit Body Threads can be perceived or interacted with using certain Beyonder Abilities. The smaller the creature, the harder it is to see its Spirit Body Threads.

While Spirit Body Threads are not part of the Spirit Body itself, they can be used as a means to influence the different parts of the Soul directly.

Astral Projection

Outside the Soul Body is the Astral Projection (星灵体, Xīng líng tǐ). It is the means for the Spirit Body to communicate with the Spirit World and with Astral World. It’s considered an external manifestation of the Soul Body. Additionally, it will be directly related to a person's personal ambition and prevailing emotions.

The scenes seen after consuming the potion are scenes that Astral Projection sees when it wanders through the spirit world. The emotional state also manifests itself because of the Astral Projection. At higher Sequences, Spirit Vision (opens in a new tab) will reach a relatively high stage and it can even be able to see someone else’s Astral Projection.

Divination and many magic spells are cast through the Astral Projection.

The inner layer of the Astral Projection represents the prevailing emotions.

| Color | Meaning | | ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | | Red | Passion and excitement | | Orange | Warmth and satisfaction | | Yellow | Happiness and extroversion | | Green | Calm and peace | | Blue | Rational thought and indifference, coldness and stillness that one is in thought | | White | Eagerness to improve | | Dark Colors | Worry, sorrow, and silence | | Purple | Spirituality dominance; spirituality is taking control of the lead, coldness, and estrangement |

Body of Heart and Mind

Further out will be the Body of Heart and Mind (心智体, Xīnzhì tǐ). From this point forward, it will combine with the physical body. It involves a person's brain and is an overall manifestation of their inferential abilities, analytical abilities, observational abilities, and identification abilities. Some potions will mainly raise this. Quite a several magic spells target it as well.

Dreams

Most people are in their unfiltered selves or their most honest sides in their dreams until they're given lucidity. Normally, their dream self would easily answer any question and are more talkative. Although, not everyone is inherently talkative and may refuse like with Cattleya (opens in a new tab), acting like a child to some extent.

Dreams are a common method of investigation for Nighthawks (opens in a new tab) for a variety of factors: people in their dreams are unfiltered, making their accounts more trustworthy without any resistance or chance of consciously lying; and having access to Nightmare, making at least half of every Nighthawks (opens in a new tab) team, which is the captain, or half of the Red Gloves (opens in a new tab) members to be able to enter and manipulate dreams. Additionally, a Beyonder (opens in a new tab) with abilities from the dream domain can also guide the target to conjure the corresponding object, simplifying the process of obtaining a sketch of any object or person they're looking for.

Examples

  • Leonard Mitchell (opens in a new tab) entered Daisy (opens in a new tab)'s dream to investigate Sherlock Moriarty (opens in a new tab), when he asked Sherlock's appearance, Daisy in the dream then conjured him and Leonard observed the conjured Sherlock Moriarty's appearance.
  • Klein Moretti (opens in a new tab) entered Elizabeth (opens in a new tab)'s dream through a Dream Charm (opens in a new tab) and immediately found her to be extremely talkative as well as to investigate what's haunting her.

Spirit Channelling (opens in a new tab)

It is a method to either communicate with a person's Body of Heart and Mind or to obtain various revelations through divination.

Spirit Channelling, too, is a common method of investigation, more especially on dead beings where any other form of communication is impossible. Not only is it possible through the ability of Spirit Medium, it can also be performed through Ritualistic Magic, allowing any Beyonder (opens in a new tab) adept at Ritualistic Magic to perform Spirit Channelling on any appropriate target.

Ether Body

The outermost layer is the Ether Body (以太体, Yǐtài tǐ). It is a manifestation of a person's vital energies and physical form. The aura colour seen by Spirit Vision (opens in a new tab) is an external phenomenon of the Ether Body. From their thickness, brightness, and colour, one can determine a person's health and emotional state.

Ether Body and the colours of the auras:

| Color | Meaning | | ------ | --------------------------------------------- | | Red | Limbs and parts that are required in motion. | | Purple | The head and brain’s surface. | | Orange | Spots that excrete waste. | | Yellow | The digestion system. | | Green | The heart and other regulatory systems. | | Blue | Throat and other parts of the nervous system. | An entirely balanced body will make a body be cloaked in white. That is a symbol of health. Once it turns dark or the thickness thins, the colour will change. That indicates that the corresponding spot has turned problematic. It means it’s in a state of exhaustion or illness.

A sufficiently potent Spirit Vision (opens in a new tab) can allow them to directly identify a Beyonder (opens in a new tab) and see the corresponding Pathway in the core of their Ether Body (through certain imagery) and even identify their sequence.

  • Spectator: Colours deep in their aura would blend together. It became pure and serene, like a lake that was clear and reflective.
  • Seafarer: A deep blue, akin to a tidal storm.

Death and Reincarnation

Before Amanises (opens in a new tab) ascended to Eternal Darkness (opens in a new tab), Souls on Earth would wander the Spirit World aimlessly until they got assimilated by it. However, following "Her" ascension to Eternal Darkness (opens in a new tab), all Souls on Earth would now go to into the River of Eternal Darkness (opens in a new tab). Eventually, to have Souls reincarnated back into Earth, the symbolism of the Mother Goddess of Depravity (opens in a new tab) would be needed to establish the Cycle of Reincarnation.

To a lesser extent, the Sequence 0 Death and Sequence 0 Mother, if "They" cooperated, could create "Their" own Cycle of Reincarnation, but limited to a single star system.
