Symbolism

The meaning and authority carried by symbols.

Symbolism, or Symbols are something beyond our sensory world. It's not something that can be accurately described by the information that ears, noses, and eyes obtain. What one obtains are simply indescribable experiences and direct revelations. They also appear as abstract characters or pictorial Symbols. These Symbols represent different items and different meanings.

Only by grasping the ability to interpret the Symbols can one really take the next step into the World of Mysticism.

  • Symbols are the origin of Authorities, and which all abilities stem from, all abilities are a mere lesser manifestation of Symbolism.
  • Authorities and Symbolisms are more like the materialization of Permissions and Concepts: without being granted access, they cannot be used.
  • As such, Symbolisms and Authorities naturally cannot be copied, one need to be granted the Permission access to use them.
  • Beyonders (opens in a new tab) at certain levels become able to utilize certain Symbolism, and at the level of Great Old One, "They" become the embodiment and owner of it.
  • Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab) can fall, but symbolism won't disappear, the Symbols will become ownerless, however, this doesn't mean they can't be used or don't have influence over the world.
  • For example, return to when the City of Calamity (opens in a new tab) had its consciousness scattered and was just sealed. If a Red Priest performed an upward Sacrifice, it wouldn't fail because there is no corresponding Great Old One (opens in a new tab) or corresponding Sefirah (opens in a new tab) consciousness.
  • The Symbolism of Contracts, even though it's currently ownerless , it can be used by Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab) and True Gods (opens in a new tab), by both parties agreeing and making someone, or something, such as a Sefirah (opens in a new tab), take the role of a witness for the Contract.
  • Even though there is no Great Old One (opens in a new tab) that symbolizes Separation, it still exists and can be used, and the Symbols of Termination naturally bring Separation.
  • During Sacrifice or Proxy, the target pointed to is the corresponding Symbolism itself, and not necessarily the corresponding Sefirah (opens in a new tab)'s consciousness.
  • Regardless of whether there is consciousness, Symbolism follows rules to automatically respond.
  • If there is a Sefirah consciousness, even at Tier 2 Half Great Old One (opens in a new tab), it can influence the Symbolism, controlling it to refuse or weaken.
  • Even when a Sefirah or Uniqueness (opens in a new tab) is Sealed, its Symbolism still exists, as such prayers directed at the Symbolism or Authority can obtain a response. If they fail, it's due to other reasons.
  • A Symbolism itself cannot be completely Sealed, at most they can be partially Sealed, otherwise a single fight would lead to cosmic apocalypse.
  • Each Pathway has its own pathway symbol, consisted from different symbols, for example:
  • The  ![Fool Symbol2 (opens in a new tab)](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway) Fool's Symbol consists from Pupil-less Eye representing Secrecy and the Contorted Lines representing Change.
  • The  ![Error Symbol2 (opens in a new tab)](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway) Error's Symbol consists from Symbols representing Parasitism, Time, and Fate.
  • The  ![Door Symbol2 (opens in a new tab)](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway) Door's Symbol is numerous layers upon layers of illusory Doors.
  • The  ![Wheel of Fortune Symbol2 (opens in a new tab)](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Wheel_of_Fortune_Pathway) Wheel of Fortune's Symbol is a gigantic silver snake formed a wheel that was connected to each other.
  • Charms essence is the power obtained from a high-level existence. and it use powers obtained from the corresponding Symbols to stabilize it.
  • Each Pathway (opens in a new tab) domain have different Symbols capable of creating different effects.
  • Four of the Error Pathway's domain's Symbols can create effects corresponding to Theft, Aging, Parasitism, and Deceit.
  • The signs obtained through Divination are often Symbolic and needs interpenetration, it can be misinterpreted or overlooked, leading to incorrect or incomplete information.

Pillar Symbolism

Each of the three Pillar (opens in a new tab) holds the corresponding Pillar Symbolism, as the ruler of one of three main layers of the universe:

  • God Almighty (opens in a new tab), Lord of the Astral World.
  • Lord of Mysteries (opens in a new tab), Dominator of the Spirit World.
  • Mother Goddess of Depravity (opens in a new tab), Sovereign of the Physical World.

Aside from the above, there are two other Pillar-level Symbols:

  • Termination: The core Symbol of the Fourth Pillar (opens in a new tab), representing the endpoint of destiny, the universe's destruction, and the finale of all things.
  • Chaos Primogenitor: The Symbol of the Nascent Original Creator (opens in a new tab) of the next Universe. Nurturing the Chaos Primogenitor is one of Mother Goddess of Depravity (opens in a new tab)'s two most vital symbols and a key reason "She" remains a Pillar (opens in a new tab) even after the Brood Hive (opens in a new tab)'s separation. Once born, the Chaos Primogenitor doesn't belong to "Her" anymore. At the moment, "She" can borrow "Its" power.

Astral World

Symbols are heavily connected to the Astral World, a realm where concepts, authorities, and Symbols converge, as such Gods can use it to modify and control concepts and Symbolism through it.

As the Lord of the Astral World, the God Almighty (opens in a new tab) wields the ability to interfere with the Symbols of others through it, or partially Seal them.

Beyonder Characteristics

High Sequence Beyonder Characteristics have some of the corresponding Pathway Symbolism which gives them some traits as materials.

  • ![Chained Symbol2 (opens in a new tab)](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway) Chained has the Binding Symbolism, making high-Sequence Beyonder Characteristics ideal Sealing material.

Symbolic Existences

Beyonders (opens in a new tab) at certain levels become able to utilize certain Symbolism or become embodiment of it.

Great Old Ones

At the level of Above the Sequence, one becomes a direct representation of a certain aspect of the universe, and is able to exert influence on the universe as a whole, or in any known location.

"Their" existence exerts Symbolic certain influence around "Them" that affects the environment by mere presence, which is something authorities don't do, but it's not the expected kind of influence, for example:

  • The mere presence of the Calamity of Destruction (opens in a new tab), if "They" didn't restrain "Themselves", would bring Calamities for everything around "Them".
  • The Monarch of Decay (opens in a new tab) would naturally cause an entire planet and all living beings to start Decaying, eventually either rotting to death or becoming disgusting creatures loyal to "Him".
  • When fighting against the Lord of Mysteries (opens in a new tab), the Symbolism of Foolishness will gradually deepen its influence over time, making it hard to notice that one has fallen under its influence.

Among all Great Old Ones, the Lord of Mysteries (opens in a new tab) is undoubtedly the best and most proficient at utilizing Symbolism, as such the pathways under "Them", such as  ![Fool Symbol2 (opens in a new tab)](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway) Fool and  ![Error Symbol2 (opens in a new tab)](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway) Error, are naturally able to use surface level Symbolism application of their authorities.

True Gods

Aside from the Pathways that belongs to the Lord of Mysteries (opens in a new tab), some Sequence 0 Gods can utilize certain Symbols that are tied to "Their" authorities, using it at surface level.

The examples include but are not limited to the following:

  • The Chained can utilize the Symbolism of Curses at surface level.
  • The Moon has the Symbolism of the Moon.
  • The Sun wields the Symbolism of the Inextinguishable Light.
  • The Hanged Man symbolizes Sin-Bearing and Sacrifice.
  • The Red Priest wields the Symbolism of Lord of War.

Angels

Angels would bring different changes due to the influence of their Symbolism, and at the level of Sequence 1 Angel (opens in a new tab), "They" become a Symbol of certain phenomena. While in a battle between equals, this Symbol has an impact, it's not decisive.

The examples include but are not limited to the following:

  • A Conqueror symbolizes War, and "His" presence might symbolize that a War can no longer be avoided.
  • A Weather Warlock's existence, if "He" didn't suppress and restrain "His" psyche, would make the weather in the areas "He" is in gradually become more volatile and extreme, and the likelihood of war or massacre events occurring will continually increase.
  • A Miracle Invoker's existence would make more impossible things more likely to happen.

Mythical Creature Form

Mythical Creatures have three-dimensional layers of mysterious Symbols around "Their" body, different Mythical Creatures have different Godhood Symbols, these Symbols will be different as the Beyonder advances based on the newly obtained characteristic, the mysticism knowledge contained within the potion, and the personal traits.

Advancing in Sequences makes the Symbols even more complex.

  • Klein Moretti (opens in a new tab)'s Mythical Creature Form as a Bizarro Sorcerer included Symbols directly presenting the abstract concepts of Bizarreness, Madness, Change, Strength, and Wisdom.
