The World

The planes of existence and the laws that separate them.

Spirit World

The Spirit World (灵界, Spirit Boundary, Língjiè) is the higher-dimensional plane inhabited by spirits, both positive or negative of various spiritual manifestations. It is the source location that encompasses all Contracted Creatures (opens in a new tab), and the convergence of all information.

Description

According to the descriptions in the Book of Secrets (opens in a new tab), the existence of the Spirit World was quite magical. It completely overlapped with the real world, so everyone could obtain revelations from the Spirit World at any time. Every person must be connected to the spirit world because one's Astral Projection is located there. It can obtain all kinds of abstract information, which is the reason why revelations can be obtained from divination.

However, the Spirit World didn't distinguish between directions, and the past, present, or future could even intersect there. It was like infinite knowledge, information, and illusions were spiritually gathered and compressed into a strange sea. It was different from the “world” as one would imagine according to usual concepts or logic. It allows for quick travel between places, for instance, a destination that would normally take months to reach would be shortened to a few days.

The Spirit World is where the Seven Lights (opens in a new tab) reside.

The Sefirah Castle (opens in a new tab) is located above the Spirit World. The Lord of Mysteries (opens in a new tab) is the Pillar (opens in a new tab) presiding over the Spirit World, and is the "Dominator of the Spirit World".

The highest sequence level a Spirit World Creature can achieve is Kings of Angels (opens in a new tab).

History

On the first day, the Original Creator (opens in a new tab) awoke and created this world, but Reality (opens in a new tab), the Spirit World, the Astral World, and Fate (opens in a new tab) were still mixed together.

On the second day, the Oldest One (opens in a new tab) birthed the Mother Goddess of Depravity (opens in a new tab). Reality gained a foundation, the Spirit World and Astral World were separated, and the River of Fate (opens in a new tab) began to flow. Soon after, the Mother Tree of Desire (opens in a new tab) was born, giving rise to life and introducing evil and desire. Then came the Son of Chaos (opens in a new tab), who established order-and with it, shadows naturally emerged.

On the third day, the Oldest One (opens in a new tab) and the Mother Goddess (opens in a new tab) united, and the Mother of All Things gave birth to a firstborn daughter, the Goddess of Fate (opens in a new tab). From then on, the fates of all things began to intertwine, no longer in absolute chaos but instead forming relative patterns and foreseeable futures.

After the Goddess of Fate (opens in a new tab), the other Outer Deities (opens in a new tab) were born one after another from the union between the Original Creator (opens in a new tab) and the Mother Goddess of Depravity (opens in a new tab).

After the awakening of the Original Creator (opens in a new tab), the Great Destruction of the Spirit World happened, and the entire Universe's Spirit World was destroyed, and the Seven Lights (opens in a new tab) of Earth and the entire world perished.

Corresponding Pathways

The Fool Pathway wields the biggest portion of the Spirit World authority, and can be called a mini-Lord of The Spirit World, with the complete dominator being the Lord of Mysteries (opens in a new tab), who wields the authorities of Error and Door, and the complete symbol of a Pillar (opens in a new tab).

As the Spirit World is a place where all kinds of Information converge and Information is Knowledge, it is naturally influenced by Knowledge Pathways. Therefore, the Hermit and the Paragon all have the Spirit World Mentor authority.

Other Sequences and Pathways can influence the Spirit World as well, such as the Door, Error, Chained, Moon, Darkness, and Death Pathways.

Astral World

The Astral World (星界, Star Realm, Xīngjiè) is a plane of existence filled with abstract symbolisms and authorities. The space within the Astral World doesn't physically exist, and is only the concept and symbolism of space.

Description

The Astral World was even more abstract than the Spirit World as though all the authorities and symbolism in the universe were included inside-every object appeared. And the light that illuminated this area wasn't spread uniformly. It was mainly concentrated in a few places, stacking together, forming spots created by a prism.

It seems to be similar to the Cosmos (opens in a new tab), dark, deep, and vast, but actually has a lot of peculiarities. For example, when you see a sun-like star hanging in the distance, it emits normal light and heat. If you try to get closer, without being damaged, you will find that the Astral World there is like a black curtain. The sun is directly painted with oil paint, and there are a lot of concepts and symbols gathered around. Furthermore, the drawing of the sun wasn't that nice, like a child without any foundation in art casually scrawled about. It was both comical and frightening.

Reality (opens in a new tab)-based concepts are ordinary things that everyone can understand, while symbolic concepts in the Astral World are higher. For example, even if a concept of Reality (opens in a new tab) disappears, as long as the corresponding Astral World concept remains, the idea will still exist. So, concepts of Reality (opens in a new tab) are merely reflections of the ones in the Astral World.

Everything in the Astral World is conceptual. If a human is transported into the Astral World, the human must also transform into a "conceptual" existence.

Klein (opens in a new tab) suspected that Astral World is a reflection of the fundamental law of the universe, chaotic and insane.

Corresponding Pathways

The Visionary holds the biggest portion of the Astral World's authority, and can be considered a mini-Lord of the Astral World, the complete dominator being the God Almighty (opens in a new tab), who wields the authorities of White Tower, Sun, Hanged Man and Tyrant, and the complete symbol of a Pillar (opens in a new tab).

The White Tower has the Astral World Mentor and the Astral World Ladder authorities.

For the Door, the Spirit World was related to Teleportation while the Astral World and the Cosmos (opens in a new tab) involved Wandering.

Physical World

The Physical World, also called Reality, is one of the three fundamental planes of existence that make up the universe, alongside the Spirit World and Astral World. It is governed by one of the three Pillars, the Mother Goddess of Depravity (opens in a new tab), who is the Sovereign of the Physical World. It includes both the current planet (opens in a new tab) where the story takes place, as well as the vast Cosmos (opens in a new tab) from beyond, home to various civilizations and Outer Deities (opens in a new tab).

Characteristics

The Physical World is the foundation of the universe, so unless everything ended, Reality would eternally exist. Thus, the Mother Goddess of Depravity (opens in a new tab), the Pillar (opens in a new tab) over the Physical World, possessed the characteristic of Indestructibility, never to perish, except for the following scenarios:

  • Encountering the Original Creator (opens in a new tab), who could make the impossible happen.
  • The emergence of the Fourth Pillar (opens in a new tab) symbolizing the end of everything.
  • Overextending the Chaos Primogenitor (opens in a new tab) unique symbolism as the nascent Original Creator of the next universe.

The Physical World has at least eleven dimensions of space.

Reality resists and dissipates Mysticism.

  • As a result, the Great Old Ones (opens in a new tab)' instant symbolic effect has a range, then gradually extends outward, needing time to fully cover. This is one of the lower manifestations of the Reality characteristic.
  • The immediate area of effect of Reality-oriented symbolisms like those of the Supernova Dominator (opens in a new tab) or Calamity of Destruction (opens in a new tab) was usually limited to a single Star System (opens in a new tab) or the radiation zone of a black hole. For other symbolisms, the immediate range would be smaller.
  • As for the upper level manifestation of the Reality characteristic, mechanism-wise, the Mother Goddess of Depravity (opens in a new tab) who symbolizes Reality cannot be killed by the Death symbolism of the Eternal Darkness (opens in a new tab) even though "Her" body is not larger than the instant symbolic range.

Underworld

The Underworld (冥界, Míngjiè) was created by Phoenix Ancestor Gregrace (opens in a new tab) and exists in the Spirit World.

According to Arrodes (opens in a new tab), "Her" divine kingdom the City of the Dead (死者之城, Sǐzhě zhī chéng), later renamed Calderón City (opens in a new tab) by an angel of Abraham Family (opens in a new tab), was also transferred there, and has gradually become a holy place for her descendants and believers. It was said that the city had a clue of the whereabouts of the River of Eternal Darkness (opens in a new tab) left behind.

After the fall of Death (opens in a new tab), the Underworld underwent a change that caused disturbances and anomalies. This disturbance leaves most of the spirits unable to sense or enter it, forcing them to wander the real world. As a result, when sentient beings die, they are very likely to become undead, wraiths, or evil spirits. Orthodox Churches of the Northern Continent (opens in a new tab) responded to this problem by sanction purification combined with compulsory cremation to prevent the dead emerging as undead creatures. Southern Continent (opens in a new tab) citizens however, devised many different measures to quell the undead outbreaks. Balam Empire (opens in a new tab), home of many beyonders following the Death Pathway, swiftly devised several solutions without having to resort to mandatory cremation. As for the areas with waning faith in Death (opens in a new tab), like Highlands Kingdom (opens in a new tab), Paz Kingdom, and Haagenti, there was a period when undead plagued the area and created a power vacuum.

It is later revealed that after the reigning Death (opens in a new tab) was deceased, one of the reasons for the original anomaly in the Underworld was the reanimation of the Phoenix Ancestor (opens in a new tab). It destroyed Death (opens in a new tab)'s palace, killed all of Death (opens in a new tab)'s Blessed present, and turned the depth of the Underworld into a Pale-White Nation. For a long time after the Underworld anomaly, the body of the Phoenix Ancestor still roamed the Pale-White Nation, keeping the high-ranked beings of the Death domain at bay. It has only fallen asleep in recent years.

As undead beings, Zombies and Wraiths can sense the presence of the Underworld before the fall of Death (opens in a new tab). After the anomaly occurred, they can only sense it if someone directly opens the door to the Underworld or is already from there. Otherwise, they can only sense it through certain traces.

Inside The Underworld

The Underworld is constructed by a seemingly bottomless pit with its edge being made up of many jumbled worlds that are layered atop each other, spiralling downwards the pit. The River Styx is a projection of the River of Eternal Darkness (opens in a new tab)'s tributary within the Underworld, and spans every layer of the Underworld.

In many notes of the Eggers Family (opens in a new tab), it is written that there are two ways to enter the Depth of the Underworld. The first is to descend layer by layer through the worlds encircling The Styx, which are used to punish sinners, ultimately arriving at the realm where the Blessed of Death (opens in a new tab) reside and where the Death (opens in a new tab)'s palace is. The second is to cross The Styx directly by being carried by the Ferryman. These seemingly contradictory methods of entering the depth stem from the fact that the geography of the Underworld doesn't completely align with the real world.

Within the Underworld, one can teleport but cannot teleport freely as it is considered as desecration of Death and attracts the punishment of the Underworld.

Pale-White Nation

Pale-White Nation refers to the Depth of the Underworld where the Death (opens in a new tab)'s palace lays in ruin.

The exterior of the palace ruin is composed of clusters of palaces half-sunken into the void, with a majestic main hall, several high towers, and aerial corridors connecting them. Deeper into the palace complex, the structure seems to sink further into the void, with the only visibly towering structure in the core area being the main hall and its four attached spires, while those on the periphery stood solidly on a barren wilderness.

All things within this nation had their color faded into pale white, infusing the entire world with pallor and giving off dim light. The only things that still retain their colour are the gold embedded on the surfaces of the building and the black at the bottom of the palace complex, representing the void. This black colour originated from two sources: one is the silently falling illusory dark River Styx from above, cascading behind the main hall. The other is the corpse of the Phoenix Ancestor, Gregrace (opens in a new tab), which slept atop the grand main hall.

Aside from the corpse of Gregrace (opens in a new tab), there is an egg below the belly of the Phoenix Ancestor, and the corpses of the blessed of Death (opens in a new tab) are also buried here. Among them were two giant serpents with feathered wings and hairy corpses, a bloated rotten giant, a humanoid mountain of white bones, and a woman with hair like thick snakes and her face being two-thirds decayed.

Mini-State Underworld

Mini-State Underworld is a rudimentary, localized version of the Underworld within the local spirit realm for spirits to return to. People of Raklev (opens in a new tab) area established one such mini-state after the Underworld underwent an anomaly. They established this mini state with the help of former official of Balam Empire (opens in a new tab) by utilizing their Gatekeeper ability to open the door to the Underworld and attach it into an Undying's skull from the Church of Death. This arrangement prevented the door from closing completely and allowing a bit of Underworld aura to leak out. The establishment of this small spirit realm is made possible by using the connection of Lake Dalsh within Raklev region with the fallen Death (opens in a new tab).

Mini-State Underworld allows the deceased to enter and rest there without emerging again. However, this is not the real Underworld. It cannot truly offer rest, nor can it completely bind the spirits. They can only gradually fade away.

Raklev's Skull Blessing annual ritual is a manifestation of the establishment of Mini-State Underworld. It is a day for spirits to emerge, revel, and be liberated. Keeping the skulls of loved ones at home is a way to protect the family from any spirits that might stray from the mini-state and causing harm.

Mini-State Underworld, as a place to return to, bears resemblances with the concept of Chaos Primogenitor Pathway's Paramita, except that mini-state offers the spirits no path for rebirth and can only let them fade away. Oxyto (opens in a new tab) used this similarity to merge her Paramita with the corresponding local Mini-State Underworld, becoming a local version of Death.

Mind World

Also known as the Mind World of all living beings, it has a veiled and miraculous connection to the Spirit World. In this world, everyone can understand each other regardless of the language used.

About

In the field of psychology and mysticism (opens in a new tab), an ordinary person’s Consciousness is an island. The Subconscious is the portion of the island beneath the sea. The Collective Subconscious is the surrounding sea. On top is the Spirituality Sky. All of these forms the Mind World.

As for a Beyonder (opens in a new tab), his island might have a controllable active volcano. A lunatic’s island might have a volcano that can erupt at any time. It would quake the foundations and pollute the sea.

If someone came in contact with the spirit of an insane person without protection, his chaotic thoughts might infect the communicator, just like how polluted water in the sea would flow outward, spreading further.

A person's dream appears like a bubble above the island and is separated from another's dream by misty boundaries. A Dreamwalker can jump into the neighboring dream bubbles and influence them without coming in contact with them.

Sea of Collective Subconscious

If a person goes deep into his/her island of subconscious, they'll enter a sea of subconscious of all living creatures. It looks gray from afar. There is the ground at the bottom of the sea, and many lights swimming above the ground. There are memories imprints of human pioneers and the spread and reflection of the consciousness of surrounding creatures.

Traveling for too long might cause a Beyonder (opens in a new tab) not from the Visionary Pathway to lose control due to the stifling loneliness and the pressure. There are also many dangerous things in the sea, including the accumulated maleficent thoughts of humanity, the desires that can corrupt Spirit Bodies, the terrifying memories since ancient times that resemble storms and waves at the sea.

One can enter other's consciousness by traveling through the collective subconscious sea. A person's consciousness might attack the intruders. One can also be affected and corrupted by others' subconscious if they stay for too long. For Beyonders of the Visionary Pathway, they can easily influence and even modify others' subconsciousness through the sea. They can also tell lies from looking at the other party's island.

Many things can be hidden in the Sea of Collective Subconscious.

In Groselle's Travels (opens in a new tab), a copy of Liveseyd (opens in a new tab) is hidden deep inside the Sea of Collective Subconscious of all the characters.

Audrey (opens in a new tab) had encountered a mind dragon, Ariehogg (opens in a new tab), inside the Sea of people in Hartlarkh Village (opens in a new tab) of East Chester County.

The headquarter of the Psychology Alchemists (opens in a new tab), Garden of Eden (opens in a new tab), was a city that existed within the heart of every man and woman, wherever there were people, it will exist. It was later destroyed by Klein (opens in a new tab), the remaining parts of the city collapsed and merged into the boundless Sea of Collective Subconscious.

Mirror World

The Mirror World is a collective manifestation of doors connected to various mirror-like entities. It has also been described as an amalgamation of the concept of doors, connected to mirrors and alternate spaces. Strictly speaking, it is not a real world.

Appearance

Ordinary Mirror Worlds consist of dark and illusory passageways connected to each other. Numerous of these tunnels intertwine, forming a complicated, mysterious, colossal and abstract “spider web” that is interwoven into a profound and strange “world.” Each illusory path is connected to a mirror or an alternate mysterious space.

**Special Mirror Worlds** (opens in a new tab), such as the ones in Fourth Epoch Trier (opens in a new tab) and Morora (opens in a new tab), vary in appearance.

Description

General Characteristics

The Mirror World is often associated with terror, mystery, horror, and the bizarre. Under normal circumstances, even through “true vision,” there is no way to directly see a Mirror World unless someone triggers it.

Mirror worlds come in two varieties: The first was conventional and universal, not a true world. Wherever mirrors existed such mirror worlds with no discernible boundaries could be found. The second type are Special Mirror Worlds (opens in a new tab), They relied on the conventional mirror world and leveraged specific corruption or corresponding abilities to take shape. Some were permanent, others temporary. Their characteristics closely mirrored the real world, projections of one facet of reality, but each had its own unique limitations and rules. The mirror world in Fourth Epoch Trier is an example.

Each illusory path in the Mirror World is connected to a mirror or an alternate mysterious space. By connecting to different mirrors and different mysterious kingdoms, if one becomes lost in there, they might directly appear in the Abyss (opens in a new tab), or even the Cosmos (opens in a new tab).

The Mirror World is a treacherous space hiding many dangers. The dark, illusory paths forming it have been known to suddenly morph into massive vortexes, pulling anything caught in it to unknown mirrors. High-Sequence Beyonders of the Demoness and Door Pathways can navigate it relatively safely.

The Mirror World contains higher spatial dimensions.

The Mirror World is connected to the Spirit World. Abilites requiring the Spirit World, such as "Spirit World Traversal", can be activated in it, albeit in a restricted fashion.

Relationship with Mirrors

In mysticism, mirrors are often given the symbolic meaning of leading to another “kingdom,” and are mostly related to terrifying horrors. Mirrors possess distinct symbolic meanings, such as one’s reflection or an entrance to another realm. The former is the principle behind a Demoness' substitutes, while the latter alludes to the Mirror World.

Each mirror has a corresponding area of the Mirror World behind it. They are connected by the illusory dark tunnels which form the Mirror World. Once a mirror surface shatters, the corresponding area behind the mirror would collapse with it. If the people and objects in that area are not able to escape in time through the mirror tunnels, they would be swallowed by the turbulence of the Mirror World and dragged into its depths. The dangers there are fatal for Beyonders without a complete Mythical Creature Form (opens in a new tab). Even for Angels (opens in a new tab) with complete Mythical Creature forms, unless they are of the Door or the Demoness Pathway, the depths of the Mirror World are places they are reluctant to venture into lightly.

When a mirror breaks, it essentially forms more but smaller mirrors. However, from a mystical concept, it is destroyed as a whole, completely losing its original symbolism and function. The new mirrors produced need time to evolve their own areas behind them, connect with the vast Mirror World, and become mirrors with complete concepts in mysticism. In other words, for a period of time, these mirror fragments cannot be used to reflect enemies, transmit curses, or traverse the Mirror World, though this doesn't affect Wraiths using them for mirror jumps.

Aside from normal mirrors, mirror-like objects can also be treated as mirrors. This includes magic mirrors like Arrodes (opens in a new tab), which look like real mirrors on the surface.Provided that there are few to no ripples, a body of water can also function as a mirror.

Related Pathways

The Pathways closely connected to the Mirror World are the Demoness and Door Pathways. The Chained Pathway can use mirrors for mirror jumps.

Klein Moretti (opens in a new tab) once speculated that the “secret” in Secrets Sorcerer of the Door Pathway might represent the Mirror World to some extent. High-Sequence Beyonders of the Door Pathway can directly open illusory doors to the Mirror World without using a mirror.

According to Clarice (opens in a new tab), the most unique node in the Demoness Pathway is Sequence 7 Witch. This isn't just because it changes gender but also because it leaves a strong, distinct imprint in the Mirror World. This imprint is what allows them to use mirror magic. According to the Demoness Card of Blasphemy, the Sequence 0 of the Demoness Pathway, the Primordial Demoness, is the "Ruler of the Mirror World".
