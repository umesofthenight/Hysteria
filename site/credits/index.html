Credits and acknowledgements

The setting, artwork and typography behind Hysteria.

Setting

Hysteria is a fan roleplay setting inspired by Lord of the Mysteries by Cuttlefish That Loves Diving. The historical illustrations provide atmosphere and are not official artwork for the novel.

Roleplay reference material is drawn from the Hysteria collection.

Historical illustrations

Historical works by Gustave Doré, used as atmosphere for Hysteria. The illustrations are public domain, via Wikimedia Commons. Images are cropped and displayed in grayscale.

  • Over London – by Rail (1872) · engraved by Adolphe François Pannemaker after Gustave Doré
  • The Titans, Inferno, plate 65 (1857)
  • Charon, Inferno, plate 9 (1857)
  • The Deluge, The Holy Bible, plate I (1866) · digital restoration by Adam Cuerden
  • Wentworth Street, Whitechapel (1872)

Display type: Boska by Indian Type Foundry, via Fontshare.

Hysteria identity

The Hysteria logos and Romantic typeface were supplied by Hysteria for this website.
