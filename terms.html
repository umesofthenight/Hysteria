Updated 9 September 2026

This site explains what the server is and provides an invite. If you join, you must follow the rules in Discord. Staff must approve your character before you roleplay.

About this website

Hysteria is an unofficial Lord of Mysteries roleplay community. This website has information about joining, a Discord invite, and an optional server widget. The community owner runs the site.

These terms cover use of the website. Discord has its own terms, and participation in the server is subject to the rules published there. You can contact the owner through a private staff ticket in the Discord server.

Age and community participation

You must be 14 or older to join Hysteria. You must also meet Discord's terms of service, including any higher minimum age in your country.

Roleplay may include horror, violence, corruption, death, psychological distress, and religious themes. Sexually explicit content is not allowed. Read the server rules before taking part.

Staff review characters and assign roles under the server rules. Opening an invite or visiting this website does not approve your character or give you a role.

Using the site

Use the website lawfully and respect other members. Do not seek unauthorized access, disrupt the site, or distribute malicious code. Do not impersonate staff or claim an affiliation with the community that you do not have.

Links to Discord and other services

The invite and widget connect to Discord. External policy links open the relevant providers' websites. When you use those services, their terms and privacy policies apply. You can follow the Discord invite with the widget turned off.

Our privacy policy explains how this website uses technical information. The cookie policy explains the widget controls and browser storage.

The site and invite may change or be temporarily unavailable. Check the server announcements for current story information, membership rules, and playable regions.

If you find a website error, have trouble accessing a page, or want to raise a content ownership issue, contact staff in Discord and ask for the owner. Send only the information needed to explain the problem.
